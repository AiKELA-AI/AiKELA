# Box-String Instrument

## Type

Handcraft and simple musical instrument

## Equipment

One clean, sturdy small cardboard box with an open top per Cub (an empty tissue box works); three or four intact, low-tension elastic bands of different thicknesses; scrap cardboard for a bridge; masking tape; markers or crayons.

## Instructions

1. Check the boxes and elastic bands before the activity. If a cardboard bridge needs cutting, an adult should prepare the strips in advance.
2. Decorate the outside of the box, keeping the opening clear.
3. Stretch three or four elastic bands gently across the opening so they pass over the hollow space. Do not pull them tightly.
4. If needed, fold a narrow cardboard strip and place it under the bands near each end of the opening to make a bridge. Tape it in place without trapping the bands.
5. Pluck the bands gently, one at a time, and compare their sounds. Cubs can use the finished instrument to play a simple beat.

Keep elastic bands low and pointed away from faces; never snap or stretch them at another person. Use only clean boxes and intact bands. Keep small scraps out of mouths and off the floor. Offer a pre-assembled instrument for anyone who cannot safely handle the bands.
