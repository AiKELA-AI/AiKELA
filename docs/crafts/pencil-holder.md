## Instructions

Equipment:

Empty plastic container (such as a yoghurt cup)

Pieces of coloured paper

Scissors

Glue and glue brush

Pictures

Clear varnish

Brush

Florist’s ribbon (optional)

Directions:

Wash and dry the container thoroughly.

Cut various shapes from paper in assorted colours. Apply glue to the outside of a part of the container and cover it with pieces of paper. The edges of the paper must overlap. Repeat the process until the entire outside of the container is covered. The pieces of the paper must only be pasted up to the neck of the container. Cut out larger shapes and paste them onto the front. Allow to dry.

Apply a coat of varnish to the outside of the container. Allow to dry thoroughly.

Measure, cut and glue florist’s ribbon around the neck of the container.

Tips:

Use gift-wrap with pictures of flowers, animals and so forth. Cut out the motifs and use them to cover the container.

Pieces of paper can also be pasted in strips or dots for a different effect.
