# ID Badge

## Type

Handcraft

## Equipment

Cardboard; Aluminum (tin) foil; Scissors; Glue; Tape; Safety pin

## Instructions

![7-Point Star 1](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Detectives_attachment.doc/Detectives_attachment_html_edf63725.gif>)

Equipment:

Cardboard

Aluminum (tin) foil

Scissors

Glue

Tape

Safety pin

Trace this star onto cardboard

and cut it out.

Cut a foil circle to fit the star.

Glue it on to the star.

## Advancement covered / badge link

No activity-specific advancement task was identified in this attachment. Check the programme-level notes; no badge completion is implied.

**Programme advancement context only — not linked to this element:**
- **Historical task; current badge link unverified:** Play Kim’s game using ONE of the five senses and succeed in identifying five out of ten objects.
- **Historical task; current badge link unverified:** Play TWO Kim’s games using TWO of the five senses and succeed in identifying seven out of ten objects, sounds or tastes in each one.
- **Historical task; current badge link unverified:** Play THREE different Kim’s games with THREE of the five senses, scoring at least ten out of fifteen in each one.
- **Historical task; current badge link unverified:** Play FIVE different Kim’s games using each of the FIVE senses, scoring at least fifteen out of twenty in each one.
