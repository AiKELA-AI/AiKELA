# Be Water Safe Tags

## Type

Awareness tag craft; 20 minutes

## Equipment

Cardboard tags, crayons, hole punch and wool.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Cubs decorate a water-safety tag to attach to a bag or pencil case. Source slogans include Stop and Think, Keep Yourself Safe, Learn to Swim, Never Swim Alone, Be Water Safe and Buddy Up. Punch a hole and thread wool; make a second tag for a friend if time permits. The source prints 112 on the reverse; verify emergency contact information before using the printable in a new plan.

BASE 2: ACTIVITY: Be water safe tags
Cubs are to create a ‘Be Water Safe’ tag which they can
share with their friends – see examples below. Using the
water safety rules, let the Cubs come up with their own
hashtag label or use the examples below. Ask the Cubs
to decorate their labels. Punch a hole in their label and
thread a piece of wool/string through. Cubs can either
give their label to a friend or attach the label to their
own school bag/pencil case to raise awareness about
water safety. Time allowing, have the Cubs make more
than one label.
