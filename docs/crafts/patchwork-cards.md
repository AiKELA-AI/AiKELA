# Patchwork Cards

## Type

Handcraft

## Equipment

Patterned paper from old magazines and coloured paper; Card; Crayons; Scissors; Glue

## Instructions

Equipment:

Patterned paper from old magazines and coloured paper

Card

Crayons

Scissors

Glue

Cut a square from a piece of thick coloured paper for the front of the card. Then, draw lots of lines across it with a coloured pencil.

Cut pieces of patterned paper from old magazines. Cut the pieces into strips, and then cut across the strips to make little squares.

Glue the patterned pieces onto the square so that it looks like patchwork. Leave spaces between them so that the coloured paper shows.

Fold a rectangle of thick paper in half for a card. Then, glue the patchwork square onto the front. Trim the edges of the card if you need to.

To make a patchwork heart, decorate a square of paper as above. Then, when the glue is dry, draw a heart on the paper and cut it out.
