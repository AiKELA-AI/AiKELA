# Smoke Leaf Prints — source method requiring safety review

## Type

Natural-material printing craft; source slot 10 minutes

## Equipment

Jars, candles, matches, petroleum jelly, paper, leaves, paper towel/cotton wool.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

The source coats a jar with petroleum jelly, holds it above a lit candle to collect soot, rubs a leaf over the coating, places the leaf on paper, covers with another page and presses. It then cleans the jar and repeats. **This is a preserved source method, not a ready-to-run recommendation; resolve heat, flame and glass handling before selecting it.**

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Hot glass, soot and an open flame require a Scouter to evaluate the setup; children must not handle a hot jar. Select an existing safer leaf-printing method from the library if this setup cannot be made appropriate. Do not invent a replacement technique and attribute it to the source. Retheming may change the fictional setting while retaining the sourced mechanic; identify the adaptation and this source.
