# Underwater Telescopes

## Type

Handcraft

## Equipment

Yoghurt tubs; Clear plastic; Scissors; Rubber bands

## Instructions

Equipment:

Yoghurt tubs

Clear plastic

Scissors

Rubber bands

Cut a hole in the bottom of the yoghurt tubs. Cover the top opening of the yoghurt tub with plastic; secure with a rubber band. Look through the bottom of the yoghurt tub and see what you can find in the water
