# Toilet-roll Bird Feeder

## Type

Nature craft and follow-up observation; 20-minute making slot

## Equipment

Cardboard toilet-roll tube, peanut butter, wild bird seed, string, blunt spreading tools, newspaper and a take-home bag.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Spread peanut butter over the tube, roll it in bird seed and attach string to hang it. The source asks Cubs to watch visiting birds for two weeks and mark them on Garden_birds.pdf. Do not treat this duration as verified badge wording.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Check peanut allergies before offering this version; select another sourced feeder if needed. Keep tools blunt, clean the area and hang only where permission and safe observation are possible. Retheming may change the fictional setting while retaining the sourced mechanic; identify the adaptation and this source.
