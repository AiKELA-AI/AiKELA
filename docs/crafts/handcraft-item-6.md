## Instructions

HANDCRAFT: Newspaper Kites NB: Do all the measuring of the kites beforehand and reinforcing (can use masking tape) and make one stick for each Cub as this activity can take a while to put together – they can cut out and make one stick. A lot of wind is needed and a lot of string is needed to fly these kites but they are worth it.
