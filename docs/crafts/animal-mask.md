# Animal Mask

## Type

Handcraft

Existing POP craft reused, with an additional Scouts Digital variant attributed separately below. No duplicate mask craft has been created.

## Equipment

Template; crayons; stick; scissors; glue/sticky tape

## Instructions

The five image files named by the original conversion are absent from this checkout. See the visual-reference section for the original Word attachment; these cannot be treated as included printables.

**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

HANDCRAFT: Animal Mask Using the template provided or your own, Cubs are to colour in an animal mask. Once coloured in, mount on a stick and hold up in front of their faces.

### Scouts Digital variant: Jungle Masks

**Reused source-specific variant:** In *Alice in Wonderland — The Jungle has gone mad*, the Queen asks Cubs to choose a jungle animal and colour a mask so she can recognise them. Each Six works together to create its best set of masks. The source offers a sweet treat for the best set but does not supply judging criteria. Its slot is 25 minutes.

The source equipment list is masks, elastic, scissors, glue sticks, four tables, chairs, pencil crayons and bottle tops. Scale furniture and materials to the selected Pack. No templates or assembly instructions accompanied this download. The later [Dance of Kaa](../activities/dance-of-kaa-reference.md) asks Cubs to bring their masks but has no supplied dance instructions.

**Planning status:** The basic colouring-and-stick method above has instructions. The Scouts Digital variant needs suitable templates and a construction method before use; do not assume its PDF includes a printable mask sheet.

**Scouter guidance added during extraction:** Supervise scissors, keep eye holes clear and check elastic fit. Offer a hand-held mask for Cubs who dislike face coverings. Check dietary needs before a food reward.

**Theme adaptation:** Reuse the colouring-and-making method with characters suitable for the meeting, naming this craft and the changed theme. The verified Gold Wolf connection applies only when a jungle character mask is made and used as required.

## Advancement covered / badge link


- **Gold Wolf practice link:** [Make a jungle character mask and use it in a jungle play/dance.](../advancements/gold-wolf/outdoor-make-a-jungle-character-mask-and-use-it-in.md) — Verified against [The Cub Trail, November 2019, p. 14](../The-Cub-Trail-Nov-19.pdf#page=14). The Cub must make a jungle character mask and use it in a jungle play/dance; mask-making alone is only part of the task. An unrelated character or displaying masks does not establish completion.

The POP source does not state this current connection. The Scouts Digital programme lists the task at programme level. Neither source establishes an individual Cub's completion merely from participation.
