# Sand Art

## Type

Handcraft

## Equipment

Construction paper; white glue; sand

## Instructions

What you are going to need:

Construction paper

White glue

Sand

Give each Cub a piece of construction paper, and allow them to draw a picture or write words with the white glue or similar. Make sure that they do not put globs of glue in any one spot. Before the glue dries take the picture over to the dry sand and with your hand pour sand onto the glue. Let it sit for a few minutes and then shake off the excess sand. Let it dry flat for about half an hour depending on how much glue was used.
