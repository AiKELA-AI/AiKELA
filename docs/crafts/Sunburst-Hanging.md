# Sunburst Hanging

## Type
Activity (listed under “Activity” in the source)

## Equipment
Two lightweight paper plates; ruler; crayons or paints; scissors; tape; glue; thread

## Instructions
Draw a circle about 7 cm in diameter in the centre of one plate. The source then says to mark every 5 cm along the outside edge and says there should be 14 evenly spaced marks. These directions appear inconsistent for a typical paper plate; check the spacing before cutting. Put the plates together and cut from each mark towards the circle, cutting through both plates. Cut every other wedge out of the circle. Decorate both shapes with crayons or paints, leaving the centre blank. Use loops of tape or glue to stick the centres together. Tape the sunburst rays together, matching them as closely as possible. Gently curl each ray around a pencil. Make a hole near the edge of the inner circle and hang the sunburst in a window.
