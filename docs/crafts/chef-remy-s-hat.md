# Chef Remy’s Hat

## Type

Handcraft

## Equipment

A3 piece of white paper (newspaper will do as well); Scissors per Cub; Sticky tape; Take the A3 size paper and fold in half lengthways.

## Instructions

Equipment:

![Source image](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Yummy_Tummy_attachment.doc/Yummy_Tummy_attachment_html_9e716f12.jpg>)

A3 piece of white paper (newspaper will do as well)

Scissors per Cub

Sticky tape

Take the A3 size paper and fold in half lengthways.

Cut the folded side to approximately 4 cm from edge of the paper.

Cut a band that will fit around the Cubs head.

Tape around the Cubs head to fit.

Colour in and then stick badge on to band.
