# Silent Pinch Pots

## Type

Handcraft / non-verbal instruction station; source rotation 12 minutes

## Equipment

Play dough or clay; source instruction sheet.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

The source asks Cubs to make a pinch pot using mimed instructions and a picture sheet without talking. Its sheet shows rolling a clay ball, pushing a thumb into the centre, pinching the walls around, smoothing with water and adding texture. The source calls this a Zulu pinch pot; cultural authenticity of the generic diagram is not verified.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Use child-suitable modelling material, wash hands and keep it out of mouths. Treat silence as a communication challenge rather than a simulation of disability; permit accessible alternatives.
