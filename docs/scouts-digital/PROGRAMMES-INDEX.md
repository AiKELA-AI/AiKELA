## Alice in Wonderland — review before reuse

- **Source / provenance:** User-supplied Scouts Digital download; original PDF linked from the conversion. Local Sixes, Scouter assignments, marbles and award records are source-Pack practices only.
- **Advancement / badge link:** Page 3 lists recruit, Silver Wolf and Gold Wolf targets. Retained as source claims, not verified credit. The Animal Sounds game covers hearing only and specifies no scoring; it does not establish the claimed five-senses/70% target. Making masks alone does not establish that Cubs used them in a jungle play/dance. Do not assume naming eight Jungle Book animals is covered by choosing one mask. Check every target against the supplied SSA source before adapting.
- **Visual aid / image reference:** Original PDF pages 1–3. Page 1 contains a video link labelled “Team Planks Caterpillar Relay”, preserved in the conversion; the video has not been viewed. Mask templates are listed as equipment but were not supplied. Dance of Kaa instructions are not supplied.
- **Completeness:** The croquet-themed introduction, relay label and soccer-ball equipment list do not establish complete relay instructions. Closing starts at 18:55; dismissal is recorded as “19.15” on page 3 without a duration for closing.
- **Practical review:** The source has blindfolded Cubs moving around the hall. Before reuse, plan close supervision and collision prevention or adapt to an eyes-open listening game. Check visibility and elastic fit for masks. Record any adaptation separately from this archive.

## Mrs Knot's House — review before reuse

- **Source / provenance:** User-supplied Scouts Digital download; original PDF linked from the conversion. The text refers to Plumstead and visiting 1st Meadowridge Cubs; this does not establish authorship. Sixes, named leaders and “bones” scoring remain source-Pack practices.
- **Advancement / badge link:** The source labels hanking “SW 49”, reef knots “SW 45” and stopping bleeding “GW 74”; the numbering and badge credit have not been verified. The bandaging instructions do not explicitly teach direct pressure, despite the stopping-bleeding heading. Reef-knot practice alone does not establish completion of a full knot requirement. The poisons discussion lacks its referenced sheet and detailed teaching content; verify against authoritative SSA requirements before use.
- **Visual aid / image reference:** Original PDF pages 1–2. A “Poison sheet” is required on page 2 but no separate attachment was supplied.
- **Completeness:** The poisons session runs 18:05–18:20 by its stated duration, but closing is listed at 18:45, leaving 25 minutes unexplained. The thank-you transition is unfinished. Handwashing appears after the sweet-eating relay; move it before food handling in any adaptation.
- **Practical review:** Use clean practice bandages without restricting circulation. For any adaptation, replace real hazardous substances with pictures or clean, safe props; obtain current first-aid teaching guidance before running the poisons session. Check food allergies and suitability, and keep eating seated and separate from running.
