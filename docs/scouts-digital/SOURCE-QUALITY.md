Flags distinguish short references, uncertain text, safety preparation and supporting visuals. Do not invent a missing method or printable. Transitions and named award/admin records remain source-only.

## General source limitations

- A named yarn/script, code key, question sheet or template may be elsewhere in the same conversion; check the linked supporting pages before calling it missing.
- Timetable rows are source extracts. Short rows are references, not permission to invent complete game rules or teaching content.
- Source dates and filename dates sometimes differ. For example, the 17 October Surat Hindoo download prints 19 September 2026, and the 18 August swimming download prints 31 July 2026. Preserve and check both.
- Some shared sources appear to have AI-written content or factual/translation errors. Source attribution is not proof that the content or badge claim is correct.
- Card/worksheet tables and pooled stations may need source-level review when adapting; no source-wide advancement list is attached to every activity.
- All emergency numbers, medical treatment, tool/fire and swimming instructions require appropriate current sources before teaching. A named game using pretend calls is not a live emergency exercise.
