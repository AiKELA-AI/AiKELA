         concentrate on details.

          1. Set up: Gather 10 -20 items (Small/Everyday kind of

          2. Observe: Give cubs a minute to memorize the items and
           their positions.

          4. Memorize: Cubs to draw or write down what they saw.

          5. Remove items and repeat it again. Cubs to spot what
         items were missing.
