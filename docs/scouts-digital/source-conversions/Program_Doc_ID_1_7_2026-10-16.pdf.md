Buona sera bambini, tonight, we are traveling to Italy to visit Geppetto a famous
woodcarver. As a woodcarver uses his hands to craft his masterpieces let’s inspect
your hands this evening.

18h10        5     Inspection: Hands              None           All

One day, Geppetto found a special piece of wood that he used to carve a
marionette puppet he called Pinocchio. A marionette is a puppet that is
manipulated with wires and controlled by the puppeteer. What Geppetto didn’t
know at the time is that the piece of wood he used was magical, so Pinocchio
didn’t need string and could move independently.  Let’s see if you can be like
Pinocchio and jump as we swing our string!

18h15        5    Steam Release: Helicopter       Rope          Rikki

Pinocchio, like most marionette puppets liked to dance. As he was from Italy we
are going to learn traditional Italian dance.
