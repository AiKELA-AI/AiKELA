Now that our cave pack has gathered at the watering hole, inspected our emergency gear, and mastered the basic vines
and ropes needed for survival, it's time to escape the confines of the dark cave. Let’s run wild into the prehistoric
wilderness to release some energy with a fast-paced game.

  17:45  Steam release game: "Beware the Sabertooth!"                Whistle      Rama
 (10 min)
        The players are members of the Crood family / Caveman Tribe.
        The leader (or animator) is Grug (or the Tribe Leader), armed
           with a loud whistle or horn (the "Warning Horn").Out in the
         dangerous prehistoric wild, roaming alone means getting eaten
         by a Sabertooth Tiger or a Bear Owl! The only way to survive is
           to stick together in Packs.

      How to Play

                   1. Scattered Tribe:
                     All the cavemen run around the area, making caveman
               noises (grunting, beating their chests, acting like
                prehistoric animals, or foraging for "food").
                   2. The Danger Signal:
            When the Leader blows the whistle, everyone stops
                running and listens carefully. The Leader yells out a
                  survival rule, such as:
                     ○ "SABERTOOTH ATTACK! PACKS OF 4!"
                     ○ "ROCK SLIDE! GROUPS OF 3!"
                     ○ "NIGHTTIME IS DANGEROUS! CAVEMEN IN
                      5s!"
                   3. Form the Pack:
                Players must quickly huddle together to form groups of
                  that exact size. To make it extra thematic, they must link
              arms or put their heads together in a "family huddle" and
                    yell "NEVER NOT BE AFRAID!").
