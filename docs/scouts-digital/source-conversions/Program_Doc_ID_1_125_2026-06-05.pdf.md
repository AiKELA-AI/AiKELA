```text
Yarn: The Story of MaZaca's Garden

MaZaca was an old lady. She lived alone next door to Sipho and his grandmother. She had a few cows and
a vegetable garden. In her garden, she had planted beans, tomatoes, and cabbage.

One Saturday, she gave Sipho a whole bunch of ripe tomatoes. "All my tomatoes have ripened at the same
time. So you must help me eat them," she said.

"Let’s make tomato sandwiches for lunch!" said Grandma. Sipho ran off to call Amos, Ayanda, and
Precious. "Hi, everyone!" he shouted. "Grandma is going to make tomato sandwiches! Come and eat with
us."

They all went to Sipho’s house and ate lots of tomato sandwiches. "That was so delicious!" said Ayanda.
"How can we say thank you to MaZaca?"

"I know," said Sipho, "we can go and pull out all the weeds in her vegetable garden."

Very quickly, they pulled out all the weeds. "That didn't take long," said Amos. "Let's pull out the weeds
on the outside of the garden too, then it will be beautifully clean everywhere! MaZaca is going to be very
happy."

That night, there was a storm. The thunder and lightning were so bad that Sipho pulled his pillow over his
head. "Don't be afraid, my son," Grandma said, "all this rain will help MaZaca's vegetables grow
beautifully."

But the next morning, they heard MaZaca crying. "Yo! Yo! Yo!" she wailed. "My garden is gone!" And
she was right. The rain had washed away half of her vegetable garden, right down the path. The cows were
busy eating the cabbage in the path.

A Lesson Learned

At school, Sipho told his teacher how they had tried to help MaZaca and what had happened next.

Akela said, "It was very kind of you to pull out the weeds to say thank you. But didn't you know that it is
the roots of plants and grass that hold the soil in place? When a person removes weeds, grass, or plants, the
rain and wind can wash or blow the loose soil away. We call this soil erosion."

"How can we help now?" Sipho asked.

"I know a good African way," said Akela. "Perhaps we can all work together."

What do you think Akela's idea was?
```
