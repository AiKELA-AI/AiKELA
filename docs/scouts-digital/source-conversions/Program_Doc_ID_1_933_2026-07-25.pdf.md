               Inspection: Nails, handkerchiefs, badges and uniform

 2:40 pm      Activity 1: A Growing Fire                       Meena
 10 min
                Pick a cub to be the first flame. They will proceed to
              chase people around and try to tag them thereby
               getting the flames to get bigger and grow into a fire. A
              tagged person must link arms with whoever tagged
             them. Whenever a flame chain gets to 4 people, they
                  split in half.


 2.50 pm      Activity 2: Camp Fire Rules                               Nila
 10 min       Discuss the dos and donts of a campfire. Different
           Camp fires


 3:00 pm      Activity 3: Skits                                            Nila
 20 min      Each six to practice their skit                             Jay
                 •  Soup                                            Prakashini
                 •  Empty Boxes                           Meena
                 •   Pass the Pepper
                 •   Silverware


 3.20 pm      Activity 4: Camp Fire                                    Prakashini
 10 min       Place pictures of campfire elements on the floor.
              Leader to call out one of the items and cubs to run and
              stand on the item. Cubs will be given 10 seconds and if
              not on the item they are out.
             As cubs get out, reduce the pictures on the floor.
