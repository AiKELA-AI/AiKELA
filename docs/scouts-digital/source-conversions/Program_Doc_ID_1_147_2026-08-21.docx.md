Safety First at Home

Mia, a curious and lively cub , was always eager to help her parents around the house, as any good cub who tries their best always does. One day, while she was helping her mother in the Kitchen , Mia realized that there were many safety thigs she needed to know.

Mia was grabbed for very sharp knife to help her mother cut some vegetables, but didn’t bother to look if she grasped it by the handle or the blade. Her mom gasped in absolute horror and gently took it away from her. “ Mia, this is a sharp object, Its not safe to just grab like that ” she explained.

Mia’s eyes widened in understanding.

Kitchen

She decided to learn about all the things she should avoid for safety in the kitchen.

She started by avoiding touching the hot stove, sharp objects and hot pots without looking at exactly what she was doing

Bathroom

Next, Mia went to the bathroom.

She remembered how her mother always insisted she never run on the wet floor.

Mia understood it was because she could slip and fall.

Mia also remembered that she should not touch the electrical appliances with wet hands.

She made a mental note of these safety tips for the bathroom.

Bedroom

After the bathroom, Mia moved to her bedroom.

Her Mom had told her not to jump on the bed as she could fall and hurt herself.

Mia also knew to keep her toys away when she was done playing, to avoid tripping over them.

The garden

Mia was proud of herself for remembering all these safety tips. However, she realized that there was one more place she had not considered – the garden.

In the garden, Mia knew not to touch any plants without her parents permission.

Some plants could be harmful if touched or ingested.

She also knew to stay away from any tools her father left out after gardening.

Mia was satisfied with her new knowledge.

Teaching friends :

She felt safer knowing what she should avoid in different parts of the house. She decided to share this knowledge with her friends the next day.

The next morning, Mia told her friends about the safety tips she learned.

They listened attentively and promised to follow the same rules in their homes.

The next week at Cubs told the OLD WOLVES all about what she had learnt and done.

OLD WOLVES and being a role-model

The OLD WOLVES praised her for being so responsible and caring about safety., They also told her what a great role model for her fellow cubs and friends.

From that day on, Mia continued to learn about safety in other areas and shared knowledge with others. She became known as the “ Safety Champion” amongst her friends. Mia understood that safety was an ongoing journey. She knew that there would always be more to learn, but she was ready for the challenge. With her curiosity and eagerness to learn, Mia was sure to continue making her home a safer place.
