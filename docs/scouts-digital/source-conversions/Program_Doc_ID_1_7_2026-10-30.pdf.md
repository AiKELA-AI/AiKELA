```text
Baloo: You’re welcome, and thanks for your help. Wally

and Bo Peep, have you taken any swimming lessons?

Wally and Bo Peep: No <quilty voice>

Chil: Bo Peep, what were you thinking? You can’t swim!
You just put yourself in danger instead of calling for help.

Now! I have two things to say. Number 1, I’m happy you
are safe. And number two, if you two were cubs you
would know not to act so dangerously, right cubs?

Baloo: Excuse me Chil, do the cubs know that we have a

gala tomorrow?

<wait for cubs to answer>

Chil: Well cubs, I hope you are not going to be as silly as
Wally and Bo Peep. We will obey all the safety rules,
right?

<Run through safety rules>

1.      Always have an adult watch you
2.       Don’t go  in water over your chest if you can’t swim
3.      Always walk around the pool — don’t run
4.       Listen to the lifeguards and obey pool rules
5.      Never play rough games  in the water
6.      Always jump feet  first and  far away from the side when you jump  in
7.      Never push someone into a pool or jump on someone
8.      Only dive in a diving area
9.         If  you  have  long  hair, make sure  to  tie   it up  before  getting  in  10.
      Remember  to  apply sunscreen two hours  before  going  outdoors and
        immediately after drying off from the pool
11.  Read  all warning signs before getting into the pool
```
