```text
Theme:   THE SNAIL TRAIL


Date: 21 September 2026                                Duty Six: Yellow


  Time   Min  Activity                          Equipment     Scouter


18h00      15  GRAND HOWL                         Totem/skin/     Akela
                  Flag                                         Flag/flag pole    Six
                  Register                                  Register
                Break
This year we have been focusing on books and stories. Some stories are fictional –
meaning they are made up and some stories are non-fiction which mean they are real or
based on facts. Tonight’s book or story is The Snail Trail. Snails are interesting little
creatures with soft bodies and a hard shell. They don’t have any legs, but they do have a
foot. Let’s inspect our feet (shoes).

18h15      5   Inspection: Shoes                    Bones               All
                                                  Bone bags

Did you know snails and slugs both belong to the same family? They are both
Gastropods. Snails have a hard shell but slugs have no shells.
            STEAM RELEASE: Snails and Slugs
18h20      5   Divide cubs into 2 teams. Cubs line up    None          Kaa
                back to back in 2 lines down the centre
                    of the playing area. One team is snails
               and the other is slugs. Leader calls out
                   sssssnails or ssssslugs. If snails is called,
                 they turn and chase the slugs. Any slugs
                 caught become snails and vice versa.
Snails can be found all over the world both on land and water. They are found in the sea
and fresh water. The largest snail is the Giant African Snail. He can be up to 35cm long
and weigh over 2 kg. Think how heavy it must be for him to drag his shell around with
him. How heavy are you?
               Relay Race: Drag your shell
18h25      10  Cubs line up in Sixes with Sixer at the     Cardboard and    All
                     front. On go the Sixer runs to the other   rope
               end of the hall to retrieve a piece of card
               and rope and runs back. The next cub in
                     line sits on the cardboard and holds the
                  rope. The Sixer drags the cub across the
                room. When they get to the other side
                  the cub being dragged brings the
                 cardboard and rope back and fetches the
                 next cub etc until the last cub. The last
                cub then drags the Sixer back across the
                      hall. First six to finish and sit wins.

When snails move they often leave a slime trail behind for other snails to follow. This
snail trail can last for weeks if it doesn’t rain. Can you recognise trail signs?
```
