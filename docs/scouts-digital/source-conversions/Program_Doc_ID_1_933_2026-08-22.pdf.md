```text
South African Scout Association
                            Surat Hindoo Scout Group

                                           Greater Durban District, Province of Kwazulu-Natal

                                         Member of the World Organisation of the Scout
                                                                       Movement

Theme:       Nature

Date:        22 August 2026

Advancements: Awareness Challenge

Duty Six:      White

 Time                                   Activity                        Person in  Equipment
                                                                    charge
 2:30 pm     Opening                                                            All Pack
 10 min        Flag, Prayer, Cub promise                                 Scouters
               Inspection: Nails, handkerchiefs, badges and uniform

 2:40 pm      Activity 1: Down the tunnel                                Nila
 10 min      Cubs to run around. When the whistle blows, the Sixers
               freeze and stand with their legs apart. The Cubs find their
                 Sixer. The first Cub crawls through the legs and stands
                right behind the Sixer with their legs apart too. Next Cub
               crawls through both pairs of legs and stands right behind
              the second Cub with his/her legs apart as well. Continue
                 until the whole Six forms a tunnel.


 2.50 pm      Activity 2: Technology in Action (Shree)                    Pia
 10 min
 3.00 pm      Activity 2: Growing a plant                                Prakashini
 15 min       Explain the process of seed germination.
               Plant a seed in a coke bottle
               Plant herbs in a coke bottle.
 3:15 pm      Activity 3: Skipping                                         Nila
 10 minutes  Two cubs hold the ends of the skipping rope and cubs
            must skip whilst the skipping rope is thrown
 3:25 pm      Activity 4: Global Awareness (Shree)                       Pia
 5 min


 3:30 pm      Activity 5: Infectious diseases                              Prakashini
 10 minutes   Explain different infectious diseases


 3.40 pm      Activity 5: Beans                               Meena
 10 min      Cubs run around and when the whistle blows a command
                    is called out and they must follow the command and
               action for each type of bean.
```

```text
3.50 pm      Activity 8: Quick thinking                                   Nila
10 min        In their sixes, they must write down the following:
                •  3 fruits
                •  3 brands of chocolate
                •  3 vegetables
                •  3 knots
                •  3 jungle book characters
                •  3 endangered animals
                •  3 types of trees
                •  3 types of birds
                •  3 things you can recycle
                •  3 ways to reduce water
4:00 pm      Activity 2: Planting a seed                        Meena
10 min        In Sixes, the Cubs need to line up and, one at a time, run
              to the sand and seeds, pick up just one seed, 'plant' it in
             the tray, run back to their Six and the next Cub goes. Each
            seed needs to be in a separate 'hole' - once all the holes
             are full, the Six needs to sit quietly.

10 min      Cubs to pick a piece of paper and act out the action.
```
