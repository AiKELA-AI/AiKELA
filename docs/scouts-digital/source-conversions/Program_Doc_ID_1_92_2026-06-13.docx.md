Yarn - Operation Super Dad

Good morning Cubs!

Last week, Akela received a very important message delivered by a rather unusual messenger. It wasn't brought by email, WhatsApp, or even by Chil flying overhead. Instead, it arrived tied to the tail of a very excited squirrel!

The message was marked:

TOP SECRET – OPERATION SUPER DAD

Akela quickly gathered the Jungle Council to find out what was happening. Baloo, Bagheera, Hathi, and Chill all came to listen.

Inside the message was an urgent request.

The Super Dad Headquarters had discovered that Father's Day was almost here, and they needed help identifying the world's best dads. The problem was that there were simply too many amazing dads to choose from!

Some dads teach us how to ride bicycles. Some help us with homework. Some fix broken toys. Some tell funny jokes. Some build fires, cook on the braai, play games, or give the best hugs when we've had a difficult day.

The headquarters decided that only a Pack of clever Cubs could complete the mission.

So today, each Cub has been chosen as a special agent in Operation Super Dad.

Your mission is to complete a series of challenges to prove that you have what it takes to become an official Dad Appreciation Expert.

You will need to use your creativity, teamwork, kindness, and observation skills. Along the way, you will create a special Father's Day card that will show your dad, grandad, uncle, or special grown-up just how much they mean to you.

At the end of the mission, every Cub will earn the title of:

Official Super Dad Appreciation Agent

But remember, Cubs, being a Super Dad Appreciation Agent isn't only about making a card. It's about thinking about all the ways the special adults in our lives help us, guide us, protect us, and encourage us to always do our best.

Are you ready to accept your mission?

Then let's begin...

Operation Super Dad starts now!
