```text
CUBS PROGRAMME - TIME TRAVEL

                                Friday, 11 September 2026  | 17:30-19:00

17:30-17:40 | GRAND HOWL & OPENING
   Opening, inspection and Grand Howl.
17:40-17:50 | THE GREAT FIRE OF LONDON - Steam Release
  Run outside in a large area. Start with one or two Cubs carrying red/orange sheets or cloths as the fire.
  When a Cub is caught, that Cub takes a cloth and becomes part of the fire. Continue until the whole of London is
   burning.
  Keep the cloths for the later distress-signalling challenge.
17:50-18:00 | SURVIVE THE FIRE
  Move into the hall. Teach and physically practise stop, drop and roll, crawling below smoke, raising the alarm, getting
   out and staying out, what to do if clothing catches fire, and what to do if smoke blocks the normal exit.
  Keep the session active. Cubs should demonstrate the actions rather than only discuss them.
18:00-18:05 | YARN - LONDON'S BURNING
   Tell a five-minute yarn about the Great Fire of London and connect the story to the fire-safety skills just practised.
18:05-18:20 | KNOTS ACROSS TIME
   Split the Pack. Baloo teaches the younger Cubs the reef knot. Mang teaches the 10-year-old Cubs the sheet bend and
   explains its use in attaching a flag to a halyard.
   Pair each 10-year-old Cub with a younger Cub. Each older Cub teaches the younger Cub the sheet bend until the
   younger Cub can tie it independently.
   Observe the older Cubs individually so the knot-teaching requirement can be assessed.
18:20-18:25 | JUICE & BISCUITS
   Five-minute refreshment break.
18:25-18:40 | LOST IN TIME - COMPASS CHALLENGE
  Move outside. Cubs navigate without GPS: identify North using a compass, use basic compass directions and complete
  a 6-10 move compass trail.
  Run the activity as a Six-based challenge with course markers or clues leading to the final time portal.
18:40-18:55 | SIGNAL THROUGH TIME
   Split the Pack into teams across the darker part of the grounds. Use torches and mirrors to send and receive signals.
  Teach or reinforce SOS in Morse: ... --- ...
    Prioritise the 10-year-old Cubs from 18:40-18:45. Once they have demonstrated the light/reflective signal, they break
  away to the Stranded challenge while younger Cubs continue signalling.
18:45-18:55 | 10-YEAR-OLDS: STRANDED DISTRESS CHALLENGE
   Scenario: You are stranded. There is no mobile phone and the torch is no longer available. A rescue party is looking for
   you. Demonstrate ways to attract attention.
  Make whistles, pots and pans, wooden spoons and the red/orange sheets available without immediately explaining their
   uses.
  Cubs should devise an audible distress signal and construct a large ground SOS with the sheets. Count the earlier
   torch/mirror signal as the third demonstrated method.
   Prompt only if needed: What could someone hear from far away? What could an aircraft see? What could you make
  much bigger than yourself?
   Ensure each older Cub personally demonstrates three distress-signalling methods.
18:55-19:00 | CLOSING
   Quick recap, notices and closing.


EQUIPMENT / PACKING LIST

 Activity                                             Items required
 Great Fire / Distress                                         Red/orange sheets or large cloths; enough pieces for the fire
```
