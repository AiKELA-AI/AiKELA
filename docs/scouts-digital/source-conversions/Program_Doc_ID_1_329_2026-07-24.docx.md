| Sometimes the best help is simple and calm—placing someone in the recovery position helps protect their airway while we wait for an adult or emergency services.<br> |  |  |  |  |
| 18:25 | 15 | DEMONSTRATION: Recovery position<br><br>The first thing to do, without delay, is to lay the victim on their side, open the airway and keep it open. If the head is tilted forwards this may obstruct the airway and hinder breathing, so tilt the person’s head back as far as it can go. Very often breathing will start again quite naturally. A person who cannot breathe naturally willstruggle for breath, and their face and lips will turn blue. <br><br>As soon as natural breathing starts, the heaving movements of the chest will stop, and the colour of the face and lips will turn pink again.<br><br>Allow the Cubs to practise putting each other in the recovery positions.<br> | <br> | Darzee |
