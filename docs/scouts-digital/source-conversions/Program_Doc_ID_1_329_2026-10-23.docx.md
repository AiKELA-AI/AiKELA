GOLD WOLF – FINDING NORTH WITHOUT A COMPASS

On 23 October 2026 at 18:15, the sun in Port Elizabeth (Gqeberha) will be sitting just under 7° above the horizon, preparing to set at 18:37.

At exactly 18:15, the sun will be positioned at an azimuth of 261°. This means it is tracking almost perfectly due West (which is 270°), sitting just 9° slightly to the left (South) of pure West.

Your 18:15 Navigation Alignment

Because 261° is virtually due West to the naked eye, you can safely use the sun as a direct compass marker:

1. Face the low sun directly: Turn your body until you are looking right at the sun hovering above the horizon. You are now facing West.

2. Extend your arms straight out: Hold your arms up straight to your sides to form a “T” shape with your body.

3. Find North: Look along your right arm. Because the sun sits just 9° left of perfect West, your right arm will point exactly True North. Your left arm will point directly South, and East will be directly behind you.

SILVER WOLF

![Embedded source visual](<assets/Program_Doc_ID_1_329_2026-10-23.docx/image2.jpeg>) The Sunridge Park Guide to the Sun

1. Sunrise (East) — Towards the Baakens Valley & the Ocean

What to tell them: "Point your left hand down towards the Baakens River Valley or Stella Londt Home side. That is East! If you kept walking that way, you'd end up at the beachfront. Every morning, the sun wakes up right over those trees and hills." 🌅

2. Midday (North) — Towards Sunridge Village & Cape Road

What to tell them: "Turn and face straight towards Sunridge Village Shopping Centre or Cape Road. That is North. At lunchtime, the sun is high up in the sky right above the shops! Because the sun is shining from the shops' side, your shadow will stretch out long behind you towards the south." 🏢

3. Sunset (West) — Towards Framesby & the Mountains

What to tell them: "Point your right hand towards Framesby High School or out towards the mountains. That is West. At the end of the day, the sun travels over there and goes down behind the houses and the far-away hills." 🌇

👣 The Sunridge Park Shadow Challenge

Since you'll be right there, have them try this quick physical trick:

Have them stand facing Sunridge Village (North).

Point their left arm out to the side (East / Sunrise).

Point their right arm out to the side (West / Sunset).

Now, tell them to look at the ground behind them. Their shadows are pointing straight away from the shops, down into the valley (South)!
