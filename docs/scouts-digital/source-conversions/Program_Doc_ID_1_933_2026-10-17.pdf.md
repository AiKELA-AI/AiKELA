           bucket with a cloth. Have one bucket for each Six (with the

            Scouter will tell the Cub what to find by feeling around in
           the water. When the item has been found, the Cub will
            take it out of the bucket and run back to their Six. The next
          Cub will run up and get told of a different item to find in

 4:00 pm     Activity 9: Drip, Drip Splash                            Rebekah
 10 min      Similar to Duck-Duck-Goose
             •  Cubs to sit in a circle
             •  Assign "It": One player stands up with a small sponge
               soaked in water or a cup filled with water.
             •   "It" walks around the outside of the circle, lightly
                    trickling or tapping a tiny bit of water onto each
                  player's head and saying "Drip".
             •  When "It" chooses a target, they pour or dump the rest
                 of the water onto that person's head and shout
                 "Splash" (or "Drop" / "Drench").
             •  The splashed player jumps up and chases "It" once
              around the circle. "It" tries to run all the way around
              and sit in the empty spot before getting tagged.
             •  Next round: If "It" is caught, they go again. If "It" makes
                         it to the empty seat safely, the splashed player
              becomes the new "It".
 4:10 pm     Activity 10: Fire Rescue                                      Pia
 10 min    A Cub is blindfolded and sitting on a chair armed with a full
            water pistol, they are the Firefighters. As quietly as
              possible, the other Cubs (Fire) have to set the house on fire
         When the Firefighter senses a Fire is near, they must try
           and spray them whilst blindfolded. If the Cub is hit, they
             are out.
 4:20 pm     Activity 9: Make a Rain storm                                Nila
 5 min      As a Pack, start by tapping one finger on each hand
             together. Keep adding fingers until everyone is clapping
              (the storm). The storm goes away as you take one finger
           away at a time.
 4.25 pm    Closing                                                                All pack
 5 min        Flag, Prayer                                                scouters
           Law and Promise, Notices and reminders
            Badges
 4:30 pm    Dismiss                                           APS
