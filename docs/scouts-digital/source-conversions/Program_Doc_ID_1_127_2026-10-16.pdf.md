18:10     20 min      Silver Wolves: The Mad Hatters Mixed-up Objects                                Object cards x 20    Ikki & Akela

                    Task: Identify a simple object viewed from the side and the top.                             (10 matching pairs)

               "Oh no, Cubs! The Mad Hatter hosted a chaotic tea party, and Wonderland is
                   completely topsy-turvy! All of his favorite belongings have been mixed up, flipped
                 upside down, and spun around. Some are seen from the side, and some are seen
                from the top! The Hatter needs the clever Silver Wolves to use their eagle eyes and
                sharp memories to match the objects up and restore order before the Queen of
                 Hearts arrives!"

                 The Setup
                                       1. Divide the Silver Wolves into two equal teams and have them stand in two                              parallel lines facing the playing area.
                                2. Shuffle all 20 cards (10 matching pairs).
                                3. Lay the cards out face down on the floor in a neat grid (e.g., 4 rows of 5                              cards).

               How to Play
                                       1. Team 1 goes first. One designated player from Team 1 runs up and flips over                       any card to reveal the object.
                                2. That same player flips over a second card, trying to find its matching                           perspective (e.g., matching the side-view teacup with the top-view teacup).
                                3.  If it’s a match: The player takes both cards back to their team’s pile. Team 1                          scores a point.
                              4.  If it’s NOT a match: The player flips both cards face down exactly where they                        found them. Turn passes to Team 2.
                              5. The game continues back and forth until all 10 pairs have been collected.
                                6. The team with the most matching pairs wins!
                   The objects cards are:
                                               1.  Tea cup
                                       2. Top hat
                                       3.  Key
                                     4. Rose
                                     5.  Bottle
                                       6. Cake
                                              7.  Mushroom
                                      8.  Pocket watch
                                            9.  Flamingo
                                        10. Playing Card
