Swimming Rules:

• Never swim alone; use the ‘buddy system’.
• Never swim in unknown waters (that is, in dams, rivers or pools you do not know).
• Never dive into water if it is not clear or if you do not know how deep it is. You could hit something and hurt
yourself.
• Never swim with your clothes on – they get heavy.
• Never swim and drink alcohol. Also, never swim when supervised by adults that have been drinking/are
drinking alcohol as their response time to help will be impaired.
• Never swim when you are too … anything … too tired; too hungry; too cold; too upset.
• Never run around the pool, river or dam, always walk.
• Never push people into a pool, river or dam, dive without first looking or duck your friends.
• Never eat or chew while swimming.
• Never use your hand to rescue a friend.
• Never jump in to save your friend – call for help!
• Never leave a child alone when they are near water (MOST important ‘never’ for Adult Leaders/supervising
volunteers or parents).
• Never be distracted by your cell phone while working with children in or near water.
