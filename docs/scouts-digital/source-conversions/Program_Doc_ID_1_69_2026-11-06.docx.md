Silver Wolfs:  Traditional Dancing

South African Zulu Dance – Indlamu

1. A little history for the Cubs

Indlamu is a traditional Zulu dance, often performed at cultural celebrations. It is famous for its strong rhythmic movements, high kicks and stamping feet.

2. What you need

Comfortable shoes or bare feet on a safe surface.

Colourful scarves (optional).

African drumming music or a steady drumbeat.

3. Easy dance movements

Teach the Cubs these simple movements:

| Step | Movement |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | March on the spot 8 times. |
| 2 | Clap hands 4 times. |
| 3 | Lift the right knee and gently stamp down 4 times. |
| 4 | Lift the left knee and gently stamp down 4 times. |
| 5 | Step to the right and clap. |
| 6 | Step to the left and clap. |
| 7 | Raise both arms and turn slowly around. |
| 8 | Finish with both arms raised in the air. |
