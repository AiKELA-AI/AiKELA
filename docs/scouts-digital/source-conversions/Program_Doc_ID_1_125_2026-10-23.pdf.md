Deaf awareness Activity: The Sign & Snack Challenge

Objective: Experience non-verbal communication while learning basic South African Sign Language
(SASL) signs related to food and courtesy.
Step-by-Step Breakdown:
Minute 1–2: Introduction & The Silence Rule
Gather the 7 Cubs and explain that today they are exploring how deaf or hard-of-hearing individuals
communicate.
Introduce the golden rule for the next few minutes: absolute silence. No talking, whispering, or making
vocal sounds—communication must happen entirely through eyes, hands, and facial expressions.
Minute 3–4: Quick Sign Training
Teach the group three simple SASL signs before starting the food prep:
Eat / Food: Bring fingertips together and tap them against your mouth a couple of times.
Please: Place a flat hand on your chest and move it in a circular motion.
Thank you: Touch your chin/lips with your fingertips and move your hand forward and down toward the
person.
Minute 5–8: Silent Snack Assembly
Set out a pre-prepared station with ingredients for a simple fruit skewers.
Have the Cubs work together to prep and share the snacks completely in silence, using only eye contact,
pointing, passing items politely, and using the new signs (Please and Thank You).
Minute 9–10: Quick Reflection
Break the silence and chat briefly:
How did it feel not being able to use your voice?
What made it easier or harder to understand each other?
Why is being inclusive and patient important when communicating with someone who is deaf or hard of
hearing?
Enjoy the snack together
