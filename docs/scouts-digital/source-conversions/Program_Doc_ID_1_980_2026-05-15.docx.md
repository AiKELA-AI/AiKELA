Yarn:

“Leo and the Very Silly Invisible Thread”

(Have Cubs sit in a circle, but encourage lots of actions and noises)

Raksha:

“Alright Cubs… this is a VERY serious story…”<br>“…about a VERY silly cub.”

“Are you ready?”

“Once upon a time, there was a cub named Leo… and Leo thought he was the bravest explorer EVER.”

<br>“Show me your bravest explorer face!”

“One day, Leo said…”

Leo (confident voice):<br>“I’m going on an adventure! I’m not scared of anything!”

“His mum raised one eyebrow…”<br>“You know that look mums give?”

Mum:<br>“You can go, Leo… but remember your invisible thread.”

Leader:

“Leo rolled his eyes…”

<br>“Ughhh, invisible thread… sure, Mum.”

“So off he went into the forest!”

“Show me how you walk like an explorer!”

(March around, encourage stomping sounds)

“He walked past tall trees…”

(Reach up high)<br>“…super tall trees…”

“…RIDICULOUSLY tall trees!”

“Then suddenly—”

(Jump a little)<br>“A noise!”

‘CRUNCH!’

“Leo froze.”

(Freeze dramatically)<br>“Everyone FREEZE!”

“Leo whispered…”

Leo: “It’s probably just… a tiny, harmless, completely non-scary… GIANT MONSTER!”

(Act scared, shaking)<br>“Show me your scared faces!”

“Then… a squirrel jumped out.”

(Quick movement)<br>“‘SQUEAK!’”

“Leo screamed…”

Leo: “AAAAAAAAAAH!”

“Then he looked again…”

“…and said…”

Leo: “Oh… it’s just a squirrel. I knew that.”

“But then… it got dark.”

“Make spooky noises!”

(Let Cubs go ‘oooooo’)

“Leo started to feel nervous.”

Leo: “Okay… maybe I’m a LITTLE bit scared…”

“Then he remembered…”

(Dramatic pause)<br>“…the invisible thread.”

“Leo said…”

Leo: “Fine, Mum… let’s try this invisible thread thing.”

“Cubs, hold your invisible thread!”

(Everyone pretends to hold string)

“Leo imagined the thread… but THIS time…”

“…it wasn’t just normal…”

“…it was…”

(Build suspense)

“GLOWING… SPARKLY… and… it made a silly sound!”

“Every time he pulled it, it went…”

‘BOIINGGG!’

“Try it!”

(Let Cubs say ‘BOIINGGG!’ and pretend to pull)

“Leo pulled again…”

‘BOIINGGG!’

“And far away,at home… his mum dropped her cup and said…”

Mum: “Leo! Stop pulling the thread, I’m making tea!”

“Leo laughed.”

Leo: “Okay… maybe this is actually helping.”

“Suddenly, he felt braver.”

“Stand tall again!”

“He said…”

Leo: “I’m not alone. I’ve got my mum… and my very noisy invisible thread!”

“So he walked home…”

“…past the trees…”

“…past the squirrel (who he DEFINITELY wasn’t scared of anymore)…"

“And when he got home…”

“He gave his mum a HUGE hug!”

“Show me your biggest hug!”

Mum:<br>“I missed you!”

Leo:<br>“I missed you too… even when I was being brave.”

Ask:

“What silly sound would YOUR invisible thread make?”

“What would happen if you pulled it too hard?”

“What’s something funny your mum does?”
