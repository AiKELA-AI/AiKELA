                The first thing to do, without delay, is to lay the victim application
                 on their side, open the airway and keep  it open. If the
                head is tilted forwards this may obstruct the airway and
                   hinder breathing, so  tilt the person’s head back as far
                  as  it can go. Very often breathing will start again quite
                     naturally. A person who cannot breathe naturally  will
                    struggle for breath, and their face and lips will turn blue.
                 As  soon  as  natural  breathing  starts,  the  heaving
               movements of the chest will stop, and the colour of the
