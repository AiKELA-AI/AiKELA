Materials

    1. 6 Wooden dowel sticks 90cm each
    2. 9 large rubber bands
    3.  Small plastic cup
    4. Punch
    5.  Scissors
    6.  Smallballs, pom poms, marshmallows

Use three of the wooden dowels and make them into a triangle by attaching the corners with rubber
bands.

Then the next three dowels you also attach as a triangle, but standing up like a tepee or a pyramid.
Add one at a time going upward from each corner of the first triangle. Then connect them with a rubber
band in the center on top. IT needs to have a lot of tension.

Next, take your plastic cup and punch three holes in it- evenly spaced around the top of the cup.
Cut three rubber bands in half and tie them through the holes in the cup. The other ends of the rubber
bands need to be tied to the top part of the catapult, and two bottom corners. You’ll need some bigger
rubber bands to make this work. If your rubber bands are not long enough (mine were not) tie on three
more runner bands to the ends so they can stretch all of the way down the dowels. The cup and rubber
bands together create a type of throwing arm.

Put a small ball, such as a ping pong ball, into the cup, pull back and let it fly!!! You have now finished
building a catapult. Everyone will be impressed. (And you don’t have to tell them how easy it was!) It is
seriously so much fun!
