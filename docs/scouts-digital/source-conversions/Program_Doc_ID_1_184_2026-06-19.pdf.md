Beetle Rules.

Dice Rolls and Beetle Parts
Each number on the die corresponds to a specific beetle part. The traditional assignment is:
    •   6: Body (1 piece) – must be drawn first
    •   5: Head (1 piece) – can only be drawn after the body
    •   4: Tail (1 piece) – can be drawn after the body
    •   3: Legs (6 pieces) – can be drawn after the body
    •   2: Antennae (2 pieces) – can only be drawn after the head
    •   1: Eyes (2 pieces) – can only be drawn after the head
Players must roll the correct number to draw the corresponding part. No part can be drawn out
of order (e.g., no head before the body, no eyes before the head).

Gameplay
The first player is chosen randomly by rolling the die. The player who ends with the highest number
goes first. Ties are rerolled until a first player is achieved. The play proceeds from the first player
clockwise around the table.

    1. Each player takes turns rolling the die.
    2. When a player rolls a number corresponding to a part they are eligible to draw, they add it
       to their beetle.
    3. The first player to complete their beetle shouts “Beetle!”, which ends the round
      immediately.
    4. Players score points equal to the number of beetle parts they completed during the
      round.
