      ●  Show how to use use a compass and care for it
How to use a compass

      1. Hold the compass flat in the palm of your hand.
     2. Keep it away from metal objects and electronic devices.
     3. Allow the needle to settle.
     4. Identify the north-seeking end of the needle (usually red).
     5. Turn the compass housing until the N marking lines up with the
       north-seeking needle.
     6. Identify North, South, East and West.

How to care for a compass

      ●  Don't drop or throw it.
      ● Keep it away from magnets and strong magnetic fields.
      ● Keep it clean and dry.
      ● Store it safely when not in use.
      ●  Don't leave it in extreme heat.
