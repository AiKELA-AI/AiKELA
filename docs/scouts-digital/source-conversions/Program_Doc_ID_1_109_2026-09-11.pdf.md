   ●  Story Setup: Akela gathers the pack inside the "cave." Explain that the Croods family needs to travel across a

 17:15   Steam Release Game: version of captains coming
(10 min)
        The Cave Rule: Just like Grug says "Never leave the cave!", water has
             strict rules. Introduce the SCOUTS SA Water Safety Rules framed as
           Prehistoric Cave Rules:
         Never swim alone — Always use the Buddy System.
        Look before you leap — Never dive into unknown or murky water
           (dams/rivers).
        Obey the Lifeguards — Respect tribal elders and pool/beach officials.
          Stay Calm & Float — Float on your back if tired or in trouble.
         Reach or Throw, Don't Go — Never jump in after someone struggling;
          use a line or branch.



 17:25   Advancement:                                                                      Aela
(15min)  Gold Wolf Station: Game Design (Gold Wolf Aptitude Task)
                ●  Objective: Gold Cubs design a floor game to teach the Silvers
                  about water safety.
                ●  Setup: Provide chalk, poster boards, dice, and scenario cards.
                ●  Task: Build a giant board game called "The Croods Safe Shore":
                        ○  Safe Spaces: "You checked for rocks before jumping in the
                       river—move ahead 2 spaces!"
                        ○  Danger Spaces: "You swam in a dam without a buddy—go
                       back 3 spaces!"
                        ○  Challenge Spaces: "Demonstrate a Starfish Float to cross
                           the river!
