2. Yarn / Story with a Meaning (10 min)

"Sophia, Ben and Arthur Make a Smart Choice"

Sophia, Ben and Arthur were riding their bicycles in the park one Saturday morning. They had packed water bottles, helmets and a small first-aid kit because Akela always reminded them to be prepared.

After a while they stopped near a playground. While they were resting, a friendly-looking man walked over and said, "I have some puppies in my van. Would you like to come and see them?"

Ben loved dogs and immediately wanted to go. Arthur was curious too. But Sophia remembered something she had learned at Cubs: never go anywhere with someone you do not know without permission from a trusted adult.

Sophia said, "No thank you. We need to stay where our parents know we are."

The man asked again, but the three Cubs moved away together and went to find a nearby park ranger. They explained what had happened.

Later that day, while cycling home, Arthur fell off his bike and scraped his knee. Ben wanted to keep riding, but Sophia checked that Arthur was okay first. They cleaned the cut, used a plaster from their first-aid kit and walked with him until he felt better.

When they got home, they told their parents about both events.

That evening their parents praised them. They had:

Stayed together.

Trusted their instincts.

Said "No" when something felt wrong.

Told a trusted adult.

Helped a friend safely.

Meaning:

A Cub is brave not because they are never scared, but because they make safe choices and seek help when needed.

SILVER WOLVES (7–8 YEARS)

Personal Safety Scenario Cards

CARD 1

A stranger offers you a chocolate and asks you to come with them to see their new puppy.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Say "NO", move away, and tell a trusted adult.

CARD 2

You are playing in the park and cannot see your parents anymore.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Stay where you are, find a trusted adult such as a police officer or security guard, and ask for help.

CARD 3

Someone you don't know asks for your name and where you live.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not tell them. Move away and tell a trusted adult.

CARD 4

You are home alone and someone knocks on the door.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not open the door. Speak through the door if necessary and contact a trusted adult.

CARD 5

A grown-up asks you to keep a secret that makes you feel uncomfortable.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Say "NO" and tell a trusted adult.

CARD 6

The phone rings while you are home alone. The caller asks if your parents are home.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not tell them you are alone. Say your parents cannot come to the phone and end the call.

CARD 7

A neighbour offers to drive you somewhere but your parents did not tell you about it.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Say "No thank you" and check with your parents first.

CARD 8

Someone grabs your arm and wants you to go with them.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Shout "NO!", pull away, run to safety, and tell a trusted adult.

GOLD WOLVES (9–10 YEARS)

Online & Personal Safety Scenario Cards

CARD 1

Someone you met online asks for your home address so they can send you a gift.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Never share personal information. Tell a parent or trusted adult.

CARD 2

A friend creates a group chat where people are making fun of another child.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not join in. Leave the chat and tell a trusted adult.

CARD 3

You receive a message saying you have won a prize and must give your full name, address and phone number.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not respond. Show a trusted adult.

CARD 4

Someone online says they are 10 years old, but you have never met them in real life.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Never assume online information is true. Do not share personal details.

CARD 5

You are angry with a friend and want to post something embarrassing about them online.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Do not post it. Think before you click or post.

CARD 6

A website asks for your full name, age, school and home address.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Ask a parent or trusted adult before entering any information.

CARD 7

Someone uses your photo online without asking permission.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Tell a trusted adult and report it if possible.

CARD 8

A friend shares their password with you and asks you to keep it secret.

❓ What should you do?

Expected Answer: Explain that passwords should be private and encourage them to keep it secure.
