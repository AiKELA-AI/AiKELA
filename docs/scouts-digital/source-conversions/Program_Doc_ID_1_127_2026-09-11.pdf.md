    ● Each Six receives a Secret Code. They need to work together to decipher it
    ●  Points awarded as follows:
      2 marbles if the Six only deciphers “Lord Baden Powell”
      3 marbles if the Six deciphers “Lord Baden Powell is our founder”
     4 marbles if they decipher the full code.
    ● Once the Six has deciphered their code, they receive a puzzle piece

18:06–18:11 - Round 3: Where does it go? (5mins)
    ● Each Six member gets a country card (in the colour of their Six)
    ●  In relay form, they have to race with their card and match it to the Country
      name, and then run to the back of their line, and the next Cub has a turn
    ● Cubs may attempt to correct an error when going up for their turn
    ●  Points awarded as follows:
      6 marbles if all countries are matched correctly
     Then 5, 4, 3, 2 & 1 for the number of correct matches
    ● Once the Six has completed their challenge, they receive a puzzle piece

      🇿🇦 South Africa                🇮🇳 India
      🇬🇧 United Kingdom             🇦🇺 Australia
      🇯🇵 Japan                     🇨🇦 Canada

18:11–18:15 - Round 4: Scouting Charades (4mins)
    ● Cubs sit in their Sixes
    ● One Cub is called up at a time
    ● A Cub activity is whispered in their ear (see examples below)
    ● They act it out for their Six, and the Six guesses the correct answer
    ● They have 20 seconds to act it out before the next cub has a turn
    ● The six earns a marble for every correct guess
    ● Once everyone has had a turn, they receive a puzzle piece
🏕️ Camping; 🔥 Making a fire; 🥾 Hiking; 🎵 Singing; 🎨 Crafting; 🪢 Tying a knot
