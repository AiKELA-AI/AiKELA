Now that our cave pack has gathered at the watering hole, inspected our emergency gear, and mastered the basic vines and ropes
needed for survival, it's time to escape the confines of the dark cave. Let’s run wild into the prehistoric wilderness to release some
energy with a fast-paced game.

  17:15  Steam release game: "Beware the Sabertooth!"                      Whistle
 (10 min)
         The players are members of the Crood family / Caveman Tribe. The
           leader (or animator) is Grug (or the Tribe Leader), armed with a loud
            whistle or horn (the "Warning Horn").Out in the dangerous prehistoric
             wild, roaming alone means getting eaten by a Sabertooth Tiger or a
          Bear Owl! The only way to survive is to stick together in Packs.

       How to Play

                    1. Scattered Tribe:
                       All the cavemen run around the area, making caveman
                noises (grunting, beating their chests, acting like
                  prehistoric animals, or foraging for "food").
                    2. The Danger Signal:
             When the Leader blows the whistle, everyone stops running
               and listens carefully. The Leader yells out a survival rule, such
                   as:
                       ○ "SABERTOOTH ATTACK! PACKS OF 4!"
                       ○ "ROCK SLIDE! GROUPS OF 3!"
                       ○ "NIGHTTIME IS DANGEROUS! CAVEMEN IN 5s!"
                    3. Form the Pack:
                  Players must quickly huddle together to form groups of that
                  exact size. To make it extra thematic, they must link arms or
                  put their heads together in a "family huddle" and yell "NEVER
            NOT BE AFRAID!").

                    4. Out of the Pack:
               Any player left without a complete group (or stuck in a group
                     that's too big) gets "caught by the Sabertooth" and sits out for
                  the next round (or becomes a "Sabertooth" who stalks around
                  the edge of the playing area).

After burning off our caveman energy, we must remember that even the most adventurous Croods need to understand their territory
and respect their tribe. Just as heroes respected their ancient kingdoms, we will gather to honor our own nation with its anthem and
map out our immediate cave surroundings.
