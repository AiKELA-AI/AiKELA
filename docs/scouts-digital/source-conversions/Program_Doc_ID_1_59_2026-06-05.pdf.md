This simple Cub Cart consists of two dowel axels cut to fit a sturdy box. The wheels (large plastic lids,
such as from an Oros bottle) are attached with wood screws to the axels. Drill pilot holes in the
dowels to make it easier for the Cubs to screw the wheels to the axels. Punch or drill holes into the
back and front of the cart, then thread a bit of string or rope through each hole. Fix in place with a
figure-of-eight knot on the inside of the cart. The cart can then be attached to other carts with a reef or
slip reef knot.
