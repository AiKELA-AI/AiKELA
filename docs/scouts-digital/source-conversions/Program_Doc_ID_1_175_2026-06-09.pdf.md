```text
•  A plastic bottle can take over 400 years to break down!
•  Many animals think plastic is food and can get very sick or
   even die from eating it.
•  Rain can wash litter from streets straight into rivers and the
   ocean.
•  Even small items like sweet wrappers can harm ﬁsh and birds.
•    If rubbish is left on the ground, it almost always ends up in
   water eventually.

    “If it’s on the ground, it can reach the water!”

2. Chemicals & Factory Waste
•  Some factories release dirty or toxic water into rivers if not
    controlled.
•   Oil spills can cover water and stop ﬁsh and plants from getting
   oxygen.
•  Chemicals in water can make animals sick—even if the water
   looks clean.
•  Soap, cleaning products, and paint washed down drains can
   harm rivers.
•  Once chemicals are in the water, they can be very hard to
   remove.

“Not all pollution can be seen—clear water is not always clean!”

3. Runoff – Rain Washing Dirt & Chemicals
•  When it rains, water washes everything on the ground into
    drains and rivers.
•   Fertilisers from gardens or farms can cause too much plant
   growth in water, which can kill ﬁsh.
•  Soap from washing cars or pavements can pollute rivers.
•   Oil from roads can be washed into stormwater systems.
•  Stormwater drains often go directly to rivers or the ocean—
   without cleaning the water ﬁrst.
```
