MAKING A WATER SCOPE.

Start with two yoghurt containers, with lids, that have had holes cut in them.

Half of Pack: glues the two narrow ends together using a glue gun. Ensure the glue goes
all around the edges to make a good seal. Once the glue has hardened, wrap duct tape
around the join to make a better seal.

Other half of Pack: takes off the lids (with the hole in them). Put cling wrap over the top of
each yoghurt container. Keeping the wrap tight, put the lid back on. This holds the cling
wrap. Also, put one, or two, elastic bands around the containers to help with holding the
cling wrap in place. Cut away excess cling wrap.

The two groups now swap to do the part they have not done.
