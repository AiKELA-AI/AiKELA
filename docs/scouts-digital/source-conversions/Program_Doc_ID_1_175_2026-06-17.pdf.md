```text
Welsh Symbol – The Red Dragon
“The dragon is a symbol of strength and bravery.”
    •  Found on the Welsh flag
    •   Very important in Welsh stories and legends
Castles & History
    •  Wales has more castles per kilometre than almost anywhere in the
        world!
    •   Caerphilly Castle is:
         o  Over 700 years old
         o  Surrounded by water (a moat 관)
         o  One of the biggest castles in the UK
Fun fact:
    •  One of the towers leans slightly!
Food in Wales
    •   Caerphilly cheese:
         o  Crumbly and creamy
         o   Originally made for farmers
    •  Welsh cakes:
         o   Small, sweet, flat cakes
         o  A bit like pancakes + biscuits combined
    •   Simple, hearty food (to keep warm in the cool weather!)
Culture & Traditions
“Wales is famous for music, storytelling and creativity!”
    •   Singing (especially in choirs)
    •   Storytelling and poems
    •   Art and crafts
Fun fact:
    •  Wales is sometimes called the “Land of Song”

Fun fact:

   There are more sheep than people in Wales!

      “Now that we know how our friends celebrate creativity… let’s create something special to send them!”
```
