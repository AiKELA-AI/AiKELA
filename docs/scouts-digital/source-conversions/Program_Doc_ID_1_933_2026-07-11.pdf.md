                Flag, Prayer Cub promise
               Register
 10 min       Inspection: Nails, handkerchiefs, badges and uniform

 2.40 pm      Activity 1: Steam release Game – Poachers and Rhino’s            Rebekah
 10 min
             Designate one cub as a poacher and have them stand in the centre of
             the play area. All the other cubs are the rhinos and should line up
               side-by-side at one end of the area.
            The poacher says “I want a Rhino” and every cub runs for the far side
               of the play area, trying to avoid being “poached” (tagged) by the
              poacher. If a runner is tagged, he or she then becomes a second
             Poacher and works with the first to tag other cub.
            The round or game is over when there’s only one cub left.

 2:50         Activity 2- Discussion on conservation                                 Pia
 10 min              -   Endangered species

 3.00 pm      Activity 3- Planting                                                   Prakashini

15 min     Wash and dry the bottles before making the pins. Put an inch of sand
               in each bottle using a funnel. The sand will weigh down the pins,
           making it harder to knock down. Set up the plastic bowling pins in
            the playing area and have each Cub, in turn, see if they can knock
          down the pins. The Cub who bowls down the pins fastest will be the
            winner
