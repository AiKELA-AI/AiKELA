Setting the Scene: Gather the Cubs in a circle around a mock campfire. You’ll need two leaders (or older Scouts)
acting as Grug (heavy-set, loud, overly cautious) and Guy (clever, inventive, energetic).

(GRUG stands in the center, frantically pacing back and forth, clutching a giant wooden club and checking
the sky.)

GRUG: (Panicked) Cubs! Gather around! Hide under the rocks! Don't touch anything! Don't look at anything! In
fact, don't even breathe the outside air!

(GUY walks in casually, holding a flaming torch or a staff, wearing his belt slotted with tools.)

GUY: Woah, Grug, take a breath. What's wrong now? Did a Punch Monkey steal your breakfast again?

GRUG: Worse, Guy! Much worse! We left the cave! Do you know what’s out here?! DANGER! Everywhere! Up
there—(points at the sun)—is the Giant Fire Rock! It cooks your skin if you stand under it too long! And down
here on the ground? Thorns! Scrapes! Tripping hazards!

GUY: (Laughs gently) Grug, the world outside isn't bad—you just have to know how to navigate it safely. With a
little brainpower, we can handle the wild!

GRUG: Oh yeah? What about inside the cave, huh? Just this morning Gran tripped over a loose rock, fell into the
fire pit, and nearly swallowed a glowing mushroom! Home isn't safe! Outside isn't safe!

GUY: That’s because Gran left her clutter everywhere! Accidents at home happen when we aren't careful. But if
you clean up hazards, home is safe. And as for outside? We have tricks! We use sun-armor for the sun, carry fresh
water, and look out for each other.

GRUG: (Squints suspiciously) What about those strange fermented berries and puff-smoke plants I saw down by
the river? They made a neighbor caveman dizzy, sleepy, and coughing all morning! He couldn't even hunt!

true cave-explorer keeps their body strong and their mind clear.
