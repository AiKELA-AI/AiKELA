Discussion Notes

Silver Wolves (8–9)

Uses of Trees

Ask Cubs to identify as many as possible:

      Produce oxygen

      Clean the air

      Provide shade

      Homes for birds and insects

      Food (fruit and nuts)

      Timber

      Paper

      Medicines

      Prevent soil erosion

       Beautify our environment

Endangered Species

Encourage Cubs to talk briefly about:

      Where the animal lives

      Why it is endangered

      How people can help protect it


Gold Wolves (10–11)

What is Conservation?

Conservation means looking after nature so that future generations can enjoy it too.

Three Natural Resources

      Water

       Soil

       Forests/Plants
        (Wildlife and clean air may also be discussed.)

How Can We Conserve Them?

Water

      Care for wild animals and plants.

      Leave places better than you found them.

      Respect other people enjoying nature.

      Take only photographs and memories.


Leader Tips

      Keep everyone moving to stay warm during the winter evening.

      Use questioning rather than lecturing—let Cubs discover the answers.

       Praise good observation and teamwork.

      Ensure frequent head counts and maintain the buddy system throughout.

        If visibility deteriorates, shorten discussion stops and prioritise a safe return before dark.
