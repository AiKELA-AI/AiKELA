Yes/No Questions

   1.  If your friend falls into deep water, should you immediately jump in after them? (No - call an
       adult or rescuer and use something that can reach or float.)

   2. Should children swim with a buddy? (Yes)

   3.  Is it safe to push someone into a swimming pool as a joke? (No)

   4. Should you listen to lifeguards and warning signs? (Yes)

   5.  If someone has a small graze, can clean water be used to wash it? (Yes)

   6.  If someone has a minor burn, should you put cream straight onto it? (No - cool it with cool
      running water.)

   7. Can even a strong swimmer get into trouble in water? (Yes)

   8.  If someone is in trouble in water, should you shout for help? (Yes)

   9.  Is a pool noodle the same as a proper life jacket? (No)

   10. Can scientists be Water Heroes? (Yes - scientists help us understand water quality, weather,
       pollution, disease and safety.)

   11. If a river is muddy and flowing fast after heavy rain, is it safe to swim in it? (No)

   12. If you can safely reach someone from the side using a long object, should you stay on land
       while helping? (Yes)


Water Hero Scenarios

Scenario 1: After heavy rain, the Cubs arrive at a river. The water is muddy, fast moving and branches
are floating past. Someone says, "Let's swim!" Your group includes a scientist, lifeguard, rescue worker
and first aider. What should each Water Hero say or do?

Scenario 2: Chilly has been swimming for a long time. He comes out shaking, cold and tired. What
should the Water Heroes do?

Scenario 3: Stuffy sees someone struggling in a swimming pool and says, "I'll jump in and save them!"
Should he? What should he do instead?

Scenario 4: Lambie is running next to the pool. She slips, falls and grazes her knee. What should the
Water Heroes do?
