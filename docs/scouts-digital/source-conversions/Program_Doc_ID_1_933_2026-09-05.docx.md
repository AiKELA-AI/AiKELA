Theme:  Knots

Date:  5 September 2026

Advancements:  Outdoor Challenge

Duty Six:  Brown

| Time | Activity | Person in charge | Equipment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2:30 pm<br>10 min | Opening<br>Flag, Prayer, Cub promise<br>Inspection: Nails, handkerchiefs, badges and uniform | All Pack Scouters |  |
| 2:40 pm<br>10 min | Activity 1: Steam Release – Statues<br>On the word go, cubs to run around, when the leader shouts “stop” cubs to freeze. | Meena |  |
| 2:50 pm<br>30 minutes | Activity 2: Knots<br>New cubs<br>Reef Knot<br>Thumb Knot<br>Figure of eight<br>Older cubs<br>Sheep Shank<br>Bowline<br>Sheet Bend | Prakashini<br>Meena<br>Prakashini<br>Pia |  |
| 3:20 pm<br>10 min | Activity 3: Turtle Soccer<br>Everyone stands in a circle facing inward with their feet spread wide. The outside of each player's foot must touch the outside of their neighbor's foot to form a continuous ring. Players pass and block a ball using only their feet while staying in position.  If the ball passes through, the player looses a hand, then the 2nd hand, third time, they are out. | Prakashini |  |
| 3:30 pm<br>10 min | Activity 4: Construction Model<br>Work in their sixes.  Each six to build the tallest tower in 10 minutes<br>![Embedded source visual](<assets/Program_Doc_ID_1_933_2026-09-05.docx/image1.emf>) | Pia |  |
| 3.40 pm<br>10 min | Activity 5:  Human Knot<br>First attempt in their sixes, then in 2 teams and final the whole pack.<br>Ask everyone to raise their right hand and reach into the center of the circle. Grab the hand of someone standing across or opposite from you. You cannot grab the hand of the person directly next to you<br>Ask everyone to raise their left hand, reach into the center, and grab the hand of a different person<br>Ensure every single person is holding hands with two different people, and that no one is holding hands with their immediate neighbors<br>Work together to untangle the human knot and form one large, unbroken circle of people holding hands. | Meena |  |
| 3:50 pm<br>10 min | Activity 6: Beat the clock<br>To be played in their sixes.  Each six to be given 10 chairs.<br>![Embedded source visual](<assets/Program_Doc_ID_1_933_2026-09-05.docx/image2.png>) | Prakashini |  |
| 4:00 pm<br>10 minutes | Activity 7: Tower of Buttons – Relay Game<br>A bucket of buttons is placed at the end of the hall.  In their sixes each cub will have a turn to collect 5 buttons and take them back to their six.  Aim is to build the tallest tower within a set time, without toppling it over. | Pia |  |
| 4:10 pm<br>10 min | Activity 8: Mushroom Ball Throw<br>Cubs stand in a circle with the leader in the middle.  The leader throws the ball to a cub who passes it back to the leader.  If the cub misses, he must stand on one leg. On the 2nd miss he goes onto his knees and on the 3rd miss he is out and must sit down. If the leader misses, the leader goes into the circle. | Meena |  |
| 4.20 pm<br>10 min | Closing<br>Flag, Prayer<br>Law and Promise, Notices and reminders<br>Badges | All pack scouters |  |
| 4:30 pm | Dismiss | APS |  |
