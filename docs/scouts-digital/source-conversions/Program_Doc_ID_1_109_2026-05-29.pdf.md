Guided by the gods, Perseus first sought out the Graeae, three old women who shared one eye and one
tooth between them. By stealing their eye, Perseus forced them to reveal the location of the Gorgons'
lair. Upon arriving at the Gorgons' island, Perseus waited until Medusa and her sisters fell asleep.
Knowing that looking directly at the Gorgons would mean instant death, he used Athena’s reflective
shield as a mirror to guide his movements safely. Wearing the Cap of Darkness to remain invisible, he
crept backward, looked only at her reflection, and used Hermes's sickle to cleanly cut her head off.
Lets use our sense of smell to find out what is poisonous and safe to eat…
