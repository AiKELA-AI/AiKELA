# Map Drawing

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Paper; instructions

## Instructions

![Source image](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Code_10_attachment.doc/Code_10_attachment_html_45fb9325.png>)

Draw on the sheet of paper an island to fill most of the page (example above)

Put a compass rose in the top right corner of the map as above.

In the centre of the island draw a lake.

From the lake draw a river running south into the sea.

Where it meets the sea, draw a house on the east bank.

On the far east of the island, draw some trees.

South of the trees, draw a windmill.

At the northeast point of the island draw a lighthouse.

Next to the lighthouse draw a heliport (where they land the helicopters – big circle with an H in the middle).

From the lighthouse draw a road to finish at the house.

At the west side of the island draw a police station in between two houses.

From the police station draw a footpath to the lighthouse along the coast.

From the houses, draw another road to the first house, crossing the river by a bridge.

Connect the windmill to the road by drawing another road.

On the northwest side, draw a hill.

Connect the hill to the lighthouse with a railway line.

Etc – you can make up your own instructions ….

At the end of the exercise tell them to mark an X at the police station so that they know where they are.
