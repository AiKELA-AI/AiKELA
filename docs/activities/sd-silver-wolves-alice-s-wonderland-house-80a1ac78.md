Silver Wolves: Alice’s Wonderland House
Task: Draw a simple map of a home/meeting place showing rooms and doorways
A message from Alice:
“Oh dear! I’ve lost my way around Wonderland!
All my friends are hiding in difefrent rooms of the Wonderland House, and I can’t
remember where everyone is.
Could you help me by drawing a map of the house? Then I can use your map to
fni d the Mad Hatter, the White Rabbit and all my new Wonderland friends.
There’s just one thing you need to remember . . . I need a map that shows what the
house looks like when you look down at it from the sky, like a bird fyling overhead.
Will you help me?”
● Each of the Silver Wolves are given a sheet of paper, a backing board and a
pencil.
● They begin at the gate and follow Akela around as she points out difefrent
parts of the hall, grounds, den, frie pit, etc. i.e. The Wonderland house &
garden, and helps them identify clues of whose room/area it is, where the
doors, windows etc. are
● Akela explains “aerial view” by having them imagine being a bird looking
down, or sitting in a plane looking at buildings and spaces from above
● Once the walkabout has completed, the Cubs fni d a place to sit and begin
to draw their map of the Wonderland House. Their guidelines are:
- Draw the outside of the house - what shape does the whole building
make?
- Draw the rooms inside - show the walls that divide the rooms
- Add the windows, doors and passages - how do you get from one
room to the other?
- Name the rooms - write the names or the characters or draw their
pictures inside each room
- Double check your map - can Alice use it to fni d her friends?
- Don’t forget to write your name on the map
● If time allows, Akela can test the SWs understanding by asking:
- Which room is next to the Mad Hatters tea room?
- Which room would Alice pass through to reach the White Rabbit?
- Can you show Alice two difefrent ways to get to the Cheshire Cat’s
room?
Let’s see how well you understood mapping:
