Challenge 1 – Escape the Palace - The Queen's Secret Code (+/-7 min)
In order to escape the palace we need to figure out this code
● The Cubs must use a cipher wheel to decode the Queen's secret message
about protecting Wonderland. (work in pairs)

WYVALJA DPSKSPML AYLLZ HUK MSVDLYZ RLLW YVHKZ HUK ILHJOLZ MYLL VM

Leader's answer: PROTECT WILDLIFE TREES AND FLOWERS KEEP ROADS AND

Challenge 2 and 3: Find Your Way Through the Queen's Maze (+/-20 min)
Now that you have deciphered the code the palace doors are open, but you are
trapped in the courtyard!
At this base split the GW’s into two groups - half do knots, half do compass and
then swap
● Show how to use use a compass and care for it
How to use a compass
1. Hold the compass fal t in the palm of your hand.
2. Keep it away from metal objects and electronic devices.
3. Allow the needle to settle.
4. Identify the north-seeking end of the needle (usually red).
5. Turn the compass housing until the N marking lines up with the
north-seeking needle.
6. Identify North, South, East and West.
How to care for a compass
● Don't drop or throw it.
● Keep it away from magnets and strong magnetic fields.
● Keep it clean and dry.
● Store it safely when not in use.
● Don't leave it in extreme heat.
● Use a compass and one other outdoor method to find North.
● Other methods to find North - Analogue clock and Shadow Stick
https://scoutwiki.scouts.org.za/wiki/Finding_North_and_South
● Tie the following knot - Sheepshank
Challenge 4: The Final Escape- Cross the Queen's Road (+/-7 min)
Beware of the Royal Trafcfi ! Now that you have escaped the courtyard you discover
a busy road filled with the Queens' playing-card soldiers.
● Correctly identify and explain 6–10 road signs to find a safe way across.
● Working in pairs each pair gets a sheet with the names of road signs on -
they must wander the courtyard looking for road signs and writing the
number that correlates to that road sign - for example stop sign = 1. - first
pair to finish can earn a marble each
"You've done it! You've escaped the Queens' palace, found your way through the
courtyard, crossed the Royal Road and opened the final gate! But the Queen is still
watching… Will you remember to look after Wonderland?"
