# Silent Man’s Sing Song

## Type
Playacting

## Equipment
Slips of paper; pencils; hat

## Instructions
The Pack sits in a circle. The leader gives each Cub a slip of paper. Cubs write the name of their favourite campfire song on their slip. The leader chooses two Cubs as “Silent Men”, collects the slips, mixes them in a hat, and has one of the Silent Men draw a slip. The two Cubs mime the song. When the Pack guesses it, everyone sings it.
