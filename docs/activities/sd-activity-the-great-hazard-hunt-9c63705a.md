```text
Activity: The Great Hazard Hunt

Instructions for Cubs:
Inspect the Scene: Lay out the Safety Problem visual. Look closely at the pictures to spot the household dangers, street risks, and internet safety problems.
Match the Rules: Read your Safety Rule cards together and use your Pritt Stick to paste each card next to its matching problem on the sheet:
Rule A (Home Safety): Pick up messy toys and stay clear of household chemicals to stop trips and poison risks.
Rule B (Pedestrian Safety): Always wait for the green light, look both ways, and cross strictly at marked crosswalks.
Rule C (Stranger Danger): Stay alert—never accept gifts or talk to strangers, and step away if approached.
Rule D (Unsafe Situations): If someone follows you or makes you feel uncomfortable, run away immediately to a public place and tell a trusted adult.
Rule E (Home Alone Safety): Keep doors locked, do not answer the door or phone for strangers, and stick to safe activities like reading.
Scouter Inspection: Shouting "Safety Clear!" alerts Akela to check your matches. Earn 1 point per correct match, with a 2-point speed bonus for the first team to correctly link all 5 safety hazards!
```
