# Bird Observation Rotation

## Type

Four birding work sessions; source says four 30-minute stations

## Equipment

Binoculars, photographs, bird calls, paper, crayons, birding books and feeder materials.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Stations in the source: identify five Southern African birds from nature/photos; recognise three birds by call/song (with a reference to Kim’s story); feed uncaged birds and record which visit/what foods they use; draw and colour a local bird. The feeder station names a toilet-roll feeder; the full sourced method is separately catalogued. Kim’s story is named but not supplied here. Neither the stated numbers nor the station list have been verified as current badge wording.
