# Edible Fire

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Pretzels; Nik Naks; hard sweets, etc.; water; pencil; paper

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Edible Fire Cubs build an edible fire, per Six, going through the steps needed to make a safe, controlled fire. After building the fire, Cubs leave the area and must write down (or draw) what they used to make their fire.
