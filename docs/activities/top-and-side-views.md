# Top and Side Views

## Type

Work session

## Instructions

Silver Wolves: Put a cup, closed cylindrical tin and rectangular box on a low table. Keep each object in the same upright position. Each Cub looks from the side, then directly above without climbing on furniture. Ask: What is it? What outline do you see from here? What changes when you look from above? Compare a circle above a tin with its rectangular side outline. Turn-taking: each Cub identifies an object from both viewpoints and explains the difference. Allow handling and verbal descriptions. This is preparation and an individual evidence opportunity for the Silver requirement, not an extra drawing requirement.

## Advancement / badge link

Silver Wolf Outdoor: Identify a simple object viewed from the side and top to illustrate the difference in shape. Cub Trail November 2019, p.12.

Source: [The Cub Trail — November 2019](../The-Cub-Trail-Nov-19.pdf). Participation alone does not award advancement.
