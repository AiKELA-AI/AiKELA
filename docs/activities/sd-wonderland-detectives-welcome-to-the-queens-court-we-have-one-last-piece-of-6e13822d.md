Wonderland Detectives Welcome to the Queens Court, we have one last piece of
evidence that has arrived this evening - come and collect the latest copy of the
Gazette and see what Alice has to say.
Now that you have seen the crime scene, done the interviews, studied the forensics
and figured out the disease you need to go to your old wolf with the answers to the
following questions:
● What made the guests sick and where did it come from?
● What could have been done to prevent it?
● What infectious disease did they have?
● What do you have to do before working with food?
● How does one make water safe for drinking?
The Queen’s Final Decree
“So nobody poisoned my tea after all!”
The guests became sick because germs from the sewage got into the stream water.
Remember, Cubs: water can look clean and still contain germs. Always drink water
from a safe source. If you don’t have safe drinking water, boiling it can kill harmful
germs.
And before touching food? Wash your hands with soap and clean water!
The Queen of Hearts has spoken:
“CLEAN HANDS! SAFE WATER! AND NO MORE GERMS IN MY TEA
