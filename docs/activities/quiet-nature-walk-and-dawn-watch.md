# Quiet Nature Walk and Dawn Watch

## Type

Outdoor observation variants; 30-minute night walk, 15-minute dawn watch, 60-minute ramble in source

## Equipment

Observation journal/paper and pencils; source venue route and competent supervision.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Night variant: take a short quiet walk, looking/listening for night creatures and recalling sights/sounds afterwards. Dawn variant: observe quietly and record on the named journal sheet, moving between permitted observation spots; the source says not every prompt must be completed. Day variant: a nature ramble around the venue. The source’s dam/ruins route is local and must not be inherited as a safe generic route.
