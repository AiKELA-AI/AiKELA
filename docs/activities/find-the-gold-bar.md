**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

SENSE TRAINING: Find the gold bar Stick a small picture up, somewhere in the playing area, of a piece of gold. When the Cubs find it they must not say a word to anyone. They need to whisper in the leader’s ear where they saw the gold and if they are correct, they are to sit quietly until everyone is finished.
