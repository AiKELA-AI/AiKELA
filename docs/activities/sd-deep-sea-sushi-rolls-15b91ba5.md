Deep sea sushi rolls
● Split the groups into SW and GW tables
● Work in pairs they must each prep the vegetable and make their sushi rolls
Each pair gets:
● A bowl of cooked sushi rice
● Small piece of carrot
● Small piece of cucumber
● Nori Sheet cut in half
● Knife x 2
● Chopping boards
● Clingwrap
When they are finished making their rolls, they can eat their sushi and then must
clean up their food prep station - washing and drying the dishes, and wiping the
table, and throwing scraps into the compost bucket
Quick decision about the compost bucket - and its purpose
Silver Wolves:
While busy prepping the food discuss the following with them
1. Know and understand the importance of good eating habits.
2. Show that you understand why you should keep your body, hands, nails and
teeth clean.
