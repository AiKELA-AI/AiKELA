# Singing in the Rain

## Type

CAMPFIRE SONG

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CAMPFIRE SONG: “Singing in the Rain” A campfire song is sung so that Cubs become familiar with the songs before attending campfires

## Advancement covered / badge link



- **Gold Wolf practice link:** [Lead a campfire song with others at a campfire.](../advancements/gold-wolf/community-lead-a-campfire-song-with-others-at-a-campfire.md) — Provides a song to lead. The Cub still needs to lead others singing it at a campfire.

- **Silver Wolf practice link:** [Sing three campfire songs with others at a campfire.](../advancements/silver-wolf/community-sing-three-campfire-songs-with-others-at-a-campfire.md) — Provides one campfire song to sing with others; Cubs need three different songs at a campfire in total.
