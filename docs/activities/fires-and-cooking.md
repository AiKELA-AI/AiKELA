# Fires and Cooking

## Type

Work Session

## Equipment

aluminum foil; knife

## Instructions

Cubs to lay and light their own fire and either boil a cup of water for tea or cook a very simple meal (two minute noodles).

An idea:

Banana Boats

Equipment: aluminum foil; knife

Ingredients: 1 banana per person; mini marshmallows; chocolate chips; Smarties; brown sugar; other optional bits.

Notes: If you peel back the peel and leave it attached at one end, it works better.

Instruction: The ¼ of the peel that is on the inside of the curve needs to be peeled out of the way. Leave the rest to hold the banana together. Try to leave the top peel attached. Cut out a groove of banana to make your boat. Fill the boat with whatever cargo you want – chocolate chips, marshmallows, brown sugar, etc. Lay the peel back on top. Wrap tightly in foil. Cook in coals for 4-5 minutes.
