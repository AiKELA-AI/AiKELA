## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Shops The names of various shops are written on the floor, e.g. butcher, chemist, supermarket, etc. The leader tells a story mentioning items for sale in the various shops. When an item is mentioned the Cubs run and form a queue at the correct shop. Anyone who goes to the wrong shop, anyone pushing the last Cub in the queue loses a life.
