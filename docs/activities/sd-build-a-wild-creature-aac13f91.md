```text
Build a Wild Creature
Give each Six five minutes to collect only loose natural
materials already lying on the ground, such as leaves, grass,
twigs, stones and seed pods. They then use these materials
to create a wild animal on the ground. Nothing may be
picked from a living plant.
Each Six has one minute to introduce its creature, give it a
name and explain:
• Where it lives
• What it eats
• How it protects itself
• Why its habitat should be protected
Finish by returning the materials to nature and leaving the
area tidy.
```
