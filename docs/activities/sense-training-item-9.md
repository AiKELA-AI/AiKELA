# Sense Training item 9

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

20 paper cups or something similar; 2 each of 10 different pairs of items to hide (e.g. marbles, sticks, stones, buttons, etc)

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Under the Cup When nobody is looking put the items in a long line and cover with the cups. Cubs are put into two teams and each Cub in each team will get a number – e.g. there will be a number 1 in team one and in team two. The leader calls out a number and the Cubs who were given that number must run and try and find a matching pair of items by turning over the cup. If the items underneath match, they get a point for their Six. If they do not match, they must carefully replace the cups and play passes to the next player.
