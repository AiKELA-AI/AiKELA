```text
Bird Detectives
Read the clues for each bird one at a time, beginning with
the most difficult. Cubs may raise their hands as soon as
they think they know the answer, but may make only one
guess per clue. The first Cub to identify the bird correctly
earns a bone for their Six. If nobody identifies it from the
verbal clues, reveal the bird’s picture as the final clue. Briefly
discuss its appearance, habitat, food and behaviour before
moving to the next bird.
```
