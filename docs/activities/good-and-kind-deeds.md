# Good and Kind Deeds

## Type

PLAYACTING

## Equipment

Cards on which are written different actions

## Instructions

PLAYACTING: Good and Kind Deeds One Cub from the circle picks up a card and tries to illustrate, without speaking, the action written on the card. The Cub who guesses correctly takes the next card from the pile and so on. Help with reading; Yes; Please; Come here; I’m frightened, etc.
