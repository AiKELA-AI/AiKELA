“Silent Damage”
◆ Step 1:
• Label one cup: “Normal Rain”
• Label the other: “Acid Rain”
◆ Step 2:
• Cup 1: Add clean water
• Cup 2: Add water + a splash of vinegar
Explain: “The vinegar makes the water acidic—like acid rain.”
◆ Step 3:
• Place a leaf/apple/chalk piece into each cup

• Immediate: look for bubbles (especially on chalk)

Colour changes
o
Softening
o
Damage
o
• Vinegar (acid rain) may:
Make chalk fizz or dissolve
o
Make leaves/apple look damaged faster
o
Ask:
• “Which one is changing faster?”
• “Which looks healthier?”
“Acid rain doesn’t always damage things instantly—but over time
it can harm plants, animals, and even buildings.”
How It Affects the Planet
• Trees get weaker and struggle to grow
• Fish can die if water becomes too acidic
• Rocks and buildings can slowly wear away
• Soil loses important nutrients
How We Can Help
• Use less electricity
• Walk or carpool when possible
• Don’t burn rubbish
• Look after nature
“Cleaner air = cleaner rain!”
