Activity 4: Traffic Signals
Cubs get into their Sixes. Traffic signals with the names of the traffic signals and cut out and placed at the end of the hall. Each cub must run, one at a time, to collect 1 piece at and time and bring it to their six. They must match the sign to the description.

Go over the signals. The winner is the six who has the most matches
