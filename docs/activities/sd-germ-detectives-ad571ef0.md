```text
Germ Detectives
Divide the Cubs into three Groups
Group 1 Coldy the Sneezeball (Blue)
● Disease: Cold/Flu
● Spread: Coughing, sneezing, handshakes, sharing food
● Stop: Washing hands, tissues, covering coughs and sneezes.
Group 2 Chicky the Spotty Chicken (Yellow)
● Disease:Chickenpox
● Spread:Touching spots, close contact, sharing food.
● Stop:Staying home, vaccine, do not scratch
Group 3 Tummy Bug Timmy (Green)
● Disease:Stomach Bug (like gastro / “tummy flu”)
● Spread:Dirty hands, food not washed, sharing food.
● Stop:Washing hands before eating, cleaning food, safe water.
How to play:
Hide cards around the hall with pictures depicting how the infectious disease gets
spread - cubs must search for their cards (they will be in the colour of their Germ
character -
“Three sneaky germs are on the loose! Coldy the Sneezeball is spreading sneezes,
Chicky the Spotty Chicken is spreading itchy chickenpox and Tummy Bug Timmy is
making everyone run to the toilet!. We need Germ Detectives to find out how they
spread — and how to stop them!”
```
