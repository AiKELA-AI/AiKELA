# Picking fruit Tie the fruit onto the rope

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Rope; different fruits; blindfolds; containers.

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Picking fruit Tie the fruit onto the rope. Cubs are blindfolded. Cubs go along the rope and ‘pick’ the fruit off the trees. Fruit will be in a jar/container and tied to the rope (or handed a piece of the fruit for them to identify).
