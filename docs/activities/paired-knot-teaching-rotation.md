# Paired Knot-teaching Rotation

## Type

Skills rotation; six 10-minute slots plus hanking

## Equipment

Ropes and competent knot instructors; source diagrams.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Two groups rotate between paired teaching stations. The source pairs reef/slip reef, then clove hitch/round turn and two half hitches, then sheet bend/Fisherman’s knot. Groups swap each 10 minutes. Finally practise a chain hank to put long ropes away. This is a station format, not step-by-step knot or chain-hank instruction; use actual checked demonstrations.
