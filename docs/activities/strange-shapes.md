**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

SENSE TRAINING: Strange Shapes This game is to help develop the artistic side of the Cub. Give to each Six an identical sheet of paper with about six strange shapes drawn on them. You could have a square, a triangle, a circle and so on. They can turn these shapes into anything they care to draw but you must still be able to recognise the original shape. Each Cub in the Six or team must do one and when they are all done you can ask another of the Leaders to judge which are the best set.
