```text
Activity: Seed Hunt & Conservation Challenge

Instructions
Base 1: Seed Hunt & Sprouting Jar:
Provide Cubs with a glass bottle to use as a water scope to retrieve the soaked seeds.
Have Cubs transfer their seeds in a plastic pocket lined with damp cotton wool to take home and watch grow.
Base 2: Compost Collection:
Ask everyone to deposit all organic waste and compostable materials into a shared bucket.
Explain that this will be turned into rich compost to feed their sprouted seeds once they are transplanted.
Base 3: Home Energy Audit (Discussion):
Gather the Cubs to identify everyday household activities that waste or consume electricity and energy.
Discuss practical steps they can take at home to conserve energy (e.g., turning off lights, unplugging unused chargers, keeping fridge doors closed, switching to cold water washes).
```
