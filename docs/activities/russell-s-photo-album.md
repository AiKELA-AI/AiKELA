**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

SENSE TRAINING: Russell’s Photo Album The pictures are set out as a memory game, face down. The Cubs take it in turns to turn over two cards. The object of the game is to find a matching pair. Once a matching pair have been found, the Cub who found the pair, keeps the pictures. The Cub with the most pictures is the winner.
