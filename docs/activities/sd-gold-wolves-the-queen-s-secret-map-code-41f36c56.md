```text
Gold Wolves: The Queen’s Secret Map Code
Task: Recognise six to ten map symbols
"Attention, Gold Wolves! The Queen of Hearts has a severe problem. She has
discovered that her top-secret street map of Wonderland is locked behind a
classifeid Royal Map Code! She knows these strange geometric symbols stand for
crucial locations like hospitals, police stations, and railway lines, but she cannot
remember which symbol is which.
The Queen has ordered the clever Gold Wolves to learn the Royal Map Code
immediately. If you can master the code and match the symbols to their
descriptions, you will gain access to the kingdoms' map. If you fail... OFF WITH
YOUR HEADS!"
The Setup
1. Divide the Gold Wolves into two equal teams and have them stand in two
parallel lines facing the playing area.
2. Shufefl all 20 cards (10 matching pairs).
3. Lay the cards out face down on the fol or in a neat grid (e.g., 4 rows of 5
cards).
How to Play
1. Team 1 starts. One Cub runs out and filps over any single card to show the
group.
2. That same Cub filps over a second card, trying to fni d its matching partner
(e.g., matching the map symbol with the description).
3. If it’s a match: The player takes both cards back to their team’s pile. Team 1
scores a point.
4. If it’s NOT a match: The player filps both cards face down exactly where they
found them. Turn passes to Team 2.
5. The game continues back and forth until all 10 pairs have been collected.
6. The team with the most matching pairs wins!
```
