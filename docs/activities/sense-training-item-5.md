# Sense Training item 5

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Different food items to taste: biltong, raisins, rusks, etc; pencil and paper. Be aware of food allergies.

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Ivik’s Food Bag The items to taste are put into food bags and given to leaders who are spread over a fairly large area. Cubs must run from leader to leader (in a given order) and are given a piece of food to taste. After completing the course, they must list what they tasted. This can be done in Sixes or individually.
