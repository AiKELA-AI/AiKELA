# Paper Elephant Chain

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Elephant template; pencils; paper; scissors

## Instructions

Instructions:

Fold a sheet of A4 paper (or similar) in a concertina style making sure that the width of the fold will accommodate the width of the elephant template.

Position the template on the folded paper so the arrows marked on the template are against the folds in the paper.

Fix the template into place on the folded paper with paper clips or something similar.

Cut along the outline of the shape, holding the folded paper firmly and repositioning the paper clips as required.

![Source image](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Ging_Gang_Gooli_attachment.doc/Ging_Gang_Gooli_attachment_html_2f44d139.gif>) Cut through all layers at once.

DO NOT CUT ALONG THE FOLD LINES.

Now open up the folds to reveal your paper chain. If you want a longer chain, you can join them using sticky tape.
