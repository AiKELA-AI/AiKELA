```text
Ladders

Detailed source attachment:
Instructions: Players split into pairs of equal size or ability.
Pairs sit on the floor facing each other in two long parallel lines with their legs
stretched straight out, touching feet with their partner. The gaps between each
pair's legs form the "rungs" of the ladder.
Each pair is given a number
The leader stands at one end of the room and calls out a number:
Both players in that pair stand up as quickly as possible.
They run down the middle of the "ladder," carefully stepping over the legs of
the other sitting pairs.
When they reach the far end of the ladder, they run around the outside of the
sitting players, heading back toward the opposite end.
They enter the middle of the ladder again from the other end and race back to
their original spot.
The first runner to sit back down in their original spot with their legs stretched out
earns a point for their team
```
