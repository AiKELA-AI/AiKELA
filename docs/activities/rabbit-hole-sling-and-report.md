# Fallen Down the Rabbit Hole — Sling and Incident Report

## Type

First-aid-themed skills station; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Triangular bandage/scarf, rope and incident-report forms.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

The source prompts “hazards, hands, hello, help”. Silver Cubs practise a triangular/scarf arm sling secured with a reef knot; Gold Cubs record what happened, where/who, injuries, what they did and what next. No complete sling teaching diagram or blank report form accompanies this source. This preserves the practice outline, not a clinical treatment procedure.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Use supervised pretend practice with a comfortable, willing participant; do not manipulate a real suspected fracture. Obtain authoritative first-aid instruction and a suitable report form before use. Do not create children’s real medical records in the shared library.
