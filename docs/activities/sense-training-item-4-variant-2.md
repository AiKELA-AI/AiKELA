# Sense Training item 4

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Egg box with 10 sections; natural objects found around the hall

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Find It A different natural object is placed in each section of the egg box. Cubs are given a minute to study the egg box and then have to go out and look for the identical objects and place them in the identical position in the egg box.
