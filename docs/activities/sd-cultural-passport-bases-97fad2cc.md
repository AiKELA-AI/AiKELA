```text
Cultural Passport Bases
Each Six will visit four difefrent countries. In every country they will complete a
mission and receive something special for their passport.
(4 bases × approximately 10 minutes)
Base 1: Japan (Chikai)
● Greeting: Cubs are asked to remove their shoes and practise greeting one
another with a bow. Learn to say: ‘Konnichiwa’ = Hello
● Culture: Learn 5 quick facts about Japan.
● Flag Challenge: Work together to assemble the Japanese fal g.
● Sensory: Taste a small amount of green tea.
● Passport: Receive a pink cherry blossom 🌸
Base 2: India (Kaa)
● Language: Learn: Alvida = Goodbye
● Sensory: Smell curry powder/spices
● Culture: Learn about Diwali, the Festival of Lights, then, one Cub at a time,
has an opportunity to strike a match correctly, and light a tea-light candle
● Five facts: Learn 5 quick facts about India
● Passport: Receive a tiger 🐅
Base 3: Birmingham, England (Akela)
● Language: Learn to say: Hello in British Sign Language then challenge the
Cubs to greet one another without speaking
● Sensory: Smell & taste chocolate (Explain the connection with Bournville and
Cadbury in Birmingham)
● Birmingham Challenge: Cubs receive a picture puzzle which is put together
incorrectly; they need to work together to correct the picture
● Five facts: Learn 5 quick facts about Birmingham, England
● Receive a chocolate bar 🍫
```
