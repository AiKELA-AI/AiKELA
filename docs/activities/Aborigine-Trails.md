# Trails

## Matches in the supplied Scouts South Africa requirement sheets

The historical POP attachment includes two tasks whose wording directly matches the supplied current-level Trail requirements:

- **Silver Wolf — Trails:** “Know how to make and recognise at least ten woodcraft signs.” The POP attachment says to know how to make and recognise at least 10 woodcraft signs. ([requirement sheet, source page 3](../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#source-page-3))
- **Silver Wolf — Trails:** “Follow a woodcraft trail.” This wording appears in both the POP attachment and the requirement sheet. ([requirement sheet, source page 3](../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#source-page-3))
- **Gold Wolf — Trails:** “Lay a woodcraft trail where 20 signs are used for others to follow.” This wording appears in both the POP attachment and the requirement sheet. ([requirement sheet, source page 3](../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#source-page-3))

These wording matches identify possible programme links; they do not show that merely running this work session completes any requirement. The Scouter still needs to check the full requirement and observe the Cub doing it.

## Other tasks mentioned in the POP attachment

- “Follow a simple trail” may relate to Silver Wolf’s “Follow a woodcraft trail”, but the attachment does not specify that the trail is a woodcraft trail. The match cannot be confirmed from the available wording.
- “Take part in a scavenger hunt” has no matching Trail requirement in the supplied Silver Wolf or Gold Wolf sheets.
