# Dry-land Rescue-rope Practice

## Type

Skills practice; 15-minute first station and later 20-minute practice

## Equipment

Source: sealed plastic bottle/foam float tied to an 8–10 m rope, blue tarp and a target 5 m away.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

On dry land, the source introduces anchoring the tail end under the foot, coiling the line, an underarm throw past a target, and steady retrieval. Gold Cubs also practise later. The source is a practice outline, not instruction to perform an actual water rescue. Use a trained leader’s checked teaching method before running it.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** No live water or people in the target area. Keep ropes away from necks and bodies and supervise every throw.
