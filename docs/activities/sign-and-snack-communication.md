# Sign and Snack — Non-verbal Communication

## Type

Communication station; approximately 10–12 minutes

## Equipment

Prepared snack ingredients, safe food utensils and checked sign references. Source schedule suggests pap/wors; detailed instructions suggest fruit skewers.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Introduce a brief no-talking communication exercise. Teach the three signs labelled eat/food, please and thank you in the source, then let Cubs assemble/pass snacks using agreed non-verbal communication. End with reflection on what helped understanding and how to be patient and inclusive. **The source’s SASL labels and demonstrations are unverified: obtain an accurate SASL reference or competent instructor before teaching them as SASL.**

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Check allergies, wash hands and supervise food tools. Do not describe a short silence game as experiencing deafness or equate deafness with inability to communicate. Cubs can opt for an accessible communication method. Retheming may change the fictional setting while retaining the sourced mechanic; identify the adaptation and this source.
