```text
Reflex grab
• Cubs line up shortest to tallest, split into two teams
• Cubs stand in two lines facing each other, holding hands
• The cubs furthest from the bean bag also hold hands so
that it now becomes one long chain.
• Identify which cub is in the centre of the chain and have
that cub SIMULTEANOUSLY squeeze the hands of the two
cubs on either side
• The cubs whose hands were squeezed then squeeze the
hand of the person next to them
• The squeeze is passed on until it reaches the last two cubs
who are closest to the bean bag
• Once the hands of the cubs at the end of the line are
squeezed, they grab the bean bag.
• Aim of the game is to grab the bean bag first
This reflect game remind us of being punctual. Why must we be
punctual. It is not polite to be late and that being late is giving in
to themselves, as with a little effort they can always be on time.!!
```
