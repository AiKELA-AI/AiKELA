# The

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

3 beans/small stones or tokens per Cub

## Instructions

The Cubs sit in a circle, one Cub in the centre holds the beanbag. The leader calls out a letter and the Cub in the centre throws the beanbag to any player. He/she must name something good to eat beginning with that letter before the Cub in the centre counts to 10. If he/she fails, or mentions something already used, he/she surrenders a bean.
