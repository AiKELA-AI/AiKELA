You might like to try this instead: Miniature Fruit Trees

Make pots out of empty orange or grapefruit skins after you have eaten the flesh. Give them a coat of clear varnish and let them dry out. When dry, fill your pots with soil and plant a pip in each. Label them so that you know which is in which pot. Water, being careful not to over-water as there are no drainage holes. Keep moist and in a dark place until the shoots are well-established. Then put in a sunny spot – about three to four hours of sunlight a day is needed from here on. If the roots push through the skin, cut them off and you will get a perfect miniature tree. When the time comes that the pot will definitely not hold the plant any longer, transplant into a larger pot or the garden.
