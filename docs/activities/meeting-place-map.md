# Meeting Place Map

## Type

Work session

## Instructions

Silver Wolves: Walk around the accessible meeting-place rooms together with a Scouter. Notice how rooms connect and where doorways are. Do not enter locked rooms, stores or kitchens in use. Return to tables. Each Cub draws their own simple overhead map of the meeting place, showing the different rooms and doorways as gaps in walls. Labels are helpful but artistic quality and exact scale are not criteria. Each Cub points out the rooms and doorways to a Scouter; check the drawing against the actual layout. Keep completed drawings as evidence. Offer larger paper, a chunky pencil or a scribe following the Cub’s directions when needed.

## Advancement / badge link

Silver Wolf Outdoor: Draw a simple map of your home or meeting place showing the different rooms and doorways. Cub Trail November 2019, p.13.

Source: [The Cub Trail — November 2019](../The-Cub-Trail-Nov-19.pdf). Participation alone does not award advancement.
