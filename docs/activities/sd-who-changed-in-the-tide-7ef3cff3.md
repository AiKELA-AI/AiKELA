```text
"Who Changed in the Tide?"
How to Play
Have the Cubs sit or stand in a circle.
Choose one Cub to stand in the middle as the Sea Creature.
Everyone studies the Sea Creature carefully for 20 seconds.
"Look closely! Notice everything – shoes, sleeves, hair, badges, glasses..."
The Cubs close their eyes and put their hands over them.
Quietly ask the Sea Creature to change ONE thing about their appearance. (Have
some items - eg scarves, caps, etc)
Everyone opens their eyes.
The frist Cub to spot the change puts a hand up (no shouting out).
The Cub who spotted it correctly becomes the next Sea Creature.
```
