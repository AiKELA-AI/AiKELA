# Nature Collection and Observation Bases

## Type

Nature stations; source Happy Bots slots are four 20-minute sessions

## Equipment

Leaf/seed labels, paper/booklets, magnifying glasses and craft materials; plaster materials for the separate casting task.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Happy Bots lists four stations: make plaster casts of three kinds of leaves; collect, press and label ten kinds of leaves; observe three insects and make notes; collect and label eight kinds of seeds. The camp also names collecting/identifying ten leaves and an insect safari with magnifiers. These quantities are source activity tasks, not verified current badge requirements. The collection/observation outlines do not give plant identification keys, pressing instructions or a plaster recipe. Record observations and identify any missing teaching material before use.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Get landowner permission, avoid damaging plants or disturbing animals, and keep unidentified plants/seeds out of mouths. Supervise plaster handling; obtain a sourced casting method rather than guessing a mix ratio.
