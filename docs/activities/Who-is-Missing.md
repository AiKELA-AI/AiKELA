# Who Is Missing?

## Type
Sense training

## Equipment
Blindfolds or scarves

## Instructions
Have the Cubs sit in a circle. They cover their eyes with scarves or blindfolds. Once everyone’s eyes are covered, one Cub leaves the room. Ask the Cubs to say who has left.
