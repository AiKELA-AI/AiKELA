# Rain Making

## Type

## Equipment

## Instructions

The leaves are rustling just before the rain starts. Just a whisper of sound – rub thumbs against first two fingers.

The first raindrops are pattering down. Rub palms of hands together slowly.

The rain is really falling now, cross your arms and run your hands up and down on your arms as if you are cold.

Now the drops are bigger. Pat your knees with your hands as fast you can.

Here comes the downpour. Tap your feet on the ground – very fast – but lightly. Then reverse the order until the rain stops.
