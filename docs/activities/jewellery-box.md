# Jewellery Box

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

A variety of different beads, gem stones, etc.; pencil; paper

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Jewellery Box Cubs are given an opportunity to look inside Pharaoh’s jewellery box. They then have to write down or draw what they have remembered. It can be played as a Six or individually.
