```text
The Unnatural Trail
Place 12–15 everyday objects along a short outdoor trail.
Some should be obvious, while others should be partly
concealed among plants, rocks or trees. Cubs walk the trail
silently in small groups and count how many unnatural
objects they can spot. They must not point at, touch or
reveal the objects to others. At the end, each group quietly
reports its total and describes what it saw. Walk the trail
together afterwards to reveal the objects.
```
