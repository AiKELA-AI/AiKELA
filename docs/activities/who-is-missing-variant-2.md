# Who is missing?

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Blindfolds

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Who is missing? Have the Cubs sit in a circle. They should cover their eyes with their scarves or blindfolds. Once they have all covered their eyes have one Cub leave the room. Can the Cubs say who has left the room?
