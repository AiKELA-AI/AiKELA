# Snakes Alive-O!

## Type

SENSE TRAINING

## Equipment

Blindfolds/scarves

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Snakes Alive-O! Pack in two teams – half the Pack sitting on the floor are snakes hissing, the rest of the Pack are Explorers. The snake team sits in a given area allotted by the leader – the Explorers are blindfolded, a couple at a time, and must pass through the snake infested area. They are guided by the Explorers who are not blindfolded. As soon as the Explorers start to guide their fellows through the area, the snakes commence to hiss as loudly as possible to drown out the directions.
