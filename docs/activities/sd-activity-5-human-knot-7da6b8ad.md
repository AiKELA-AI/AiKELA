```text
Activity 5: Human Knot
First attempt in their sixes, then in 2 teams and final the whole pack.
Ask everyone to raise their right hand and reach into the center of the circle. Grab the hand of someone standing across or opposite from you. You cannot grab the hand of the person directly next to you
 Ask everyone to raise their left hand, reach into the center, and grab the hand of a different person
 Ensure every single person is holding hands with two different people, and that no one is holding hands with their immediate neighbors
Work together to untangle the human knot and form one large, unbroken circle of people holding hands.
```
