# Poisons in the Home — Discussion and Yes/No Review

## Type

Work session with movement-based revision. Source slot: 15 minutes. **Needs teaching material and an attachment before use as a poisons session.**

## Equipment

Source lists a “Poison sheet” and “Different types of poisons”. The sheet was not supplied. **Scouter adaptation:** use pictures or clean, safe props, not hazardous substances. Allow space to step or jump safely.

## Instructions

Reused source outline:

1. Discuss what poisons are and what to do if someone takes a poison. The source names this topic but supplies no treatment instructions or teaching script.
2. Have Cubs stand in a line and ask yes/no revision questions.
3. For “yes”, Cubs jump forward; for “no”, they jump backwards.
4. If many Cubs give an incorrect answer, explain the correct answer before continuing.

The source supplies neither the questions nor an answer key. Obtain current authoritative teaching material and prepare/check questions before running the session. Do not infer treatment advice from this activity outline.

## Safety and variations

**Scouter guidance added during extraction:** Use only harmless props or pictures. Keep the movement space clear, with room behind the line; offer a step, pointing or seated yes/no response instead of jumping.

**Reusable mechanic:** The same yes/no movement review can reinforce another subject already taught in the meeting. Credit this source, name the changed subject and supply checked questions and answers in the meeting plan. This is an adaptation of the review mechanic, not a verified poisons lesson.
