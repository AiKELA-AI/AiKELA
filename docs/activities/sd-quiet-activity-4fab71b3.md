First aid response 
• One person got burnt (SW) 
• One person got a gash (Long deep cut_ on their leg (GW)
• One person got stung by a bee (GW)
• And Jojo got a nose bleed (SW)
Each group is assigned one emergency situation to discuss and present to the Pack: 
Step 1: Stage the Injury – Create a visual wound on your injured Cub using the provided art supplies. (5min)
 Step 2: Plan Your Response – Silver Wolf (SW) Cubs create a short play, while Gold Wolf (GW) Cubs write and perform a skit or song demonstrating the correct first aid response. (5mn)
Step 3: Include Required Details: Use the 24-hour clock to state when the incident happened, how quickly help arrived, and the total treatment time. Incorporate the Figure of Eight knot (SW requirement) or the Sheepshank knot (GW requirement) into your medical scenario. (5min)
