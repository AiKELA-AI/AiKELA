# Reef-knot Bandages

## Type

Work session / paired knotting practice. Source slot: 15 minutes. This is a practice activity, not treatment instructions.

## Equipment

Clean practice bandages suitable for tying a reef knot; space for pairs and a Scouter's demonstration.

## Instructions

Reused from Mrs Knot's House, with wording organised for a Scouter:

1. Demonstrate tying a reef knot.
2. Pair an older/more experienced Cub with a younger Cub; the source specifically pairs Gold Wolf and Silver Wolf Cubs. One is the pretend patient and one the first aider.
3. Call out a suitable body part for practice. The first aider places the practice bandage and secures it with a reef knot.
4. When finished, the Cub signals by placing a hand on their head so the Scouter can check progress.
5. Give everyone a turn in each role; repeat with another suitable practice location.

## Safety and adaptation

**Scouter guidance added during extraction:** Supervise all bandaging. Keep ties loose, easy to release and comfortable; stop immediately if uncomfortable. Do not bind the neck, face or head, restrict movement, or apply tight pressure as part of a game. This entry does not teach treatment of bleeding or injuries. Use a rolled towel as a practice patient if a Cub prefers not to be bandaged.

**Theme variation:** Keep the demonstration and patient/first-aider pairing, and change only the fictional setting (for example, a campsite first-aid practice station). Credit this source and distinguish first-aid teaching from knot practice.
