SW: “Home Alone Hero” Role-Play Cards (Silver Wolves, ages 7–8)
Cubs draw scenario cards (stranger knocks, phone rings, etc.) and act out the safe response using simple picture prompts. Practice naming 3 trusted adults and what to say to a stranger at the door.

GW: “Safe or Not Safe?” Digital Dilemma Cards (Gold Wolves, ages 9–10)
Scenario cards: online friend asks for your address, someone sends a mean message, a pop-up asks for your name. In pairs, decide: Safe or Not Safe? Why? Introduce terms: cyberbullying, identity theft, social network. Groups share reasoning with Akela.
