# Big Birdie Song

## Type

Singing

## Equipment

Not stated in this attachment.

## Instructions

Way up in the sky (jump high)

Where the big birdies fly (flap arms like wings)

While down in the nest (form a nest with arms)

The little birdies rest (hands next to head like sleeping)

Shhh! Shhh! THEY’RE SLEEPING! THEY’RE SLEEPING! (stage whisper shh! shout they’re sleeping)

The bright sun comes up (jump high with arms above head)

The dew goes away (hands like banging on a keyboard)

Good morning, good morning the little birdies say (raise arms up and down)
