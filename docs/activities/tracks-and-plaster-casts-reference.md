# Who Goes There? — Tracks and Plaster Casts

## Type

Nature observation/casting outline; 60 minutes

## Equipment

Plaster of Paris, containers and actual tracks/leaves.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Find animal/bird tracks, try to identify them and make casts using Plaster of Paris; leaves may also be cast. The source provides no mixing ratio, release method or identification key. Preserve the activity outline and obtain a complete sourced casting method before use.

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Do not handle unidentified animal remains or disturb wildlife.
