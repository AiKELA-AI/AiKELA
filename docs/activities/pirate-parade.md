# PIRATE PARADE

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Suitable music (optional)

## Instructions

PIRATE PARADE Cubs to come dressed as sailors who, by the end of the meeting will become Pirates! Before boarding ship, the Pirate Captain needs to have a good look at his/her Sailors. Cubs parade around in their sailor costumes and the Pirate Captain (the Leader/s) chooses the best dressed sailor.
