```text
The story of Kim
Baden-Powell’s believed that Kim, the main character in a story his friend Rudyard
Kipling wrote, was a good example of what a scout could be. One of Kim’s skills was
that he was incredibly observant. This is Kim’s story.
A long time ago in India there was a boy named Kimball O'Hara, but everyone called
him Kim. He grew up on the streets of Lahore, clever and quick-eyed, always
watching, always learning. One of the things he was called by the locals was, “"Little
friend of all the world.” He was a friend to all, just like a scout (and cub) should be.
One day Kim met a spy trainer called Lurgan Sahib. He trained Kim and his test was
simple: objects on a tray, covered, and you had to remember every single one. Kim
trained and trained until his memory became razor-sharp. He became one of the
greatest secret agents in the land.
```
