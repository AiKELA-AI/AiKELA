# Flag Fiasco — Parts, Puzzle and Sheet Bend

## Type

Flag skills station; 10 minutes per rotation

## Equipment

Flags, parts-of-a-flag labels, SA flag puzzle and rope/halyard.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Lay out a flag. Cubs work together placing parts labels; older Cubs explain parts to younger Cubs. Gold Cubs practise a sheet bend on the flag or a rope with a bight; younger Cubs assemble a South African flag puzzle. The original spelling “bite” is not a new knot instruction. A leader must already know and demonstrate the correct knot.
