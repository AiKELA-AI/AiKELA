```text
Step 1: Prepare the Bottle
• Turn the top half of the bottle upside down (like a funnel)
• Place it into the bottom half or into a cup
Explain: “This will act like our filter system.”
Step 2: Add the First Layer (Cloth/Cotton)
• Place cloth, cotton wool, or a coffee filter at the bottom
(the bottle neck)
Explain:
“This layer stops the smallest dirt from passing through.”
Step 3: Add Sand Layer
• Pour in a layer of fine sand
Explain:
“Sand catches tiny particles we can’t easily see.”
Step 4: Add Small Stones
• Add a layer of small pebbles or gravel
Explain:
“These trap bigger bits of dirt and debris.”
Step 5: Add Larger Stones (Top Layer)
• Add larger stones at the top
Explain:
“This helps catch large pieces like leaves and litter first.”
Step 6: Pour Dirty Water Slowly
• Carefully pour dirty water into the top
Important:
Go slowly to avoid mixing the layers
Step 7: Watch the Water Drip Through
• Observe as water filters into the container
Ask Cubs:
• “Is it clearer?”
• “What changed?”
Always say: “This water may look cleaner, but it is NOT safe to
drink.”
Discussion Questions
• Which layer worked the best?
• What got trapped at the top vs bottom?
• Is it easy or hard to clean water?
```
