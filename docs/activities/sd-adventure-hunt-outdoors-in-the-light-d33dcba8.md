Adventure Hunt — outdoors, in the light
Six stops. At each one there are four pages pegged up
— Yellow, Green, Blue and Red. A Six reads its own
colour only.
Every Six walks a different route, so the four colours
at a stop carry four difef rent clues and nobody can
follow anybody.
Every Cub takes a tag at every stop, so every child
ends the hunt with all six words in their own hand.
The answer: GET OUT · STAY OUT · CALL 112. The
words are not in walking order, so every Six has to
solve it.
Finish line is the hall centre (Hathi). First Six back
with a full set of tags AND the sentence wins.
