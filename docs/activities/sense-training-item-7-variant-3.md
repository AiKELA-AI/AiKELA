## Type

## Instructions

SENSE TRAINING: Three Jolly Sailors The Pack is divided into two; six or seven Cubs one end of the room, rest of the Pack at the other end. The smaller number choose the type of workmen they are to be and then advance towards the other Cubs, saying: “Three (or four or five) jolly Sailors come to look for work.” Pack: What can you do? Answer: All sorts. Pack: Show me one. The Cubs then act whatever they have chosen, e.g. driving in a nail, cleaning the deck, typing, etc. Pack allowed three guesses and as they guess they chase actors; if it is a wrong guess they return and act again.
