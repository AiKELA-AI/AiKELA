```text
Activities : Machine Challenge
Cubs to complete the various challenges. They can
divide into smaller groups if needed and do each
challenge separately.
Lever – Lift the Load
Place a heavy box or bucket on the floor. Give Cubs a
broomstick and a wooden block as the fulcrum. They
have to lift the load without picking it up. Move the
fulcrum closer to and farther from the load so they
discover that its position changes how much effort is
needed.
Key idea: A lever lets us move a heavy load using less
effort.
Examples: seesaw, bottle opener, crowbar, scissors.
Pulley – Rescue the Supplies
Run rope through a pulley or over a pole attached to
something secure or even a tree branch. Cubs lift a
bucket containing beanbags or another small weight. If
possible, let them first lift the bucket directly and then
with the pulley.
Key idea: A pulley changes the direction of the force
and can make lifting easier.
Inclined Plane – Mountain Supply Run
Place one end of a plank on a chair or sturdy box. Cubs
race cars down the ramp. Change the angle to see
what effect it has (cars go faster/further if incline is
higher).
Key idea: A ramp lets us use less force over a longer
distance.
Examples: wheelchair ramp, stairs, loading ramp, slide.
Screw – Up We Go
Give each Cub a large bolt and nut. Race to see who
can move the nut from one end to the other. Then show
that the thread is essentially an inclined plane wrapped
around a cylinder. If you have wood and appropriate
supervision, Cubs can also drive a screw into soft wood
with a screwdriver. The nut and bolts can be done as a
relay race.
```
