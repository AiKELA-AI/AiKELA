```text
LEARN SAFE WAYS BY MAKING A PLAN:
Run through the PLAN with the Cubs to reinforce the
importance of having this in place and refer to the ‘be
water safe’ facilitator manual.
P – prepare/pack: ask the Cubs what they think they
would need for a day at the beach or swimming pool.
L – look: ask the Cubs what they need to look out for
at the beach or near water – look for dangers.
A – ask an adult: ask the Cubs why it is important ask
for permission to go swimming. What else do they need
to tell a responsible adult?
N – never: ask the Cubs to tell you what the safety
rules are.
```
