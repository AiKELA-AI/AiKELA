```text
Silver Wolves: The Mad Hatters Mixed-up Objects
Task: Identify a simple object viewed from the side and the top.
"Oh no, Cubs! The Mad Hatter hosted a chaotic tea party, and Wonderland is
completely topsy-turvy! All of his favorite belongings have been mixed up, filpped
upside down, and spun around. Some are seen from the side, and some are seen
from the top! The Hatter needs the clever Silver Wolves to use their eagle eyes and
sharp memories to match the objects up and restore order before the Queen of
Hearts arrives!"
The Setup
1. Divide the Silver Wolves into two equal teams and have them stand in two
parallel lines facing the playing area.
2. Shufefl all 20 cards (10 matching pairs).
3. Lay the cards out face down on the fol or in a neat grid (e.g., 4 rows of 5
cards).
How to Play
1. Team 1 goes frist. One designated player from Team 1 runs up and filps over
any card to reveal the object.
2. That same player filps over a second card, trying to fni d its matching
perspective (e.g., matching the side-view teacup with the top-view teacup).
3. If it’s a match: The player takes both cards back to their team’s pile. Team 1
scores a point.
4. If it’s NOT a match: The player filps both cards face down exactly where they
found them. Turn passes to Team 2.
5. The game continues back and forth until all 10 pairs have been collected.
6. The team with the most matching pairs wins!
The objects cards are:
1. Tea cup
2. Top hat
3. Key
4. Rose
5. Bottle
6. Cake
7. Mushroom
8. Pocket watch
9. Flamingo
10. Playing Card
```
