```text
DEMONSTRATION:
Have two Cubs step up to the front. Have one stand on
a step or something higher, and the other standing
opposite on the ground. Use this time to demonstrate
one child needing assistance in the water (the one on
the ground) and the other helping from the shore/bank
(the one on the step). The Cub ‘in the water’ then pulls
the other slowly off the step and they are now both ‘in
the water’. Explain that the step is the bank of the
river/lake/dam and when you try to help with your hand
you have very little balance and are at risk of falling into
the water. Highlight that the drowning Cub (victim) will
use all their strength to stay above water and
unintentionally push the other (the Cub that was pulled
off the shore) underwater.
Fear will set in for the second Cub, and they react in the
same manner thus pushing the first Cub back under
water. This see-saw action will continue until one Cub
can no longer get back above water and it's at this point
that one or both will drown.
```
