Leaping Wolf Presentation — Josie
Pack sits in a horseshoe on the fol or. Josie has the
fol or for her Leaping Wolf presentation, then takes
questions from the Cubs.
Leaders sit down with the Pack rather than hovering.
Sixers are responsible for their Six listening — brief
them at inspection.
Have two questions ready in case the Pack goes
quiet, and make them specific to what she has done
rather than "did you enjoy it".
Finish with a proper cheer. A leader notes the date in
her record book on the night.
Running late? Take the time out of the discussion,
not out of Josie’s ten minutes and not out of the
bases.
