```text
Activity: The Great Flag Recall Challenge
How it Works:
The Study Phase (Look & Learn):
Show the cubs the visual flag diagram with all parts labeled (Toggle, Fly, Hoist, Breadth/Width, Rope, and Length).
Give each Six 60 seconds to study the diagram, memorize the locations, and plan their strategy together.
The Memory Sprint:
Hide the diagram!
Hand each Six a stack of 6 unplaced label cards and a strip of blue-tack/tape.
On the whistle, the cubs race over to their physical Pack flag laid flat on the ground or table.
Labeling the Flag:
Working completely from memory, team members must place each label on its matching part:
Toggle: The small wooden/plastic pin near the top.
Fly: The outer edge that waves in the wind.
Hoist: The vertical edge closest to the rope/pole.
Breadth / Width: The top-to-bottom height line of the flag.
Rope: The line (halyard) used to pull the flag up.
Length: The front-to-back horizontal measurement of the flag.
Scouter Inspection:
Once all 6 labels are stuck down, the Sixer shouts "Flag Complete!"
Scouters check the placements. Award 1 point per correct label, plus a 2-point speed bonus for the first Six to get all 6 parts 100% correct!
```
