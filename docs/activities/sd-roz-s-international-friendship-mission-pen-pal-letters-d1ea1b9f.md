Roz's International Friendship Mission – Pen-Pal Letters
Cubs sit in their Sixes and Akela briefyl explains what Pen-Pals are.
Pack! Roz is excited to learn that there are Cubs all around the world and she wants
us to get to know them better, and to do that, she’d like us to make the frist move.
Your mission is to write a letter introducing yourself to a mystery Cub friend
somewhere else in the world. Tell them a little about yourself, your Six and what you
enjoy doing — and then ask them some questions so that you can get to know them
too.
