# Aqua Scope

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

plastic milk bottles; scissors; clear plastic, waterproof tape

## Instructions

Equipment: plastic milk bottles; scissors; clear plastic, waterproof tape

Cut the bottom out of the bottle. Cover it with clear plastic and secure with tape. Put the part covered with the plastic in the water and look through the other end to see what is under the water.

If you don’t have a pond of dam nearby, you can use buckets of water into which you have dropped a number of different items for the Cubs to look at.
