## Equipment

## Instructions

ACTIVITY: Thumbprints Use childproof/waterproof ink to make thumbprint (fingerprint) pictures. This is easier to clean off the fingers. You can also use water based paint. Touch finger onto the pad of ink (or paint). If using paint... dab into paint, touch finger on paper towel to remove excess paint, then press finger onto drawing paper.
