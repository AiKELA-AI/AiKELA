● Challenge 1 The Queen's Secret Characters(+/5 min)
The Queen has hidden the names of 10 Jungle Book characters!Your job is to
match each animal picture to its correct Jungle Book character name to decipher
the code and unlock the palace door.
● Challenge 2: The Room of Danger -Escape the Hazards (+/-10 min)
The palace door opens into a room full of dangers! The Queen has left hazards
everywhere, and the Cubs must identify them before they can escape.
Set up a room of hazards for the cubs to identify and say what they should do to
prevent accidents from happening
● Challenge 3 – The Cheshire Cat's Disappearing Directions Escape the
Courtyard (+/-10 min)
You have fni ally reached the courtyard, but the Queen has another demand!
“My bed must face the sunrise! Fix the compass points they keep disappearing.
The Cheshire Cat has made the compass points disappear! Can you remember
where they belong?"
- Place all eight cards in their correct positions around the courtyard.
- Cubs study them for 30 seconds.
- Everyone closes their eyes while the leader removes two cards.
- Cubs must identify which directions are missing and stand where those cards
belong.
- Continue removing cards until none remain.
- Ask each cub which compass point they are
- Put the compass back together and figure out which way the queen's bed must
face!
● Challenge 4 – The Queen's Knotty mess - Escape the Palace Grounds (+/-10 min)
You have reached the final gate, but it is tied shut with the Queens' ropes!
To escape, you must demonstrate two knots:
- Slip reef knot
- Figure-of-eight knot
