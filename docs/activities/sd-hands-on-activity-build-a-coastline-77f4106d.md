```text
Hands-On Activity – “Build a Coastline”
National Challenge: Task 1.1 Flood in sandpit
SW - task 18 what does conservation mean.
Purpose
● Understand erosion and deposition
● Encourage creativity and teamwork
Setup
● Each Six makes a mountain out of sand/soil.
Ask them to:
○ Create a coastline
○ Add hills, bays, beaches
Instructions
1. Once built, leader announces
“The waves arrive!”
2. Cubs gently:
○ Drip water onto the coast
○ Observe what happens
3. Ask:
○ “Where did the sand go?”
○ “What changed?”
Safety
● No throwing sand
● Gentle water only
After the experiment - talk about what is nature
conservation? Nature conservation also includes
protecting our shores from sand erosion. Yes, we
can not stop the wind or sea but we can plant
plants that help the sand stay in place. We can
not play on sand dunes. We could also put up
shade cloth to protect the sand from moving.
```
