# My Bonnie lies over the ocean

## Type

Singing

## Equipment

Words

## Instructions

My Bonnie lies over the ocean,

My Bonnie lies over the sea,

My Bonnie lies over the ocean,

Oh bring back my Bonnie to me.

Bring back, oh bring back,

Oh bring back my Bonnie to me, to me. (Repeat last two lines)

Action: As you sing each word with the letter B, change from a standing to a sitting position and vice versa. All should be standing at the end of the song. When you have mastered these movements, sing it again faster.
