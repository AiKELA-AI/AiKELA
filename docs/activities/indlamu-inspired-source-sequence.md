# Indlamu-labelled Dance — Source Movement Sequence

## Type

Dance work session; source slot 25 minutes

## Equipment

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

The source labels this an easy Indlamu/Zulu dance sequence: march on the spot eight times; clap four times; lift the right knee and gently stamp four times; repeat on the left; step right and clap; step left and clap; raise arms and turn slowly; finish arms raised. Cultural authenticity of this simplified sequence is not independently verified. Preserve it as the source’s labelled movement exercise, not an authoritative traditional choreography.
