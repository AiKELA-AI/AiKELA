# The Wheels of the Bus

## Type

Singing

## Equipment

Words of song

## Instructions

The wheels on the bus go round and round, round and round, round and round. The wheels on the bus go round and round, all through the town.

Substitute these also:

The doors on the bus go open and shut. The bell on the bus goes ding-ding-ding. The lady on the bus says, "Get off my feet"... The people on the bus say, "We had a nice ride"...

**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

SINGING: The Wheels of the Bus
