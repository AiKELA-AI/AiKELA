```text
Play together Stay Together

Give each Cub a piece of cardboard. Cubs have to stand in a line and start the ball rolling down the end of the line. Once the ball has gone through the first piece of cardboard that child runs to the other end of the line and joins with their cardboard. See how far around the hall it goes before the ball falls off the end.
```
