```text
Activity Base 2 (SW53, colour flag, GW57, parts of flag)
The two sixes will sit in their own groups on one side of the
hall, the scouters will be on the other.
The scouters have the coloured bottle caps and the
unlabelled flag diagrams.
The sixes have a labelled flag diagram.
The goal is to build a south african flag out of bottle caps.
In order to claim a cap a cub must run up to the leader and
name the "randomly" selected flag part, if they do not know
they can run back and get the answer from their diagram.
Seniors will help with reading as needed.
If the game needs to speed up, allow more than one cap per
answer after a while.
Note time and judge flags for bones close to the end.
```
