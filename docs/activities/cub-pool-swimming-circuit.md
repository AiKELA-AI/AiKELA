# Cub Pool Swimming Circuit — Source Programme

## Type

Water-safety briefing, four swimming stations and rescue-method practice. Source: 10-minute opening base, 40-minute rotation (nine minutes per base plus one minute to change) and 14-minute closing base. Requires a qualified swimming leader's assessment and venue-specific arrangements before use.

## Equipment

Suitable supervised pool; timer; sinking objects; staves; rope and empty 2 litre plastic bottles. The source includes its pool layout and station positions; these are specific to Pinelands North School, not a generic safe layout.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused source circuit, with preparation limits made explicit; no swimming or diving technique has been generated.

1. Opening base: discuss safety near water, local swimming hazards, the buddy system and safe-swimming rules. The source names these topics but does not supply a complete teaching script.
2. The four source stations are: a racing dive and 50 m using a racing stroke; tread water for one minute and float on the back for 30 seconds; swim 50 m using a different stroke; duck dive and collect an object from the deep section. These are the source's tasks and measurements, not verified SSA requirements or a prescribed sequence for every Cub.
3. Closing base: the source names reaching with a stave and throwing a rope attached to a floating plastic bottle. It supplies no complete teaching method for either rescue technique.

**Planning status:** Keep as a source-based circuit outline. A qualified leader must establish ability groups, appropriate depths, permissible diving, safe equipment and actual instructions before any station is scheduled. Do not treat the four written tasks as permission for all Cubs to enter deep water or dive.

## Preparation and safety

Use the actual pool's rules and qualified supervision. Check each Cub's swimming ability and provide appropriate alternatives from verified swimming material. No one attempts a real rescue or unsupervised deep-water activity on the strength of this outline. Keep the source's local leader names and pool allocation out of another Pack's plan.

## Advancement / badge link

The source calls this “Cub National Challenge 2026 — Be Water Safe”. That challenge credit, any swimming-badge link and all task thresholds remain unverified. Do not silently replace the source's 50 m with another requirement or assume that completing the circuit awards a badge. Check the full applicable official SSA material before assessment.
