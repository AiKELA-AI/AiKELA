# Incy Wincy Spider

## Type

Singing

## Equipment

Polystyrene fruit packaging; Scissors; Paint; Paint brush; 4 black pipe cleaners – sticks will do; Adhesive tape or masking tape; Transparent red beads; Glue; Darning needle; String

## Instructions

You will need:

Polystyrene fruit packaging

Scissors

Paint

Paint brush

4 black pipe cleaners – sticks will do

Adhesive tape or masking tape

Transparent red beads

Glue

Darning needle

String

Instructions:

Cut out one of the round shapes from the fruit packaging and paint it black. Allow to dry.

Attach four black pipe cleaners to the underside of the body with adhesive or masking tape to form eight legs on either side of the body. Bend the ends.

Attach two red beads for the eyes.

Use the darning needle to make a hole in the centre of the body. Knot one end of the string and thread the string through the hole.

Suspend Incy Wincy Spider from the roof.

Tips:

Use egg boxes instead of fruit packaging. The spider will be smaller.

Apply glue to the body and sprinkle dried rooibos tea leaves over for a hairy look.

As 10 minutes is all that is allowed for this activity, we suggest the spider’s bodies are pre-cut and pre-painted. All the Cubs need do is assemble their spider.
