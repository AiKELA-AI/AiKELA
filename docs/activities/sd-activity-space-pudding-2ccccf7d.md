```text
Activity – Space Pudding
Give each Cub a small Ziploc bag and put the contents of half
a box of instant pudding into it, then add sufficient milk and
seal the bag PROPERLY. The Cubs squish their bags around
until all the contents are mixed properly. During the Yarn (or at
the end of the meeting) cut the corner of the bag and let the
Cubs suck out the contents.
```
