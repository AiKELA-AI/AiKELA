# Floor Collage

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Sheets of paper to cover an area of floor; Coloured sand; Flour; Crushed coloured chalk; Fine pebbles; Shells; Pulses (dried peas/legumes); Any brightly coloured material/paper; Etc.; Broom to sweep up the mess afterwards!; Gather together the above articles (and any others) and spread paper over the floor. Allow the Cubs to work together to create a picture or pattern of their choice. Enjoy the results.
