```text
10-YEAR-OLDS: STRANDED DISTRESS CHALLENGE
Scenario: You are stranded. There is no mobile phone and the torch is no longer available. A rescue party is looking for
 you. Demonstrate ways to attract attention.
 Make whistles, pots and pans, wooden spoons and the red/orange sheets available without immediately explaining their
 uses.
 Cubs should devise an audible distress signal and construct a large ground SOS with the sheets. Count the earlier
 torch/mirror signal as the third demonstrated method.
 Prompt only if needed: What could someone hear from far away? What could an aircraft see? What could you make
 much bigger than yourself?
 Ensure each older Cub personally demonstrates three distress-signalling methods.
```
