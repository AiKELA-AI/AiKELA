```text
Listen Like an Owl
Prepare short recordings of these three distinctive bird calls:
1. Hadeda ibis
2. Laughing dove
3. Grey go-away-bird
Play each recording loudly enough for the whole Pack to
hear, but keep the phone screen hidden.
Instructions
1. Gather the Cubs quietly and ask them to close their
eyes.
2. Introduce each bird by playing its call and then revealing
its name and picture.
3. Replay all three calls in a different order.
4. Cubs raise their hands when they recognise a call.
5. The first Cub to identify it correctly earns a bone for
their Six.
6. Finish with a memory round by playing all three calls
consecutively. The first Six to give the complete correct
order earns a bonus bone.
Remind the Cubs that they must listen silently until the
recording has finished and may make only one guess per
call.
```
