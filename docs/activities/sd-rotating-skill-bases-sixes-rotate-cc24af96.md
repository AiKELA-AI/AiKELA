```text
Rotating Skill Bases (Sixes rotate
every 10 mins):
Base 1: Screwdrivers & Fasteners
Practice driving screws into soft wood
with pre-started holes. Practice
identifying flathead vs. Phillips screws
and tightening loose hinges on test
boards.
Base 2: Hammer & Precision Nailing
Use small claw hammers to tap nails
into scrap timber. Use a peg or comb to
hold the nail in place safely.
Base 3: Sanding & Tool Maintenance
Sand rough wooden edges using
sanding blocks (coarse to fine grit).
Practice wiping down tools and oiling
hinges/pliers.
```
