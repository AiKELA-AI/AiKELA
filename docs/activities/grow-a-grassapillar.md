# Grow a Grassapillar!

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

potting soil (or plain soil); grass seed; small bowl for mixing; cardboard egg carton; scissors; felt-tip pen; dish

## Instructions

Equipment:

potting soil (or plain soil)

grass seed

small bowl for mixing

cardboard egg carton

scissors

felt-tip pen

dish

Instructions:

Mix ¾ cup of potting soil and 2 tablespoons of grass seed in a bowl. Add water to moisten the mixture and then set it aside.

Cut away a three-cup section of an egg carton, then trim the section along the longer edges so your grassapillar will look the same from both sides. With permanent marker, draw a face on one end. Gently spoon the seed mixture into the cups.

Set your creation on a dish in a brightly lit spot and keep the soil moist. The grass should sprout in about a week.
