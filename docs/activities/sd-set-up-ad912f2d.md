```text
Set-up
4 clear jars:
A - Tap water - clear
B - Muddy water - soil mixed in
C - Water with leaves/grit - visibly dirty
D - “Stream water” - completely clear, but labelled as collected from the
Wonderland Stream
2 Jars of Jam
1. Jam marked Warning Extremely Tart
2. Jam label says made in a kitchen with dirty hands and unwashed fruit
Also give them a large red heart printed on white card.
Their challenge
Tell them:
The Caterpillar wants to know which water is cleanest. But in Wonderland,
appearances can be deceiving…
They investigate each jar without tasting it and answer the following questions
For each sample they:
1. Look at the colour
2. Look for fol ating particles
3. Smell it from a safe distance rather than putting their nose over the container
4. Put the jar over the small red heart - how clearly can they see through it to see
the heart?
5. Rank the samples from cleanest-looking to dirtiest-looking.
Then ask:
Which one would you drink?
Cubs must put them on a scale from I would drink this water - I won’t drink this at
all
Give them the Caterpillars secret clue
● Whooo said you can see everything that's in water?” Some microorganisms are
far too small to see. Clear water can still contain germs that can make you
sick.
● Make sure to read the label of the jam - youuuu wouldn’t want to miss a clue
```
