| Yarn: | The Collie proved proof |
| --- | --- |

After many years of farming, a farmer retired and passed the farm onto his son. The young man knew everything about tractors, harvesters and other modern machinery, but would have nothing to do with dogs. He believed that they were just a nuisance and he could bring the sheep in anyway without a dog. When a neighbouring farmer also retired, he offered his collie to the young farmer. The young man accepted the dog although he did not want it and because he did not want to offend the old man.

The dog took up his duties and was very good at bringing the sheep in every night. The first week everything went very well – at the same time each day the collie would trot off, round up the sheep, and bring them safely home. On the 8th day, however, the sheep arrived back but not the collie. The farmer, with bad grace, eventually went to look for the collie and found him sitting beside a sheep which was on its back and unable to right itself. The dog had shown the young man just how trustworthy he was.

From then on the man respected and began to love the dog who followed him everywhere.
