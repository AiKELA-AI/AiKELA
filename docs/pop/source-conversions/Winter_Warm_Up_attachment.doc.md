| Yarn: | Freddy to the Rescue |
| --- | --- |

Freddy had always been afraid of heights and even at eleven, he still had the same problem. Walking to the shop for his mother one day he came across a fire. He saw the firemen in action; heard people yelling from an upstairs window; saw firemen putting up a ladder with a sort of cabin which they sent up with a fireman and brought people down. Nobody saw a little face at another window. A brown dog, looking very frightened. Everybody seemed too busy to notice the dog – they were treating the people who had been rescued. Freddy pointed him out to one of the firemen, who swung the ladder round and said he’d go up on the ladder himself. He started raising the ladder and when it was in position a falling beam hit him in the chest. There was no way out. Freddy decided to rush up the ladder himself, without thinking how frightened he was. Luckily the dog was calm, and small enough to fit under his arm. Only when he got down again did he realise what he had done. He no longer has a fear of heights.
