| Activity: | Telecommunications |
| --- | --- |

2 paper or yoghurt cups

2 shirt buttons

A ball of string

A sharp skewer

![Source image](assets/Music_to_my_ears_attachment.doc/Music_to_my_ears_attachment_html_249c82b4.png)

Make a small hole in the bottom of each carton with the skewer (it may be easier if the tip of the skewer is heated in a candle flame, if using a yoghurt cup). Thread the string through the hole and tie the button on the inside of the carton. Now stand at a distance from a friend who listens into his/her carton while you talk into yours.

| Yarn: | The Bees’ Drum |
| --- | --- |

The bees loved to dance. The rhythm of the drum drove them wild and they could dance for days on end. So when they got a new drum, the bees had a huge celebration. They hummed and drummed, making so much noise that they attracted the attention of other animals in the area.

Soon a group of animals had gathered to watch and listen to the bees. The leopard, the elephant, the warthog, the gnu, the ostrich, the giraffe, the tortoise and the snake were all there. As they listened, they began to want the drum for themselves so that they could have a party and dance like the bees.

The leopard turned to the rest of the group and said, “Why don’t I try and steal the drum? Most creatures are frightened of me.”

So saying, he leapt into the centre of the dancing bees. Within seconds, he had turned and fled, yelping, with a swarm of bees after him. “Ouch! Oww! That stings – help!” he cried, as he disappeared.

The other animals didn’t want to try after that because, as you know, a bee sting can be very painful. Finally, the tortoise said he would do it. He went up to the bees and asked if he could play the drum.

He turned out to be a brilliant drummer and the bees were enjoying themselves until, suddenly, the music stopped. The bees looked round but the drum was nowhere to be seen. In fact, the tortoise had hidden it under his shell. As he slowly ambled away, the bees started to suspect that somehow he had stolen it. They tried to sting him. But, of course, a tortoise has a hard shell and stings could not hurt him.

That evening, the tortoise invited the animals to a party. There, in pride of place, was the bees’ drum. The tortoise took it up and beat a rhythm that had the animals dancing all night.

Moral: It’s no use trying to do something unless you are properly prepared. Be like the tortoise, not the Leopard.
