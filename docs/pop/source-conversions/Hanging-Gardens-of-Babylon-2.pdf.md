some wheelbarrows?


         10   TEAM GAME: Wheelbarrow Race                            None

              Cubs to line up in size order - tallest to shortest.

They needed to plant seeds as well …

         10   RELAY GAME: Plant the Seeds                              Popcorn (or
                                                                       similar), empty
              In Sixes, the Cubs need to line up and, one at a         egg tray
              time, run to the seeds, pick up just one seed,
              'plant' it in the tray, run back to your Six and the
              next Cub goes.        Each seed needs to be in a
              separate 'hole' - once all the holes are full, the Six
              needs to sit quietly.


We sometimes get some frogs in our garden -they are good for our garden because they
keep insects out that are bad for the plants - they can be noisy though!

         10   CIRCLE GAME: Chase the frogs!                            None

              Pack in a circle. They are spaced well apart and
              bend over. Two Cubs are outside, one chasing the
              other. When the chased Cub is tired they leap over
              the back of another Cub in the circle, who then
              becomes the chased.


Sometimes when the queen was sitting in the garden, she would run her hands through the
sand – and guess what she found?

         10   KIMS GAME: touch (in sand).                              15 items,
                                                                       containers of
              Place up to 15 different items in a container filled     sand, cloth
              with sand or similar. One container per Six – cover
              the container with a cloth or similar. One at a time
              the Cubs will come up and are told what they need
              to find in the container. They need to place their
              hands under the cloth and feel around in the
              container until they think they have found the
              requested article. After the first Cub has had a
              turn, the next lot will try their luck. Continue until
              all the items have been identified even if the Cubs
              go more than once.
