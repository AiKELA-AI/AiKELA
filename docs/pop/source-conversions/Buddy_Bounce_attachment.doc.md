| Activity: | Popcorn |
| --- | --- |

Equipment: popcorn; oil; tin foil; teaspoon; tablespoon; string; salt/margarine; long stick; fire; bucket with sand/water

In the centre of about 40cm square of heavy or doubled foil, place one teaspoon of oil and one tablespoon of popcorn. Bring the foil corners together to make a pouch. Seal the edges by folding, but allow room for the popcorn to pop. Tie each pouch to a long stick with string and hold the pouch over the hot coals. Shake constantly until all the corn has popped. Season with salt and margarine.
