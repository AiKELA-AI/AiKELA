The Lighthouse was put up to ward off any emergency which may occur if a ship had
to crash on the rocks – are you ready for an emergency?

         05   INSPECTION: Emergency Kit


The Lighthouse of Alexandria was built because of the dangerous sailing conditions -
can you miss the rocks and get safely to the island?

         05   STEAM RELEASE: Safe island                              Chairs, newspaper,
                                                                      whistle
              Cubs to run around - when the whistle blows they
              are to run for a safe island (pieces of paper). After
              a couple of turns, remove one piece of newspaper
              at a time to make it more challenging. Have chairs
              scattered around the playing area to act as rocks -
              these need to be avoided.


Oh no - what happens if the ship hits the rocks? It will capsize!

         10   ACTIVE GAME: Life Boat (ladders)                        None

              Players sit opposite each other, with their legs out
              straight and feet touching. They are in the ship
              and it is capsizing. Each player is numbered off. If
              their number is called, the Cubs need to stand up
              and race to the end of the row, stepping in
              between legs. Then run back around the outside,
              and up through the middle to sit in their place
              again and they are now in the lifeboat and safe.


Looks like there may be a problem - we did not see the lighthouse so have crashed
onto the rocks! Into the life rafts …

              sharks and you want to make sure that everyone
              survives. Squeeze the entire group/team onto a

              the raft without anyone falling off. The game can
              be quite challenging and involves a lot of squeezing
              together and careful folding of the blanket.


We definitely need a lighthouse to make sure we don’t crash into the rocks again!
Lighthouses are lovely and keep the ships safe by warning them of possible dangers
by showing their comforting light. In the old days, these lights were just a big fire as
there was no electricity ... so first we need to make sure we know about making a fire
safely and correctly....

         15   WIDE GAME: Fire Making                                      Strips of paper,
                                                                          envelopes, glue,
              Cut out the strips separately and put all the same          paper
              strips into an envelope – e.g. All the ‘grid’ strips go
              together, etc. Scouters will have one or more
              envelopes with the strips inside them. Cubs to
              work in Sixes or small groups. The Cubs need to
              run around and ask the Scouters for the strips in
              order – i.e. Need open space before anything else.
              Once they have the open space, they then need to
              get rocks/bricks, etc. Scouters can swap the strips
              around to make the game more fun and interesting
              (Vanishing Post-box style). As the Cubs get the
              correct strip, it should be stuck down onto a piece
              of paper to form a pyramid. They need to carry on
              until all the strips have been obtained in the correct
              order and stuck down on the paper. First Six to
              complete the pyramid, wins. There are eight strips
              in total so Scouters may need to hold onto more
              than one strip.

        10   QUIET GAME: Harbour Master

             One Cub is blindfolded and is the ship. Another
             Cub is the lighthouse operator. The rest of the
             pack spread themselves throughout the playing
             area and are the buoys, rocks and any other
             obstacles which you might find in the sea. These
             obstacles cannot talk or move and the lighthouse
             operator must remain at the finish point (port).
             Using only his/her voice, the lighthouse operator
             must guide the ship safely through the harbour and
             into the Port. Switch roles and repeat the game.
