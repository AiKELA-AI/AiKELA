SIX ACTIVITY: Story Time

In their Sixes, the Cubs collaborate and come up with a story.

E.G. – as we were walking down the beach early one morning, we heard a gunshot. Next minute the robbers ran into the sea with the jewels, dragging lots of sea weed behind them. We knew we had to do something, so started throwing sea shells at them………….

Imagination

No matter how we are challenged, many things can be achieved, if we put our minds to it.

10

RELAY GAME: Toe Testing

Cubs in relay formation. The bucket is placed a distance away, opposite each Six. The pegs are scattered in between. The first Cub will run to a peg, pick it up using his/her toes, place it in the bucket and run to the back of the Six and sit down. The next Cub will do the same and so on until everyone has had a turn. First Six all sitting down is the winner.

1 bucket per Six; wooden pegs (at least one per Cub)

Have you ever tried using your toes to write your name?

10

CIRCLE GAME: Toe Writing

Cubs are given a pencil and paper. They are to try writing their names only using their toes.

Pencils; paper

We all have different foot-prints. Can you identify some of these prints?

15

FOOT-PRINT TRAIL: Foot Trail

Cubs are to follow a foot-print trail. They are to identify 10 different footprints. Upon their return, they try to match the footprint to the name of the animal.

A set of foot-prints and name cards per Six.
