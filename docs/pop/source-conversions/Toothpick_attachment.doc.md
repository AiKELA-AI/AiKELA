| Yarn: | Why the Woodpecker pecks at the trees and has a long beak |
| --- | --- |

The woodpecker was originally not a bird at all, but an old lady with a very long nose, which she put into everybody’s pots and pans, telling tales and making all kinds of mischief. One day, the Great Spirit saw her doings. He took a sack and filled it with midges, beetles, ants, etc. When it was full, he said to her: “Take this and carry it home; beware of opening it, because if you put your nose into it, it will be the worse for you.” “Heaven forbid,” said the old lady. “I’ll be very careful.” She started home, but her fingers itched. She had only gone a short way when she sat down in a meadow and opened the sack. It was just what the insects wanted. They hid in the grass, climbed the trunks of the trees, etc. When the old lady saw this, she was frightened. She tried to put them back again, but they wouldn’t wait. Some she managed to catch, but not all. Then the Voice of the Great Spirit came: “Where are the beetles, etc. which I gave you to carry? You shall be a bird from now and go about picking up insects until you get my sack full again. Only then will you become a human being once more.” The old lady became a woodpecker, with a long beak, and she has not completed her task yet.

(Rumanian Bird and Beast Stories, M. Gaster)
