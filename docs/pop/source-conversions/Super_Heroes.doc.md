TEAM GAME: Super Hero to the rescue

In their Sixes, two Cubs must transport each of their remaining Super Heroes, one at a time, from one side of the playing area to the other. Two Cubs hold the Cub by an arm and leg on each side. They carry the Cub to the opposite side of the playing area, leave him/her there and return for the next Cub, until all the other Cubs have been carried across.

None

Super Heroes all seem to wear some form of head-gear – helmets, caps, masks, etc.

15

PLAYACTING: Who am I?

The leader gives each Cub some newspaper and sticky tape and they make a hat of a Super Hero. When every Cub has made a hat, the leader asks each, in turn, to act their character and the rest of the Pack must guess who they are.

A supply of newspapers; sticky tape

10

REVISION GAME: Nature Noughts & Crosses

Demarcate an area using either rope or chalk – 3 x 3 blocks = 9 squares. One team are the “O’s” and the others the “X’s”. The leader asks questions relating to conservation and preserving the environment. As they answer correctly, the Cubs move to a square of their choice. First Six to get 3 in a row is the winner. Use more than one set of blocks for large packs.

Rope or chalk to demarcate the squares
