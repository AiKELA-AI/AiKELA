CIRCLE GAME: Lost in the desert.

All the Cubs stand in a circle with their right arms outstretched, with one Cub outside. He/she runs around the outside of the circle and grabs an arm of one of the other Cubs who follow him/her around. They go collecting others until there are about six to eight running around. Then the first runner calls out home and they all dash to get in the circle. The Cub who is left out is the new runner.

KIM’S GAME: Uncle George

The leader tells the Pack about Uncle George and at the same time draws his portrait on a big piece of paper; e.g. he has only one hair left on the top of his head; he has a moustache, etc. Ten to 15 different features should be mentioned. Then the leader turns the paper around and Cubs have to draw Uncle George from memory.

Paper; pencils; large sheet of paper; marking pen; Prestik

PLAYACTING: What were you doing when the storm cleared?

The Pack forms a circle and passes around the broomstick or similar. When the leader blows the whistle, the Cub holding the broomstick has to act with it, e.g. using it as a fishing rod, sword or even a hair brush – something they might have been doing when the sand storm cleared. If the cubs act something which has been done before, they pay a forfeit.

Broomstick or similar; whistle
