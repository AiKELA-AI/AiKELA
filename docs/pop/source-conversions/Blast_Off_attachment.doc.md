| Activity: | Rocket Launch |
| --- | --- |

Materials:

1 drinking straw

1 long piece of string

Balloon (round balloons work better than long balloons – they are easier to blow up!)

Masking tape

2 chairs

Procedure:

Thread the string through the straw.

Tape the ends of the string to two chairs and push them apart to create tension. The string will form the path of the rocket.

Blow up the balloon and hold the end so the air does not escape. Tape the balloon to the straw lengthwise so that the balloon can propel the straw.

Move the balloon to one end of the string and release!

If you set up more than one balloon rocket, you can have a balloon rocket race!

#### What you'll need:

2 large coffee tins (used)

Hand bottle/can opener (with the triangular end)

2 long pieces of rope

Heavy-duty scissors (for cutting the rope)

Various colors of electrical tape or various colors of paint

and a paintbrush (optional)

How to make it:

Use the bottle/can opener to punch two holes in either side of one tin towards the top.

Repeat with other tin.

Cut two pieces of rope long enough to go through the holes in the can and up to the child's hands to hold on to.

Thread each piece of rope through the holes in the cans.

Tie the two ends of each piece of rope together. You should have a loop of rope going through each can that is long enough so your child can hold onto the rope as handles.

Use the scissors to cut the different colors of electrical tape and decorate the cans. You can also use the paint to decorate them (optional).

How to play the game:

Cubs in their Sixes; each Cub takes a turn walking on the stilts to a given point and back again.
