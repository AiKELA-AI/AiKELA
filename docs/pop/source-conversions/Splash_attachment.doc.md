| Yarn: | The Flood |
| --- | --- |

A number of families living on the banks of a river were warned to evacuate their houses. The rivers were overflowing after the heavy rains and the homes were in danger of being washed away. The people were being warned to move to higher ground.

Peter’s family was moving out of their home and he suddenly remembered the dear old lady who lived across the road. She was deaf. He had visited her often and helped her in the garden. Peter was a Cub and loved to do good turns for her. She had a lovely home. It was always clean and tidy – everything was in its place.

Peter realised that she would not hear the shouts of warning. The water was already beginning to rise and Peter decided that he must make sure that she was awake and safe. He waded across the road and was feeling very frightened, but he was determined to help. He tried to remember all he had learned at Cubs; don’t panic – listen – hold on to trees or solid structures. He prayed quietly for God’s help. Soon he felt the fence around the house. He banged on the door and then realised that the old lady would not hear him. He broke a small window, climbed into the house and ran down the passage to her bedroom. He quickly woke her and wrote a short message to tell her what was happening. She was very frightened and asked him to find her handbag and slippers – they were just where she said they would be. Peter helped her to safety and sat with her until the danger had passed.

She thanked him over and over again. Peter just smiled – he had only done his best. He had been able to help because of what he had learned at Cubs and because the old lady was so neat and tidy.

| Activity: | Rain Gauge |
| --- | --- |

Equipment:

2 litre cold drink bottle per Cub

Small stones or gravel

Black permanent marker

Ruler

Scissors

Cut the top off the bottle.

Place some stones in the bottom of the bottle. Turn the top upside down and tape it to the bottle (like a funnel).

Use a ruler and marker pen to make a scale on the bottle.

Pour water into the bottle until it reaches the bottom strip on the scale.

Cubs to put their rain gauge outside where it can collect water when it starts raining.

After a rain shower has finished, check to see how far up the scale the water has risen.
