|  | 05 | STEAM RELEASE: Come Along All but one form a large circle. The Cubs turn to the right, with right arms stretched to shoulder level. The Cub ‘out’ runs round the circle; after a short distance, he/she gets hold of an arm and says “Come along!” This Cub falls in behind him/her, and both run round the circle. The second Cub then grabs someone, and says “Come along!” The one caught falls in behind No. 2. Continue until about six or eight are running. Then the first runner shouts “home” and all must try to get a place in the circle. The one left out is the next runner. | None |  |

|  | 10 | CIRCLE GAME: Friends Cubs form two circles; each Cub of the outer circle has a partner. In the centre of the circles are the washers/curtain rings (or other suitable items), one less than the number of Cubs in the outer circle. On the word ‘go’ each Cub of the outer circle has to run around the circle once and then dive between the legs of his/her partner and take one “ring”. He/she must then jump on his/her partner’s back holding it above his/her head. The pair that do not get a “ring” lose a life. The two circles may then change places and the game carries on as before with one less “ring’ in the centre each time. NB: It is important that the partners are of the same size. | Washers/curtain rings or something similar |  |
