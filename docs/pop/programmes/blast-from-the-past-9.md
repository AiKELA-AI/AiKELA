## Page 1

Team
 Game:            Shelter Building

An idea for the clue for the Cubs to find.

Adapt the clue to according to what you have on hand in your hall. The idea of the challenge is for the Cubs
to build something which will withstand the harsh elements of the desert – at the end of the challenge, if
available, get two or three fans blowing wind onto the shelter to see if it can withstand the wind blowing ….



                         CONGRATULATIONS
  You have found the clue which will inform you of your mission for
                             today …

In the hall you will find some items you will need to build a shelter!

You will need to work in your team and everyone should get a chance to do
 something. You will be judged on how well you work together as well as
                   how you have decorated your shelter.
    Use the newspaper as a cover and make sure you decorate it using
  whatever is on hand. Take the following items and find a spot and start
                        putting your shelter together

     Stave/Common household brooms with rounded handle
                       end, 2 per team
              Duct tape/Masking tape/Sticky tape
                             String
          Cardboard sheets/Newspaper/Garbage bags
       Packet of crayons/kokis/paint and paint brushes
                          Scissors

    If there is anything else you need to build your shelter
    apart from what you have been given, you need to find
                     these items yourselves

                    REMEMBER:
    YOU NEED TO WORK IN YOUR TEAM AND EVERYONE
  SHOULD WORK TOWARDS MAKING IT THE BEST SHELTER
                       EVER!
