| Circle Game: | Guarding the Chocolates (alternate circle game) |
| --- | --- |

Equipment: pile of stones or sticks

One Cub in middle of circle (4 paces across) protects a heap of stones or sticks or wood b kneeling over them. Three others try to steal them without being touched. They start outside the circle and must retire when touched.

| Activity: | Fingerprints |
| --- | --- |

Equipment: stamp pad; piece of card per Cub; pencils

Each Cub puts his/her thumbprints on the stamp pad and then presses them down on his/her card. Everybody is given five minutes to turn their fingerprints into faces with their pencils. The leader inspects and awards points.

If you don’t have a stamp pad make your own:

Cut a piece of sponge to fit inside a jar lid.

Make “ink” from paint and water and soak sponge.
