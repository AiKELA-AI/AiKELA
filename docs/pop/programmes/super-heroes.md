TEAM GAME: Super Hero to the rescue

In their Sixes, two Cubs must transport each of their remaining Super Heroes, one at a time, from one side of the playing area to the other. Two Cubs hold the Cub by an arm and leg on each side. The leader gives each Cub some newspaper and sticky tape and they make a hat of a Super Hero. When every Cub has made a hat, the leader asks each, in turn, to act their character and the rest of the Pack must guess who they are.

REVISION GAME: Nature Noughts & Crosses

Demarcate an area using either rope or chalk – 3 x 3 blocks = 9 squares. One team are the “O’s” and the others the “X’s”. ## Attachment instructions and source material

| Yarn: | The Life of a Super Hero |
| --- | --- |

The leader begins for a minute or two, establishes a few characters and situations, then hands over at an exciting moment to the Cub on his/her right. Each Cub should be given a MAXIMUM of half a minute. The leader has the right to step in at any gap and elucidate any muddle, if necessary.

| Singing: | Out on a lion hunt |
| --- | --- |

(Audience echoes each line and sets up clap/lap-slapping rhythm)

Going on a lion hunt.

Going to catch a big one.

I’m not afraid.

Look, what’s up ahead?

Mud!

Can’t go over it.

Can’t go under it.

Can’t go around it.

Gotta go through it. (Make sloshing sounds and move hands as if slogging)

Repeat, replacing mud with:

Sticks (snap fingers)

Tree (make gestures climbing up and down)

Gate (make gate-opening gestures)

River (make swimming gestures)

Cave (go in it and find lion. Reverse all motions quickly to get home)
