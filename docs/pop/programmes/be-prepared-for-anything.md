## Attachment instructions and source material

| Steam Release: | Obey Orders (alternate steam release game) |
| --- | --- |

For this game Cubs must be very smart and listen carefully. The leader tells the Pack that when he/she calls a certain number the Cubs must do a certain action. For instance:

Stand with right leg up;

Put tongue out;

Hold left foot with right hand;

Sit down;

Hold nose and ears;

Pull a funny face;

And so on. When the number is called any Cub who does a wrong action loses a life. More orders can be added.

| Work Session: | Activity Challenge |
| --- | --- |

| Candle Lighting: | Know the correct way to strike a match and light a candle |
| --- | --- |

| First Aid: | Show how to treat a simple cut or graze; OR Show how to treat insect bites and stings, and how to remove splinters. |

You may want to change the above challenges to meet the needs of your Cubs.
