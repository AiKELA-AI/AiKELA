Programmes-on-a-Plate (PoP)

As part of project REVIT, the Cub work-stream identified a number of key components, one of which is the
need for fun and exciting weekly programmes.

We all live rather hurried lives and don’t always have the time to sit and plan weekly programmes.
Programmes-on-a-plate (PoP) have been developed to ensure that your Cubs receive quality programmes,
which will not only provide the fun and adventure they are seeking, but will also assist them through the
different advancement trails.

PoP will give new Pack Scouters an idea of how weekly programmes should be drawn up, some great ideas
and, more importantly, the confidence to start drawing up their own. Experienced Scouters can use the
ideas to enhance their own programmes.

A lot of time and effort has gone into these PoP and for this we would like to thank the following
programme writers:

Jill Dickinson                                   Debbie Symons                       Joy Hutchinson
Kitty Sales                                      Rochelle Booyens                    Jenny Romans

Pop can be found on www.scouting.org.za/resources/cubs/pop

In order to sustain this exciting concept, and to provide you with even better programmes, we ask that you
evaluate each and every one, once you have run it. Your input on what worked and what didn’t will provide
future programme writers with very useful feedback.

We also call upon each and every Pack Scouter to share any ideas on games, yarns, activities, songs,
playacting, handcraft, etc.; successful ideas which we can use in future programmes. These can be sent to
info@scouting.org.za, clearly marked Cub Programme Ideas.

We hope you and your Cubs enjoy these programmes.
