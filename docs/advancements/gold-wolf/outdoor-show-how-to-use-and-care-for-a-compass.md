# Show how to use and care for a compass.

## Requirement

> Show how to use and care for a compass.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- A compass needle points towards magnetic north; it can be affected by nearby metal or magnets.
- Hold it flat and follow the specific compass instructions demonstrated by the leader.
- Store it safely and keep it away from strong magnets and rough handling.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Compass activity](../../activities/compass.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
