# Identify at least ten traffic signs correctly.

## Requirement

> Identify at least ten traffic signs correctly.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Traffic signs use shapes, colours, words and symbols to communicate instructions or warnings.
- Meanings should be taught using current South African road signs and reliable local materials.
- Practise recognising signs from pictures in a safe indoor setting; never play in the road.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Safety work session](../../activities/safety.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
