# Discuss the natural dangers you may find in the veld.

## Requirement

> Discuss the natural dangers you may find in the veld.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Natural hazards vary by place and season and may include weather, steep or slippery ground, water, plants and animals.
- Notice hazards from a safe distance and tell the leader; do not touch unfamiliar plants, animals or substances.
- Before an outing, follow the leader’s safety briefing and stay with the group.
