# On a street map explain and recognise six to ten map symbols.

## Requirement

> On a street map explain and recognise six to ten map symbols.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Map symbols are small signs that stand for real places or features.
- Use the map legend to learn what each symbol means; symbols can differ between map types.
- Practise locating the symbols and matching them to places on the map.
