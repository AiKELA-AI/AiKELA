# Identify everyday activities in your home which waste or consume energy.

## Requirement

> Identify everyday activities in your home which waste or consume energy.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Energy is used for lighting, heating, cooking and running appliances.
- Leaving something on when it is not needed can waste energy.
- Cubs can suggest practical ways to save energy with their family, without changing appliances or electrical fittings themselves.
