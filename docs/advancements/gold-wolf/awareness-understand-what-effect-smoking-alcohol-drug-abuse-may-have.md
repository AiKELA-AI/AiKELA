# Understand what effect smoking, alcohol and drug abuse may have on your body.

## Requirement

> Understand what effect smoking, alcohol and drug abuse may have on your body.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Smoking can harm the lungs and make breathing and physical activity harder.
- Alcohol and drugs can affect judgement, coordination and health; some substances are especially dangerous to children.
- If offered a substance or worried about someone, say no and tell a trusted adult; avoid blame or frightening detail.
