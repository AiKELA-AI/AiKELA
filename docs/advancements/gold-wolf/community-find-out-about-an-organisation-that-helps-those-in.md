# Find out about an organisation that helps those in need in your community and do something to support it.

## Requirement

> Find out about an organisation that helps those in need in your community and do something to support it.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Community section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#community)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Community organisations respond to different needs, such as food, shelter, health, animals or the environment.
- Find out what the chosen organisation does, who it helps and what support it actually needs.
- Choose a safe, practical contribution with the organisation and adult leaders; respect the dignity and privacy of people receiving help.
