# Show a sportsmanlike attitude in all Pack games and activities and know why this is important.

## Requirement

> Show a sportsmanlike attitude in all Pack games and activities and know why this is important.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Sportsmanship means playing fairly, following agreed rules and respecting teammates, opponents and leaders.
- Winning and losing are part of games; congratulate others and handle disappointment safely.
- If a rule or result seems unfair, speak calmly to the Scouter rather than arguing or retaliating.
