# Understand the importance of always drinking clean drinking water. Demonstrate how to make water safe for drinking, if no clean water is available.

## Requirement

> Understand the importance of always drinking clean drinking water. Demonstrate how to make water safe for drinking, if no clean water is available.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Water that looks clear is not always safe to drink; germs or chemicals may be invisible.
- Use water known to be safe and follow the current official method taught by a qualified adult if treatment is needed.
- Never experiment by drinking untreated water; the demonstration should be supervised and clearly labelled.
