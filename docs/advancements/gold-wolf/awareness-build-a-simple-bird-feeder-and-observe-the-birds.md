# Complete one of the following: build a simple bird-feeder and observe the birds that visit it for at least two weeks OR hold the Birds badge; or describe the uses of trees and take care of a tree for one month.

## Requirement

> Do one of the following:
> - Build a simple bird-feeder and observe the birds that visit it for at least two weeks OR hold the Birds badge.
> - Describe the uses of trees. Take care of a tree for one month.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Birds need suitable food, water, shelter and safe places to nest; needs differ between species.
- Keep a feeder clean, place it where it is safe for birds, and observe without disturbing them. Follow local wildlife guidance about suitable food.
- For the tree option, discuss uses such as shade, habitat, materials and helping hold soil; caring for a tree means observing and meeting its needs over time.
