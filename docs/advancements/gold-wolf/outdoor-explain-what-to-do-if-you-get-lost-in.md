# Explain what to do if you get lost in the veld.

## Requirement

> Explain what to do if you get lost in the veld.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Stop moving, stay in a safe visible place and call or signal for help.
- Do not wander further or hide from rescuers; follow the agreed lost-person plan.
- Tell a trusted adult immediately when found; practise only in a supervised scenario.
