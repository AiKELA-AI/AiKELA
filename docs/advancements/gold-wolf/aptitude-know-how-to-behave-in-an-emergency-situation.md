# Know how to behave in an emergency situation.

## Requirement

> Know how to behave in an emergency situation.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Stay as calm as possible, move away from immediate danger if safe, and alert a responsible adult.
- Give clear information: what happened, where you are and whether anyone is hurt.
- Do not take risks or try unfamiliar rescue or first-aid actions; follow the adult’s instructions.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [First Aid activity](../../activities/first-aid.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
