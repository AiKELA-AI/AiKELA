# Help pitch a tent and know how to take care of it OR build a simple shelter.

## Requirement

> Help pitch a tent and know how to take care of it OR build a simple shelter.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Keep the tent clean and dry where possible, and use it only as the leader directs.
- Avoid flames and cooking inside or near a tent; keep exits clear and pegs/guylines visible.
- Pack away only when dry if possible and check the area for rubbish or damage.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Shelter Building game](../../games/shelter-building.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
