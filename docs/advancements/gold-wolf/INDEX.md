Healthy Body: do four of five options; the exercise option itself requires two exercises. Communication: do two out of three. Earn at least one interest badge per challenge (four total); any four interest badges may be earned. Selection notes appear on the applicable task pages. Check the full requirements when deciding what counts toward the badge.
