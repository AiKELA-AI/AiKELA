# Find out about two infectious diseases and discuss how to prevent them from spreading with your Pack Scouter/Pack.

## Requirement

> Find out about two infectious diseases and discuss how to prevent them from spreading with your Pack Scouter/Pack.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Infectious diseases are caused by germs that can spread in different ways, such as through air, hands, food or insects.
- Prevention depends on the disease and may include handwashing, vaccination, ventilation or safe food and water.
- Choose two examples with an adult using current trusted health information; avoid stigma toward people who are ill.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Healthy Eating work session](../../activities/healthy-eating.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
