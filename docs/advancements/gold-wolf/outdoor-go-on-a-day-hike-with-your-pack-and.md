# Go on a day hike with your Pack and know what to wear and take with you.

## Requirement

> Go on a day hike with your Pack and know what to wear and take with you.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Wear comfortable weather-appropriate clothes and suitable closed shoes.
- Pack only what the leader requests; useful basics may include water, sun protection and a light layer.
- Stay with the group, follow the route leader and tell an adult if tired, unwell or uncomfortable.
