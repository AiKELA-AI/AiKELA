# Know the parts of the flag.

## Requirement

> Know the parts of the flag.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Use the South African flag diagram or official Pack resource to identify the parts accurately.
- Describe the flag’s colours and layout without assigning invented meanings to them.
- Handle and display the flag respectfully according to Pack guidance.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [SA Flag game](../../games/sa-flag.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
