# Explain what to do in the case of a fire emergency.

## Requirement

> Explain what to do in the case of a fire emergency.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Raise the alarm and tell an adult immediately; give the location and describe the fire.
- Leave by the safe route, do not hide or go back for belongings, and gather at the agreed meeting place.
- Do not try to fight a fire; follow the adult’s directions and current fire-safety guidance.
