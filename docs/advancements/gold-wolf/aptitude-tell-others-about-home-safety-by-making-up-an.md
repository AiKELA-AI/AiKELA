# Tell others about home safety by making up an activity or game.

## Requirement

> Tell others about home safety by making up an activity or game.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Discuss common hazards such as heat, falls, sharp objects, medicines, cleaning products and electricity.
- Prevention often means keeping hazards secured and asking an adult before using unfamiliar equipment or substances.
- Have Cubs share simple, accurate safety messages; household arrangements differ, so avoid presenting one home plan as universal.
