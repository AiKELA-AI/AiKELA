# Know and understand the following terms: Cyberbullying, Surfing the net, Identity theft, Social network.

## Requirement

> Know and understand the following terms: Cyberbullying, Surfing the net, Identity theft, Social network.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Cyberbullying: using digital communication to repeatedly hurt, threaten or humiliate someone.
- Surfing the net: exploring websites and other online content. Social network: an online service where people connect and share.
- Identity theft: someone using another person’s identifying information without permission; protect passwords and tell an adult about suspicious requests.
