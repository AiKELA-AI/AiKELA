# Find out how plants help fight air pollution & run an experiment to show this.

## Requirement

> Find out how plants help fight air pollution & run an experiment to show this.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Plants take in carbon dioxide for growth and can trap some dust on leaves, but they do not remove every kind of air pollution.
- Trees and other plants also provide shade and habitat.
- An experiment can show one small effect; discuss what it can and cannot prove.
