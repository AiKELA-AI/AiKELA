# Know and explain how to activate the Emergency Medical Services in your neighbourhood.

## Requirement

> Know and explain how to activate the Emergency Medical Services in your neighbourhood.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Explain that Emergency Medical Services respond when someone is seriously ill or injured and needs urgent professional help.
- Cubs should know how to get a trusted adult, give the location clearly and share what has happened.
- Teach the locally verified emergency contact method for the Pack’s area; practise what information to communicate without making a real call.
