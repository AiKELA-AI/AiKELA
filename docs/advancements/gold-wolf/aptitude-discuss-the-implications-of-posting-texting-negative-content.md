# Discuss the implications of posting/texting negative content.

## Requirement

> Discuss the implications of posting/texting negative content.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Online messages can be copied, shared and seen by people beyond the intended audience.
- Negative posts can hurt someone, damage trust and create lasting consequences.
- Pause before posting, do not join in, and tell a trusted adult if content is harmful or worrying.
