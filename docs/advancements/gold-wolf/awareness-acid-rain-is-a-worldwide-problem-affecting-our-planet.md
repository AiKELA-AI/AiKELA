## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Acid rain forms when certain air pollutants mix with moisture in the atmosphere and return in rain or other precipitation.
- It can affect lakes, soils, plants and buildings; impacts depend on local conditions.
- Use a safe demonstration or supplied experiment to model effects, and explain that a model is not the full process.
