# Discuss the importance of never giving out personal information to someone you have never met.

## Requirement

> Discuss the importance of never giving out personal information to someone you have never met.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Personal information can identify or locate someone, such as a full name, address, school, phone number, passwords or live location.
- Only share information online with trusted adults’ guidance; privacy settings do not guarantee privacy.
- If someone asks for personal details, stop replying and tell a trusted adult.
