# Know how the 24 hour clock works. Using this system, give correctly the times of the main events of your day OR show an adult that you know what time to carry out important actions during the day.

## Requirement

> Know how the 24 hour clock works. Using this system, give correctly the times of the main events of your day OR show an adult that you know what time to carry out important actions during the day.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- The 24-hour clock counts from 00:00 at midnight through 23:59; afternoon times continue after 12:00.
- Compare familiar examples such as 07:00 in the morning and 19:00 in the evening.
- Practise reading and matching times to daily events, then let Cubs complete the choice stated in the requirement.
