# Show how to use a street map.

## Requirement

> Show how to use a street map.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- A map is a simplified view of an area from above.
- Use the title, street names and key to understand what the map shows.
- Relate a known starting point to the map and trace a simple route with the leader.
