# Find out how to treat the effect of poisons that can be found at home.

## Requirement

> Find out how to treat the effect of poisons that can be found at home.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Poisonous substances can include some medicines, cleaning products, chemicals and plants; a substance can be dangerous even if it looks or smells harmless.
- Teach prevention: never taste or touch an unknown substance, and ask an adult before using medicines or household products.
- If exposure is suspected, tell an adult immediately and follow professional poison-information or emergency advice. Do not guess at treatment; keep the product container or name available for the adult if safe to do so.

**Scouter reference:** [South African National Department of Health: Poison Information Centres](https://www.health.gov.za/eh-contacts/) — check current contact details and follow professional advice.
