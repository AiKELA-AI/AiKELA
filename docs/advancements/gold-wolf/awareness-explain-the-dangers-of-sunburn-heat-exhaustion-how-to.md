# Explain the dangers of sunburn and heat exhaustion and how to prevent them.

## Requirement

> Explain the dangers of sunburn and heat exhaustion and how to prevent them.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Too much sun can damage skin; excessive heat can make the body dangerously unwell.
- Shade, suitable clothing, rest and water help reduce risk during outdoor activities.
- Tell an adult quickly if someone feels unwell; use current qualified health guidance for response.
