# Tell others about water safety by making up an activity or game.

## Requirement

> Tell others about water safety by making up an activity or game.

**Badge:** Gold Wolf
**Source:** [Gold Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Gold-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Open water, pools and even unfamiliar water features can present risks; conditions are not always easy to judge.
- Stay with responsible adult supervision, use approved places and follow local rules.
- If someone is in trouble, alert an adult and call for help; do not enter the water to attempt a rescue.
