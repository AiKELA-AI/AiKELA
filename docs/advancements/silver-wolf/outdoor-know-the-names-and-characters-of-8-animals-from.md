# Know the names and characters of 8 animals from the Jungle Book.

## Requirement

> Know the names and characters of 8 animals from the Jungle Book.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Review the names and distinguishing traits of the eight Jungle Book characters selected for the Pack’s version.
- Use the supplied story or approved Pack material so Cubs learn the intended characters accurately.
- Discuss how each character behaves in the story; this is story learning, not a claim about real animal behaviour.
