# Go on a nature ramble with your Pack and listen to and identify sounds, look for and identify, if possible, some interesting natural objects.

## Requirement

> Go on a nature ramble with your Pack and listen to and identify sounds, look for and identify, if possible, some interesting natural objects.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Listen quietly and notice that natural sounds may come from birds, insects, wind, water or other sources.
- Look closely at natural objects without damaging or removing living things; describe shape, colour, texture and location.
- If unsure of a sound or object, describe what was noticed rather than guessing its identity.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Living Things activity](../../activities/living-things.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
