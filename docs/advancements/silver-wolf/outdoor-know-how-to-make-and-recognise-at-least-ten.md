# Know how to make and recognise at least ten woodcraft signs.

## Requirement

> Know how to make and recognise at least ten woodcraft signs.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Woodcraft signs are simple marks or arrangements used to leave messages along a route.
- Show only signs agreed for the activity; meanings can vary, so explain the key before starting.
- Use temporary, low-impact materials and remove them afterwards; never damage living trees or property.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Foot Trail activity](../../activities/foot-trail.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
