# Throw a ball to somebody else, and catch it again eight times out of ten.

## Requirement

> Throw a ball to somebody else, and catch it again eight times out of ten.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## Challenge

**Awareness — Healthy Body, Do three of the following:** This task is one of the five Healthy Body options in the 2019 Cub Trail group. Do three of the five options.

## Related elements

These games, activities and crafts offer relevant practice. Read each note to see what it covers; a link does not prove that a Cub completed the requirement.

- [Ball Pass](../../games/ball-pass.md) — The game has Cubs throw and catch a ball. Rotate roles so each Cub practises both actions and record the required eight catches out of ten; the game score alone does not establish this task.
- [Bush Ball](../../games/bush-ball.md) — Each Cub passes a ball by throwing and catching. Rotate the starting and final positions so each Cub practises both actions, and record the required eight catches out of ten.
