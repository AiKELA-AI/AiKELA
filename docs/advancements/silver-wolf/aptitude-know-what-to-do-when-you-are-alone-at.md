# Know what to do when you are alone at home.

## Requirement

> Know what to do when you are alone at home.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Follow the household plan made with a parent or caregiver, including how to contact them.
- Keep doors secure, do not let unknown people in and do not share that you are alone.
- If worried or in danger, contact a trusted adult using the agreed plan.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Stranger Danger work session](../../activities/stranger-danger.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
