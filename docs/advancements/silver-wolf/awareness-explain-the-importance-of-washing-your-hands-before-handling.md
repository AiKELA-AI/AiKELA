# Explain the importance of washing your hands before handling food, before eating, and after using the washroom.

## Requirement

> Explain the importance of washing your hands before handling food, before eating, and after using the washroom.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Hands pick up dirt and germs from surfaces, people and the toilet.
- Washing with soap and clean water helps remove them and reduces spread to food and other people.
- Demonstrate washing all hand surfaces and drying with a clean method.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Healthy Eating work session](../../activities/healthy-eating.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
