# Show that you understand how to look after yourself when you are outdoors and active on a sunny day.

## Requirement

> Show that you understand how to look after yourself when you are outdoors and active on a sunny day.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- The sun and heat can make people ill, especially during active play.
- Shade, suitable clothing, rest and drinking water help protect the body.
- Tell an adult promptly if you feel dizzy, sick, unusually tired or unwell; follow current local health guidance.
