# As part of a Pack activity outdoors, show that you know and practise the Outdoor Code.

## Requirement

> As part of a Pack activity outdoors, show that you know and practise the Outdoor Code.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- The Outdoor Code is about caring for nature, respecting other people and leaving places as you found them.
- Explain local Pack expectations for litter, plants, wildlife and shared spaces.
- Practise the ideas during the outdoor activity rather than treating them only as words to memorise.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [Outdoor Code game](../../games/outdoor-code.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
