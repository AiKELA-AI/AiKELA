# Explain the dangers of talking to strangers.

## Requirement

> Explain the dangers of talking to strangers.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- A person’s safety cannot be judged only by whether they look familiar or friendly.
- Teach Cubs to seek help from a trusted adult, stay with their group and never go somewhere or accept a lift without permission.
- Practise a clear “no” and how to get away and tell a trusted adult; avoid frightening or blaming children.
