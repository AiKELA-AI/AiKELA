# Show how to tell the time and demonstrate the importance of being punctual.

## Requirement

> Show how to tell the time and demonstrate the importance of being punctual.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Being punctual helps a group start together and shows respect for other people’s time.
- Plan enough time to get ready and travel, allowing for delays.
- A clock or watch can help organise the day; practise reading the time in the format used in the task.
