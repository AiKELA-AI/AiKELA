# Know the importance of summoning adult help in the case of accidents.

## Requirement

> Know the importance of summoning adult help in the case of accidents.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- An adult can organise help and keep the injured person safer.
- Cubs should get a nearby responsible adult and explain what happened and where.
- Do not ask Cubs to attempt care beyond their training; use current qualified first-aid guidance.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [First Aid activity](../../activities/first-aid.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
