# Make and use the required Silver Wolf knots

## Requirement

> Make and use the following knots: reef knot; slip reef; round turn and two half hitches; figure of eight.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf  
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor — Knotting](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#knotting)

## Challenge

**Challenge:** Outdoor

## Related elements

These games, activities and crafts offer relevant practice. Read each note to see what it covers; a link does not prove that a Cub completed the requirement.

- [Knotting Revision of the knots the](../../games/knotting-revision-of-the-knots-the.md) — The game practises the reef knot, slip reef and figure of eight. It does not include the required round turn and two half hitches, so add and assess that knot separately.
- [Knotting Market On ‘go’ the](../../games/knotting-market-on-go-the.md) — The game lets Cubs tie knots for checking. Have them practise all four Silver knots: reef, slip reef, round turn and two half hitches, and figure of eight.
