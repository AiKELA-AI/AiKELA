# Know the eight main points of the compass.

## Requirement

> Know the eight main points of the compass.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- The four cardinal directions are north, east, south and west.
- The four points between them are north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west.
- Use a compass or a clearly marked diagram to practise how the directions relate.
