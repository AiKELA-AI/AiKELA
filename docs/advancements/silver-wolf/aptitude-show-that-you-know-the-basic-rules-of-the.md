# Show that you know the basic rules of the road as a pedestrian.

## Requirement

> Show that you know the basic rules of the road as a pedestrian.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Use a safe crossing place where available and stop before entering the road.
- Look and listen for traffic in every direction; cross only when it is safe and keep paying attention.
- Walk with an adult where appropriate and follow local road-safety guidance.
