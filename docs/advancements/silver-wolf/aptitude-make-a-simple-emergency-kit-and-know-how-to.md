# Make a simple emergency kit and know how to use it.

## Requirement

> Make a simple emergency kit and know how to use it.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- An emergency kit holds useful supplies for the situation and household.
- Items might include a torch, water, basic contact information and supplies chosen by the family.
- A responsible adult should choose, store and check the kit; Cubs should know where it is and when to ask for help.
