# Show that you understand why you should keep your body, hands, nails and teeth clean.

## Requirement

> Show that you understand why you should keep your body, hands, nails and teeth clean.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Washing the body and hands removes dirt and helps limit germs and unpleasant smells.
- Clean nails reduce dirt being carried into the mouth or onto food.
- Brushing teeth helps remove food and plaque; regular dental care matters.
