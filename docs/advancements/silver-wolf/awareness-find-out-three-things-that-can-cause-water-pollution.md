# Find out three things that can cause water pollution and show how you could possibly undo pollution in water.

## Requirement

> Find out three things that can cause water pollution and show how you could possibly undo pollution in water.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Pollution can enter water through litter, oil or chemicals, and dirty water from homes or farms.
- Pollution can harm people, animals and plants that depend on that water.
- Prevention is safest: keep rubbish and harmful substances out of drains and waterways; clean-ups should be adult-led and never involve touching unknown substances.
