# Explain the dangers of swimming in the type of water found in your area.

## Requirement

> Explain the dangers of swimming in the type of water found in your area.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Local water hazards vary: currents, waves, sudden depth changes, slippery edges and cold water can all be dangerous.
- Water may look calm while conditions below or farther away are unsafe.
- Cubs should swim only with responsible adult supervision in an appropriate place and follow local safety instructions.
