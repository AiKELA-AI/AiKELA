# Show that you respect your uniform and understand the importance of wearing it.

## Requirement

> Show that you respect your uniform and understand the importance of wearing it.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Outdoor section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#outdoor)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Uniform helps members recognise one another and can show belonging to the Pack.
- Explain the Pack’s expectations for wearing and caring for uniform, including comfort and practical needs.
- Respect includes treating others kindly whether or not they are wearing uniform.
