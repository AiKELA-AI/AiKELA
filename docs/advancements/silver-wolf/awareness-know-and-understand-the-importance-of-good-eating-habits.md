# Know and understand the importance of good eating habits.

## Requirement

> Know and understand the importance of good eating habits.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Food gives the body energy and nutrients for growing, learning and play.
- A varied diet includes different kinds of foods; water helps keep the body hydrated.
- Healthy choices should be discussed without shaming foods, bodies, allergies, culture or what families can afford.
