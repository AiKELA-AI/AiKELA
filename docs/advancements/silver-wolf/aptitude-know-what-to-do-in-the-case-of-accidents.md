# Know what to do in the case of accidents at home or at your pack meeting place.

## Requirement

> Know what to do in the case of accidents at home or at your pack meeting place.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Pause, check for immediate danger and get a responsible adult straight away.
- Tell the adult clearly what happened, where it happened and who needs help.
- Do not move someone who may have a serious injury unless there is immediate danger; follow trained adult direction.

**Related teaching resources:**
- [First Aid activity](../../activities/first-aid.md) — use as a discussion or practice aid; review its instructions before use.
