# Explain to a Pack Scouter what conservation means.

## Requirement

> Explain to a Pack Scouter what conservation means.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Awareness section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#awareness)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Conservation means caring for natural resources and habitats so they remain healthy and available.
- Resources Cubs can recognise include water, soil, plants, animals and clean air.
- Use everyday examples: avoid wasting water, reuse materials, and leave plants and wildlife undisturbed.
