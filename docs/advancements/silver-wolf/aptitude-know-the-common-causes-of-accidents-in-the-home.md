# Know the common causes of accidents in the home.

## Requirement

> Know the common causes of accidents in the home.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Common hazards include hot surfaces, medicines and chemicals, sharp objects, falls, electricity and choking risks.
- Some hazards are hard to see, so children should ask an adult before touching unknown items or using appliances.
- Discuss simple prevention: keep walkways clear and store dangerous items as adults direct.
