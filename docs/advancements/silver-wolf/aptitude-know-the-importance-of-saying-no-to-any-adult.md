# Know the importance of saying “NO” to any adult who wants to do something that makes you feel uncomfortable.

## Requirement

> Know the importance of saying “NO” to any adult who wants to do something that makes you feel uncomfortable.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Cubs may say no and move away if an adult’s request or behaviour makes them feel unsafe or uncomfortable.
- They should tell a trusted adult and keep telling until someone helps.
- Make clear that unsafe behaviour is never the child’s fault, even if the person is known to them.
