# Know the safety rules to follow when in or near water.

## Requirement

> Know the safety rules to follow when in or near water.

**Badge:** Silver Wolf
**Source:** [Silver Wolf requirements, Aptitude section](../../Silver-Wolf-Challenge-Badge-Requirements.md#aptitude)

## What to teach

**Scouter teaching notes (general guidance, not additional badge criteria):**

- Water can be dangerous even when it looks shallow or still.
- Stay with the supervising adult, use only approved swimming places and obey local instructions.
- Do not enter water to rescue someone; call an adult and raise the alarm.
