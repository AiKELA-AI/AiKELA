```text
Relay Race: Drag your shell
Cubs line up in Sixes with Sixer at the
front. On go the Sixer runs to the other
end of the hall to retrieve a piece of card
and rope and runs back. The next cub in
line sits on the cardboard and holds the
rope. The Sixer drags the cub across the
room. When they get to the other side
the cub being dragged brings the
cardboard and rope back and fetches the
next cub etc until the last cub. The last
cub then drags the Sixer back across the
hall. First six to finish and sit wins.
```
