# Underwater Puzzle The

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

Picture of any underwater creature, cut into puzzle pieces

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Underwater Puzzle The Cubs line up in relay formation. On ‘go’ the first Cub runs, picks up one puzzle piece at a time and places it upwards. The next Cub runs and puts a second piece down, and so on until the picture is complete.
