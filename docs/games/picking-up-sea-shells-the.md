# Picking up Sea Shells The

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

1 bucket per Six; sea shells (or substitute with pictures or stones/potatoes)

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Picking up Sea Shells The Cubs line up in relay formation, each with a bucket in front of their team. Also, in front is a line of sea shells about 1 metre apart. On ‘go’ the first Cub runs, picks up the shells one at a time and places them in the bucket. The next Cub puts them out again, while the next puts them back into the bucket, and so on.
