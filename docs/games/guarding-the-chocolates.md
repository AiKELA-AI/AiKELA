# Guarding the Chocolates (alternate circle game)

## Type

Circle Game

## Equipment

pile of stones or sticks

## Instructions

Equipment: pile of stones or sticks

One Cub in middle of circle (4 paces across) protects a heap of stones or sticks or wood b kneeling over them. Three others try to steal them without being touched. They start outside the circle and must retire when touched.
