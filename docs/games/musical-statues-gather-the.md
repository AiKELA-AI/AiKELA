# Musical Statues Gather the

## Type

ACTIVE GAME

## Equipment

Music/whistle

## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Musical Statues Gather the Cubs in a group. Play some music and instruct the Cubs to dance around the room, the more energetic and enthusiastic, the better! The person playing the music will pause the music and instruct the Cubs to freeze. The person playing the music must then decide if anyone is moving too much or froze too late. If so, those Cubs are out. The music starts again and the process is repeated until there is only one Cub left. Instead of music, a whistle can be blown to ‘freeze’ the Cubs.

### Non-elimination clapping variation

[Sound Signal Shuffle](sound-signal-shuffle.md) is an AI-developed variation of this game. It uses a clapped beat and varied in-place movements; Cubs freeze when the clapping stops, nobody is eliminated, and seated or walking alternatives are offered.

### Scouts Digital variant: Nature-sound Statues

**Reuse status:** Existing canonical entry reused; this separately attributed variant comes from **Let’s Go Wild Camp (23–25 October 2026; Honeydew and 1st Randburg)**, Friday Wild Night Walk alternative.

The source offers musical statues with nature sounds (water, crickets, owls) as a walk alternative. Freeze when the sounds stop, then recall/recognise sounds. Equipment: speaker and phone with suitable recordings. It does not provide audio files or an assessment score.
