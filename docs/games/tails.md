# Tails

## Type

STEAM RELEASE

## Equipment

Scarves

## Instructions

STEAM RELEASE: Tails Divide the Pack into two teams. All players tuck their scarves loosely into their belts in back to signify tails. On a signal, each team rushes toward the other, trying to capture their tails. Once a Cub’s tail is taken, that Cub is out of the game. The capturer ties the tail to the front of his/her belt. The team that captures the most tails wins. Set a time limit.
