```text
Game: Stuck in the mud
Aim: Don’t get caught and become “stuck in the mud”!
Choose 2-3 players to be the catchers (“the mud”).
Everyone else runs around inside the playing area.
If a catcher tags you, you are stuck in the mud.
When stuck, stand still with your legs apart and call, “Help me!”
Other players can rescue you by crawling underneath your legs.
Once someone goes underneath your legs, you are free and can run again.
No pushing, pulling, or tackling.
Players must stay inside the marked playing area.
The game ends when everyone is stuck, or after a set time.
Choose new catchers and play again!
```
