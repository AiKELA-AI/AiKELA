# Dog Sled

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Side of a box; rope; packaging tape for reinforcement

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Dog Sled Cubs are put into teams of three. Each team is given a large piece of cardboard box (thick) and a rope tied to the front of the piece of box like a handle. One Cub sits on the “sled” and the other two need to pull him/her to a designated spot in the playing area. Once they get to the spot, they should all carry the box and run back to the start and the next Cub gets to sit on the sled and the remaining two pull it towards the spot. Once all three Cubs have had a turn to sit on the sled, they should go back to the start and sit quietly until all teams are finished. The holes where the rope is tied to the box needs to be reinforced with packing tape so that the box does not tear.
