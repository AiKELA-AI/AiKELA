```text
Activity 1: Steam Release – Mr. Wolf
Choose one cub to be the wolf. The wolf stands at one
end of the room or field, with their back facing the other
cubs. The other cubs line up at the opposite end of the
play area.
The other cubs call out in unison: "What's the time, Mr.
Wolf?" The wolf turns around and yells a time, such as
"It's 3 o'clock!" The cubs take that same number of steps
forward (three steps, in this example). The cubs keep
moving closer to the wolf with each new time.
When the cubs get very close, the wolf can shout: "It's
dinner time!" Instead of giving a time, the wolf turns
around and chases the cubs. The cubs run back to the
starting line to stay safe.
If the wolf tags a cub before they reach safety, that
tagged cub becomes the new wolf for the next round.
```
