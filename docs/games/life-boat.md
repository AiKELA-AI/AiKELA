# Life Boat

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Life Boat Players sit opposite each other, with their legs out straight and feet touching (like a ladder). They are in the ship and it is capsizing. Each pair of players is numbered off. If their number is called, the Cubs need to stand up and race to the end of the row, stepping in between legs. Then run back around the outside, and up through the middle to sit in their place again and they are now in the lifeboat and safe. Note: ensure legs are kept flat and straight at all times and that hands are tucked away as they may get trodden on.
