```text
Game : Drop the Hankerchief
For this traditional Egyptian game, two teams of 5
players or more gather plus one leader. Each person
from the teams call out numbers, 1 through 5. The
leader holds a handkerchief up between the two teams
and yells out a number; the players from both teams
that have that number run and try to catch the
handkerchief before it falls to the ground. If you can
catch it first, you get a point, but only if you can make it
back to your side without the opposite team tagging
you.
```
