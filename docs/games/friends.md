# Friends

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Washers/curtain rings or something similar

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Friends Cubs form two circles; each Cub of the outer circle has a partner. In the centre of the circles are the washers/curtain rings (or other suitable items), one less than the number of Cubs in the outer circle. On the word ‘go’ each Cub of the outer circle has to run around the circle once and then dive between the legs of his/her partner and take one “ring”. He/she must then jump on his/her partner’s back holding it above his/her head. The pair that do not get a “ring” lose a life. The two circles may then change places and the game carries on as before with one less “ring’ in the centre each time. NB: It is important that the partners are of the same size.
