# Animal Sounds

## Type

Sense training game — hearing and finding a group. About 10 minutes, as scheduled in the source; suitable for Cub-age participants with the supervision/adaptation below.

## Equipment

Hat; repeated slips naming animals with recognisable calls; blindfolds in the source version. Enough slips for each animal to form a group.

## Instructions

Reused from the Scouts Digital programme, with wording organised for a Scouter:

1. Each Cub draws an animal name from the hat without showing the others.
2. In the source version, each Cub is blindfolded. On the signal, Cubs move around the hall making only their animal's sound, without talking.
3. Cubs who find another Cub making the same sound stay together and listen for the remaining members of their group.
4. The first complete animal group wins. The source awards each member a marble for their Six; use the selected Pack profile's reward system instead.

## Safety and adaptations

**Scouter guidance, added during extraction:** Do not run a crowded blindfolded search. Clear and bound the space, use walking only and close adult supervision, and stop on any collision risk. Offer an eyes-open version for Cubs who do not want a blindfold; use this version if safe supervision cannot be provided. Pair a Cub who needs help reading or moving with a helper.

**Theme variation:** Keep the listening-and-grouping mechanic and choose animals suited to the meeting theme. Label the result as an adaptation of Animal Sounds. Avoid unfamiliar or indistinguishable calls; visual animal cards can support Cubs who cannot access an audio-only game.
