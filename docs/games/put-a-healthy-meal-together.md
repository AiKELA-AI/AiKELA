# Put a Healthy Meal Together

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

Pictures of food from magazines, paper plates

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Put a healthy meal together …. Pictures of healthy and not so healthy food. One at a time the Cubs need to run up and put something on a paper plate which they think will be a healthy meal. They can swap foods out as well if they feel there is something on the plate which is not as healthy as it should be. Awareness Challenge – Healthy Living. Silver Wolf: Know and understand the importance of good eating habits.
