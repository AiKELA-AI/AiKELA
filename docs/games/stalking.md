# Stalking

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Blindfolds; stones

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Stalking Half the Pack is given blindfolds to wear. These players are scattered within the boundaries of the playing area. A stone is placed between their feet, but not touching them. The other half of the Pack is to stalk the blindfolded players in an effort to obtain the stone from between their feet. In an attempt to pinpoint a stalker, the blindfolded player may point to a sound. If a stalker is caught, the two players switch positions. Stalkers try to collect as many stones as possible without being caught.
