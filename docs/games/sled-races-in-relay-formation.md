# Sled Races In relay formation

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

Cardboard box for each Six

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Sled Races In relay formation. First Cub sits in a big cardboard box. Second Cub pushes him/her over a given course. Second Cub then gets in box and third Cub pushes him/her, and so on until everyone in the Six has had a turn. First Six sitting at the alert is the winner.
