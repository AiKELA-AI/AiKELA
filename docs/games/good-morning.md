# Good Morning

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: “Good Morning” The Cubs are formed up in a circle with their hands behind their backs, and their heads bowed so that they cannot see what is happening. One Cub remains outside the circle; he/she walks round the outside, and touches the hand of one of the Cubs. The Cub who has been touched walks round the circle in the opposite direction from his/her friend. At the point where they meet, they stop, salute, shake hands with the left hand, say ‘good morning’, and then walk on. The first Cub back to the vacant place occupies it, and the other walks round as before.
