ACTIVE GAME: Float or sink
Cubs stand in the middle of the playing area. Label one of the ends as ‘floating’ and the other as ‘sinking’. The Cubs must listen to the objects being called out and then have to run to that end of the playing area. The last one there must tell you a water safety rule before they can re-join the game.
Objects that sink: Rocks; stones; marbles; coins; keys
Objects that float: Apples; wood; empty plastic bottle; ball; inflated balloon; boat
