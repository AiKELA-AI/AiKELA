```text
Smuggling game – The aim is to place the mulberry leaves on a silk shape at the end of the field, without being touched. 2 beans awarded for any leaf safely smuggled. Each six has three leaves which any cub (secretly chosen by the Sixers) may have in his closed hand. One Six (the smugglers) stand on one side of field with another Six (the catchers) standing in the middle ie 3 sets altogether including all 6 Sixes. All make a fist and on “Go!” the smugglers all run to the other side. The opposite sixes try to touch all of them before a leaf is placed on a shape. Swap smugglers and catchers and repeat. Beans awarded if successful.
```
