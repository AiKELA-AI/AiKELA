# Musical Shoes

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

Music; dirty old shoe; complete shoe cleaning kit

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Musical Shoes Cubs sit in a circle and while the music plays they pass around an old and very dirty shoe. When the music stops the Cub with the shoe must fetch an article from the shoe cleaning kit in the centre of the circle and proceed to clean the shoe while the other Cubs watch to see if he/she is doing it correctly. When the music starts again he/she returns his/her equipment and passes on the shoe. When the music stops again the Cub holding the shoe does the next step in cleaning. To give every a Cub a chance, it is best played in Sixes.
