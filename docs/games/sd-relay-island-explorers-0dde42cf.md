```text
Relay: island explorers
Divide the cubs into two island tribes.
Blue tribe and red tribe.
In relay formation, collect one item at a time. Once your tribe member brings an item back the next player can go.
You will need to collect all your items needed to survive the night on our mystery island. But you can only collect the items with the correct colour matching your tribe. 
Once all your items are collected your tribe must make a shelter. You need to tie your shelter down with rope. All your supplies and your tribe must be under your shelter. 1st team done correctly wins.
```
