# Water Flowing

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

1 x hula hoop per team

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Water Flowing Cubs are divided into two teams. The two teams stand in rows, holding hands with their team mates to form a chain. Give the first player on each team a hula hoop. The aim is to pass the hoop along the chain (keep the water flowing), without breaking hands. Step in and out of the hoop, wiggle and jiggle around, and use your knees and elbows to move it along – whatever it takes!
