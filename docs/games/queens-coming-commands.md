## Type

Captain’s Coming variation; 10 minutes

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Call the source’s tasks: fetch the Queen’s tarts, check prisoners, set up croquet, prune roses, find the White Rabbit. The rabbit task involves hopping and looking behind trees/bushes. On “The Queen is coming!”, Cubs line up neatly in their Sixes and report alert; the first Six earns a reward in the source. The other tasks have no fixed movements supplied, so agree and demonstrate the mimes as a labelled local adaptation before play.

```text
Steam Release - The Queens Royal Commands
Set up and how to play
1.⁠ ⁠All in a circle
2.⁠ ⁠Go around and give them a number, 1-4
3.⁠ ⁠Call out, “Cub promise number 1’s”
4.⁠ ⁠They run around, back through their gap, stand in the middle and have to say
the cub promise
5.⁠ ⁠Only the first person to give a word-for-word rendition earns a marble
6.⁠ ⁠Then just cycle through promise, law, motto and keep going 1/2/3/4. That way it
shifts who says what
Last round - to get into there sixes and first six to say the cub promise correctly
together earns 3 marbles
```
