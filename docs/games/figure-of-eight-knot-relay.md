# Figure-of-eight Knot Relay

## Type

Knotting relay; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Ropes and space for two teams.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

First Cub goes to the rope and ties a figure-of-eight knot while standing on one foot. The next unties and reties it behind their back. The source invites further ways of tying it but provides no more challenges. Preserve those two source challenges rather than inventing a sequence.
