# Who Is Missing?

## Type

QUIET GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

**Walking option:** Cubs walk slowly in a circle. At the leader’s signal, everyone covers their eyes. The leader taps one Cub, who leaves quietly. The leader calls “stop”; Cubs uncover their eyes and identify who is missing. Do not walk for long, as Cubs may become dizzy, and watch for peeking.

**Seated option:** Cubs sit with eyes covered while one Cub leaves quietly. Ask who is missing. This option is described in the Temple of Artemis source and avoids walking with covered eyes. Use a clear, supervised exit route.
