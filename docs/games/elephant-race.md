# Elephant Race

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

Chair (optional)

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Elephant Race Cubs in relay formation. First Cub bends at the waist, puts an arm in front of their face like an elephant trunk, and put their other hand through their legs towards the person behind them. The next Cub bends over, grabs the first Cubs ‘tail’ (hand) with their ‘trunk’ (hand), then extends their ‘tail’ through their legs and so on until you have a team of “elephants” lined up and ready to go. The team of elephants can then walk to a tree (chair), go around the tree and back to their Six. First Six sitting at the alert are the winners.
