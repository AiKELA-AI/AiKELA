# Spinning Blades

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

A length of rope with ends tied to form a circle

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Spinning Blades. Cubs pair off with another member of their Six and form a circle, keeping Sixes together. Place the rope loop in the centre. The inner Cubs are “ones” and the outer Cubs are “twos”. On “go”, the twos move clockwise around the circle until they return to their partners. They go through their partner’s legs and try to take the rope for their Six. The ones and twos then swap places and play again. The source suggests pulling a player around the waist if a tug-of-war starts; skip that step and stop the round if players come into contact.
