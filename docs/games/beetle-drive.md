# Beetle Drive

## Type

Circle Game

## Equipment

Dice; beetle picture; pencils

## Instructions

![Source image](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Tree_Tops_attachment.doc/Tree_Tops_attachment_html_9939003c.jpg>)

**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

CIRCLE GAME: Beetle Drive Cubs to form a circle in their Sixes. Each Cub in the circle gets a turn to roll the dice. If they roll a six, they can then draw the beetle’s body. They continue as per the drawing – e.g. 5 for head, etc.
