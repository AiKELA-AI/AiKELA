# Beware the Sabertooth

## Type

Steam-release grouping game; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Whistle or horn; clear playing space.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

1. Cubs scatter and move around as members of a prehistoric tribe.
2. On the whistle they stop and listen. Call a group size: packs of four, groups of three, or cavemen in fives.
3. Cubs form a group of that exact size; the source suggests linking arms or a family huddle.
4. In the source, an unmatched Cub sits out one round or stalks around the edge as a Sabertooth. Repeat with another group size.
