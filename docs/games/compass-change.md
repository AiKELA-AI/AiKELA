# Compass Change

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

Chalk

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Compass Change Draw a circle on the floor and mark the eight points of the compass. Write ‘N’ for North. Put two or three Cubs on each mark, one behind the other. One Cub is in the centre of the circle. The Leader calls two directions. The front (inside) Cubs of each direction called comes out and changes with the other direction (they go to the rear) while the Cub in the centre tries to get to one of the directions first.
