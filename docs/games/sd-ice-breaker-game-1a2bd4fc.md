```text
Ice breaker GAME: 
Directions
Have the group stand in the circle.
Everyone must reach in with both of their hands and grab somebody else’s hand. They must be holding hands with two different people. This creates the “human knot”.
Now, everyone must work together to untie the knot without letting go of any hands. They will need to step over or duck under each other.
In the end they will be untied into on large circle or sometimes two small circles.
Notes
Occasionally you get a combination which makes it impossible to completely untie the knot. In this case, the group leader can direct the knot to break at one point, untangle a little, and then reconnect
```
