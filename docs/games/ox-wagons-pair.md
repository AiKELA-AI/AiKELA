# Ox Wagons Pair

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Ox Wagons Pair Cubs in teams of two and mark off start and finish lines. One player in each team must walk on his/her hands while his/her partner holds his/her ankles (ox harnessed to the front of a wagon). Together they go as fast as they can to the finish line, then switch places and race back to start.
