# Tackie Test

## Type

QUIET GAME

## Equipment

Whistle

## Instructions

QUIET GAME: Tackie Test Cubs lie on their back with their tackies (shoes) very loosely tied. On the blow of the whistle, the Cubs kick their foot backwards to get the tackie (shoe) off the foot and see whose travels the furthest.
