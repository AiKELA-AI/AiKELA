## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Lifeboats The Explorers are on a sinking ship. The leader is the captain. He/she calls out an order, and then a number. The number indicates the capacity of each lifeboat. The order must be obeyed before anyone gets into a lifeboat. Those who do not find a place in a boat lose a life. Example: “Turn three somersaults: five!” Each Cub turns three somersaults, and then endeavours to find a place in a group of five Cubs anywhere in the room. If there are twenty-four Cubs, four must inevitably be drowned. Vary the number each time.
