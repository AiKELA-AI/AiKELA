# Kangaroos in Pouches

## Type
Circle game

## Equipment
Not stated in source.

## Instructions
Half the Cubs form a circle. Each of the other Cubs stands in the middle in front of one Cub in the circle, facing them. This makes an inner circle and an outer circle facing each other. When the leader says “go”, the Cubs in the inner circle go through their partners’ legs and hop around the circle. When the leader says “change direction”, they reverse direction. When the leader says “return to pouch”, they continue in the direction they are going until they reach their partner, then go through their partner’s legs and sit down. The last Cub back is out, as is their partner; both remain seated in the circle. The Cubs swap places with their partners so the others can have a turn. Continue until a pair wins or time allows.
