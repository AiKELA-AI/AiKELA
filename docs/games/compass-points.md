# Compass Points

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

1 card (paper plate) per Six; 1 long pencil per Six

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Compass Points Mark the card (paper plate) with crosses as for the points of the compass, but only name North. The Pack line up in Sixes and the cards are placed opposite each Six at the far end of the playing area. The pencil is placed on the card like the needle of a compass, pointing North. The leader calls out a compass bearing. The first Cub runs to the card and places the pencil as he/she thinks correct. If he/she is right, he/she may stand behind the compass. If he/she is wrong, he/she returns to the back of his/her Six. The game continues until one Six has all its members behind the compass (or time allows).
