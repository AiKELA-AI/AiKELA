# Hat Exchange

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Caps

## Instructions

GAME 4: Hat Exchange Cubs form a circle, all facing in, cap on head. The numbers 1,2,3 and 4 will be called in sequence. Each Cub takes the hat off the person on their right and places the hat on his/her own head. Repeat until it appears to be a continuous movement around the circle. When they have got themselves organised with that way, reverse the hat taking.
