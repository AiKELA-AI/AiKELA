# Touch in Sand

## Type

KIMS GAME

## Equipment

15 items, containers of sand, cloth

## Instructions

KIMS GAME: touch (in sand). Place up to 15 different items in a container filled with sand or similar. One container per Six – cover the container with a cloth or similar. One at a time the Cubs will come up and are told what they need to find in the container. They need to place their hands under the cloth and feel around in the container until they think they have found the requested article. After the first Cub has had a turn, the next lot will try their luck. Continue until all the items have been identified even if the Cubs go more than once. Awareness Challenge – Healthy Mind. Silver and Gold Wolf requirement - Play sense training games using all five senses and achieve a 70% score. (Game played using sense of touch).
