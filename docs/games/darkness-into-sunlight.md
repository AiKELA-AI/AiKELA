# Darkness into the Sunlight — Voice Recognition

## Type

Circle listening game; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Soft newspaper-and-stocking club; blindfold in the source.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Cubs stand in a circle. One blindfolded Cub walks until the soft club touches another Cub. The blindfolded Cub introduces themself with a changing pretend name; the touched Cub replies “We are of one family.” The first Cub guesses who spoke. Swap players and repeat.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Use the club as a gentle pointer, never a striking object. An adult guides the blindfolded Cub; offer a seated/eyes-open alternative.
