# Whizz, Bang, Flash!

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Whizz, Bang, Flash! Cubs sit in a circle; the Leader allocates them either “Whizz, Bang or Flash”. Cubs are given the appropriate action to perform when their firework is called: Catherine Wheel (Whizz) – walk around the circle, spinning as they go. Rocket (Bang) – run around the circle saying “whoosh”. Sparkler (Flash) – walk around the circle in a weaving pattern. Going out – all go back to their own space.
