```text
Active game – House Builders
Cubs line up in relay at the end of the hall. The CI stands a
distance away holding a beanbag (brick). The first Cub
balances a board on his/her head (roof tile). On go the Cub
must walk towards the CI who will throw the beanbag (brick) to
the Cub who must try and catch it. If successful he/she
continues towards the CI, hands the beanbag (brick) over and
runs back to the starting line with the board (tile) in his hand,
for the next Cub to start. The second Cub has a turn until all
have had a turn. If the Cub catches the brick they get a point,
if he/she misses the catch, the CI must collect the brick and
the Cub continues. The Cub with the most points, wins.
```
