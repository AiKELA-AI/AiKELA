# Apes and Leopards

## Type

## Equipment

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Apes and Leopards The Pack divides into two teams. One team stands in line at the end of the room facing the wall. They are the Apes. The second team, who are Leopards, form a line at the opposite end of the room. They creep quietly up on the Apes. When the leader of the Leopards thinks that his/her team has crept as near as they dare, he/she gives the signal and all the team give one loud clap and then turn and run home. As soon as the Apes hear the clap, they turn and chase the Leopards. Any who are caught become Apes. When there are no Leopards left, the teams change roles.
