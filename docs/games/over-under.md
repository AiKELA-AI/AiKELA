# Over, under

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Balloon/ball

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Over, under Players stand behind one another in two teams. The one in front passes a balloon over his/her head and the second passes it under his/her feet. This continues alternately to the end of the line, when the last player runs to the front. Continue until all players have changed places. The first team to complete gives a shout. (Alternative: a simpler way is to have the two teams facing, and to pass the balloon/ball from hand to hand to the end, when the last player runs round with it.)
