```text
Steam Release: a game with a difefrence (long game): The World Scouting Mission
Att Old Wolves:
This is not your average 10min steam release, but rather a fast-paced team
challenge where Cubs race, solve, act and discover their way around the world of
Scouting. This is a 25-minute challenge, consisting of a series of short games. Please
help to keep the Sixes moving and don't let individual challenges become
discussions. The aim is energy and momentum rather than getting every challenge
perfect.
17:55–17:58 - Explain Mission (Kaa) - 3mins
17:58–18:02 - Round 1: The OW shouts out a challenge (4mins)
Cubs start the round lined up in their Sixes, but have the freedom to leave their six
to complete the challenge called out (challenges below are examples and can be
adapted)
● Everyone wearing something GREEN, touch the wall
● Everyone whose name is more than 5 letters, sit down
● Everyone who has been camping, touch the fol or
● Everyone who knows the Cub Promise - hands on your head
● OW shouts ALERT - All Cubs must race to line up with their Six again and
stand at alert
● Each Six receives a puzzle piece on completion of the challenge
18:02–18:06 - Round 2: The Scouting Code (4mins)
● Each Six receives a Secret Code. They need to work together to decipher it
● Points awarded as follows:
2 marbles if the Six only deciphers “Lord Baden Powell”
3 marbles if the Six deciphers “Lord Baden Powell is our founder”
4 marbles if they decipher the full code.
● Once the Six has deciphered their code, they receive a puzzle piece
18:06–18:11 - Round 3: Where does it go? (5mins)
● Each Six member gets a country card (in the colour of their Six)
● In relay form, they have to race with their card and match it to the Country
name, and then run to the back of their line, and the next Cub has a turn
● Cubs may attempt to correct an error when going up for their turn
● Points awarded as follows:
6 marbles if all countries are matched correctly
Then 5, 4, 3, 2 & 1 for the number of correct matches
● Once the Six has completed their challenge, they receive a puzzle piece
🇿🇦 South Africa 🇮🇳 India
🇬🇧 United Kingdom 🇦🇺 Australia
🇯🇵 Japan 🇨🇦 Canada
18:11–18:15 - Round 4: Scouting Charades (4mins)
● Cubs sit in their Sixes
● One Cub is called up at a time
● A Cub activity is whispered in their ear (see examples below)
● They act it out for their Six, and the Six guesses the correct answer
● They have 20 seconds to act it out before the next cub has a turn
● The six earns a marble for every correct guess
● Once everyone has had a turn, they receive a puzzle piece
🏕️ Camping; 🔥 Making a frie; 🥾 Hiking; 🎵 Singing; 🎨 Crafting; 🪢 Tying a knot
```
