## Instructions

QUIET GAME: Harbour Master One Cub is blindfolded and is the ship. Another Cub is the lighthouse operator. The rest of the pack spread themselves throughout the playing area and are the buoys, rocks and any other obstacles which you might find in the sea. These obstacles cannot talk or move and the lighthouse operator must remain at the finish point (port). Using only his/her voice, the lighthouse operator must guide the ship safely through the harbour and into the Port. Switch roles and repeat the game.
