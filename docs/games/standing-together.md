# Standing Together

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Soccer ball or two

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Standing Together The Pack divides into two teams and line up about 3 metres apart facing each other. They stand, legs apart, with their feet touching those of their neighbours. The leader throws a soccer ball down between the lines and each team tries to score a goal by sending the ball through the legs of the other team. The Cubs may only defend with their hands and must not move their feet. When the teams become adept at this, the leader can send in a second ball.
