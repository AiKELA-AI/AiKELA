```text
Steam release- Chuck the Chicken
Divide the pack into two teams. One team shouts
“chuck the chicken” and throws the bean bag as far as
they can. While the other team runs to retrieve it, the
Cub that chucked the beanbag runs to a marker and
back as many times as he/she can, counting out the
number of laps loudly. The opposing team member who
gets the bean bag chucks it back to the next Cub who
repeats. The Cub with the most laps run wins.
```
