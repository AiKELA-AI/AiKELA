# Ball Pass

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Rope; soccer ball

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Ball Pass Best played in an area the size of a badminton court and with a soccer ball. Rope instead of net, any height found suitable. Teams either side must throw the ball so that it falls into the other court. Other team must catch it before it touches the ground. Penalty points for failing to catch the ball, throwing it under the rope or touching the rope, and for throwing it outside the playing area. Score up to 15 or 21.
