# Pick up Coins

## Type

ACTIVE GAME

## Equipment

Small pieces of paper; 1 straw per Cub

## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Pick up Coins Scatter small pieces of paper around a designated area. Cubs to run around and pick up a 'coin' (paper) one at a time, by sucking on a straw. Once they have picked up the coin, they need to place it is a safe place with the rest of the coins for the team. If it drops, they need to stop and pick it up again. When all the coins have finished, the team with the most coins is the winner.
