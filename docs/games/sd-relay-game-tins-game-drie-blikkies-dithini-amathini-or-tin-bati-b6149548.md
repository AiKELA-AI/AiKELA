Equipment:

3 empty tin cans (stacked vertically) and 1 soft ball (a tennis ball, rubber ball, or rolled-up socks).

```text
RELAY GAME: Tins game: Drie Blikkies, Dithini, Amathini, or Tin Bati

1. Knock 'Em Down: Break the tower to start.
Line up 5 steps away from the stacked cans. A Builder throws the ball to knock down the 3 cans. You get 3 tries!
2. Get the Ball:Run and dodge!.
As soon as the cans fall, the Catchers run to grab the ball. The Builders run in to fix the cans!
3. Build and Dodge:Fast hands and quick feet.
Builders try to stack all 3 cans back up. Catchers throw the ball at the Builders to tap them OUT (below the waist only!). Catchers can pass the ball to each other to get closer.
4. Win the Point!: How to win the round.
Builders Win: If you stack all 3 cans and shout " Drie Blikkies, Dithini, Amathini,!" before anyone gets hit, you get 1 point!
Catchers Win: If the Catchers hit a Builder with the ball, that Builder is out. Hit all Builders to swap sides!
 
Bones:
1st Place: 3 Bones
2nd Place: 2 Bones
```
