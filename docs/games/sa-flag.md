# SA Flag

## Type

Revision Game

## Equipment

SA Flag printed and cut up into puzzle pieces per Six

## Instructions

![Source image](<../pop/source-conversions/assets/Our_Heritage_attachment.doc/Our_Heritage_attachment_html_2b344ef.jpg>)

**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

REVISION GAME: SA Flag Relay Cut up a South African flag into 6 puzzle pieces. Cubs stand in relay formation. On ‘go’ each Cub, one by one, has a turn to put the puzzle pieces in the correct place to form our flag.
