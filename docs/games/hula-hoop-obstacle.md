# Hula Hoop Obstacle

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Hula Hoop

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Hula Hoop Obstacle Cubs all hold hands, forming a circle. A hula hoop is placed between two Cubs on their arms. The Cubs must move the hula hoop from one person to the next without breaking their hold.

### Scouts Digital variant: Three-hoop Circle Challenge

**Reuse status:** Existing canonical entry reused; this separately attributed variant comes from **Traditional dancing, cultures, languages and time (6 November 2026)**, Steam release row.

The source adds a second hoop moving the opposite way and then a third following the first, while Cubs keep holding hands in a circle. The challenge is to keep the hoops from meeting. Slot: 15 minutes; equipment: three hoops. Keep space, avoid pulling arms and offer support/alternative participation where needed.
