```text
ACTIVE GAME:
Reach and rescue
Cubs line up, back-to-back, in two equal lines down the
centre of the playing area. One team are ‘reaches’ and
the other team ‘rescuers’. The leader calls out rrrreaches
or rrrrescuers (rolling the ‘r’ so that the Cubs cannot
guess what is coming). If ‘rescuers’ is called, they turn
and chase the ‘reaches’. Any ‘reaches’ caught join the
‘rescuers’ team. The ‘reachers’ who cross the designated
boundary line cannot be caught. The game continues.
When time is up, the largest team is the winner.
```
