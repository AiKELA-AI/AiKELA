```text
Game – Rescue Relay
Divide the Pack into 2 teams who line up 3m away from a
Cub. Each of the Cubs in the team are given a short rope. On
go the Cubs tie the short ropes together with sheet bends.
Once long enough the line is thrown to the Cub sitting waiting,
who ties it around his/her waist and ties a bowline. Once, tied
the team pulls the Cub back over the line while holding onto
the sides of the cardboard box.
```
