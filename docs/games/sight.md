# Sight

## Type

KIM’S GAME

## Equipment

15 ‘treasure’ items, pencil, paper

## Instructions

KIM’S GAME: Sight Two Scouters throw 10 to 15 objects to each other from one side of the hall to the other (items that the pirates could have stolen). Once all items have been thrown, the Cubs are to write down what they have seen (example of item could be silver cup (goblet), gold stone, etc.). Awareness Challenge – Healthy Mind. Silver and Gold Wolf requirements: Play sense training games using all five senses and achieve a 70% score. (Game played using sight).
