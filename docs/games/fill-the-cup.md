# Fill the Cup

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Plastic cup and spoon per Six; bucket of water

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Fill the cup Place the plastic cup at the finish line. Each Cub fills their spoon with water from the bucket and puts it in the cup. The first Six to fill the cup, wins (or you can stop the game after an allotted time, and which ever Six has the most water, wins).
