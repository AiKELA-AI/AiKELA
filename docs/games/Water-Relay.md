# Water Relay

## Type
Relay game

## Equipment
Two buckets per Six; one small tin or cup per Six; water.

## Instructions
Give each Six a bucket full of water and a small tin or cup. Cubs take turns transferring water, one tin or cup at a time, to a second bucket about 30 metres away. The Six with the most water in its second bucket wins. Assign a Scouter to supervise the relay.
