# Hungry Birds

## Type
Active game

## Equipment
About 20 dried beans or similar objects; a marked central square; a designated nest or corner for each Six.

## Instructions
Name each Six after an Australian bird. Mark a square in the centre of the playing area and place about 20 beans or similar objects in it. At a signal, Cubs fly around the area, flapping their wings and making bird calls. At “feed time”, each Cub tries to collect one bean and run with it to their nest (Six corner). The group with the most beans wins. Play three or four rounds so each Six has a chance to win. Assign a Scouter to lead the game.

## Advancement covered / badge link
No activity-specific advancement task is stated. The Aborigine programme lists trail and woodcraft-sign advancement context (Caracal, Cheetah, Leopard and Lion); it is programme context only and is not linked to this game.
