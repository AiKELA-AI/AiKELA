# How Do You Like Your Neighbours?

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Chairs

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: How do you like your neighbours? The Pack sits in a circle on chairs. One Cub is ‘It’. His/her chair is removed and he/she stands in the centre. ‘It’ asks one of the players ‘how do you like your neighbour?’ The Cub then replies ‘I don’t like him/her’. ‘It’ then asks ‘who do you prefer?’ The Cub replies with the names of two Cubs in the circle. The two Cubs named may then change places, ‘It’ endeavouring to capture one of the seats. Alternatively, the Cub addressed may answer, ‘I like him/her – but I am going to move!’ This is a signal for everyone to change places. If ‘It’ obtains possession of a chair, the Cub left out becomes ‘It’.
