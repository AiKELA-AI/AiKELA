# Sound Signal Shuffle

## Type

High-energy listening and movement game

## Equipment

None. Use a clear, non-slip space.

## Instructions

Spread Cubs out so they can move without bumping. The Scouter claps a steady beat. While the beat continues, Cubs move briskly around their own spot using a chosen movement: march, jog on the spot, side-step or gentle hop. When the clapping stops, everyone freezes and listens for the next instruction. Change the beat and movement every 20–30 seconds. Continue for about 10 minutes. Do not eliminate anyone; offer seated arm movements or walking as alternatives. Keep the floor clear and stop if the space becomes crowded.

## Advancement / badge link

No specific advancement requirement is linked to this game. Movement and listening are part of play; participation does not by itself complete an advancement task.

## Visual aid / image reference

No visual aid is needed. The game was first used in the [1st Pinelands musical instruments programme](../../packs/1st-pinelands/programmes/2026-10-30-musical-instruments-programme.docx).
