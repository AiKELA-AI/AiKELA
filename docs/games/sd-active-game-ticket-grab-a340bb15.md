Active Game - Ticket Grab –
Cubs sit in a large circle and a sheet of newspaper lies in the
middle (The ticket). Number the Cubs – ensuring that there 2-

numbers and the Cubs with that number will scramble for the
paper to see who can grab the biggest piece.
