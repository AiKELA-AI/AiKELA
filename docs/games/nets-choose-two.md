# Nets Choose two

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Nets Choose two Cubs to join hands and let them catch other Cubs who then become part of the ‘net’. When there are four Cubs, allow them to break into two ‘nets’ and to continue catching other Cubs.
