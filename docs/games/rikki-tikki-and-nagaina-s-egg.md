# Rikki-Tikki and Nagaina’s Egg

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Ball; chalk

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Rikki-Tikki and Nagaina’s Egg Cubs in a circle, but the Cub representing Rikki-Tikki must stand in a chalked square (+/- 2 feet) and must not move out of it. The egg is behind his/her feet. Chalk a line in front of him/her. Beyond this Nagaina kneels and from there tries to regain her egg. Rikki-Tikki may move his/her feet as he/she likes but may not move out of the square. One minute is given to each Cub acting as Nagaina. If Rikki-Tikki allows the egg to be taken, then Rikki-Tikki is replaced. If Nagaina does not regain the egg then he/she is replaced.
