```text
Instructions:  Divide your group into two equal teams.
 Have the teams form two parallel lines facing each other, about 1 meter apart.
 Designate one line as the Weeds and the other as the Wegetables so that every
player has a partner opposite them.
 Mark two parallel safety lines (baselines) about 10–20 meters away from the center,
one behind the Rats and one behind the Rabbits.
 The leader/coach shouts either "Weeds" or "Wegetables".
 If "Weeds" is called, the Weeds must turn and sprint to their safety line before their
Wegetable partner can tag them on the waist or back.
 If "Wegetables" is called, the roles reverse; the Wegetables sprint to their safety line
while the weeds give chase.
 Players score a point if they successfully reach the line without being tagged, or if they
tag their partner before they reach the safe zone.
To make the game harder or more engaging for older kids, try starting from different
positions instead of standing up: [1]
• Back-to-back: Players start sitting or standing back-to-back.
• Plank position: Players start in a push-up/plank position facing away from each
other.
• Belly-down: Players lie flat on their stomachs with their feet touching the center
line.
```
