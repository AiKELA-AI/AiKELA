```text
Steam Release: Time Runners
● Layout the 12 points of a clock in a large circle. Place a “12” at 12 o’clock.
● Cubs line up in sixes close to 6 o’clock
● Work in sixes.
● Two piles, one of numbers (randomly sorted), one of events of the day
● One cub from each six grabs a number and run clockwise to where it needs
to be placed, then continues around and back to tag the next cub.
● Cubs keep going until all numbers are placed
● After numbers are placed, events of the day need to be placed at the correct
time.
● Award marbles to the sixes: 6, 4, 2, 1 in order of completion
```
