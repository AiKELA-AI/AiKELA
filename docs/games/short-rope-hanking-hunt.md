# Short-rope Hanking Hunt

## Type

Steam-release / knotting skills game. Source slot: 15 minutes including a 10-minute hunt. Suitable for Cubs with a prior demonstration of short-rope hanking.

## Equipment

Short lengths of coloured twine or rope; a colour-matched resealable bag for each team; adult helpers holding the bags. The source assigns three adults to bags; adapt supervision to the selected Pack's numbers and venue.

## Preparation

Spread the pieces around a bounded garden area. Assign the colours and bags so Cubs know which bag to take their hanks to. Keep the pieces clear of trip hazards.

## Instructions

Reused from Mrs Knot's House, with wording organised for a Scouter:

1. Demonstrate how to hank a short rope. The source names the skill but does not give a step-by-step hanking demonstration.
2. Cubs search for one piece of twine at a time, then bring it to the Scouter holding their colour's bag.
3. The Cub hanks the twine. When the Scouter is satisfied, the hank goes into the bag.
4. The bag-holding Scouters keep moving within the game area while Cubs continue collecting.
5. Stop after 10 minutes. Count the accepted hanks and rank the Sixes. The source awards “bones”; use the selected Pack's reward system and decide amounts before play rather than inventing a source scoring scale.

## Safety and variations

**Scouter guidance added during extraction:** Define safe boundaries, keep helpers visible, and use lengths that will not trail into other Cubs' paths. Do not tie rope around people. Count in all pieces afterwards.

**Theme adaptation:** The source frames this as collecting Mrs Knot's dropped craft twine. The same mechanic can tidy a fictional campsite or recover ship's rope; retain the hanking skill and credit this source when retheming.
