# Finding our Way

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

Paper plates (or similar) and pencil per Six

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Finding our Way Cubs line up in their Sixes. Opposite each team is a plate with North marked off on it and a pencil. The Scouter calls out a direction and the first Cub in each Six runs up to the plate and turns the pencil to indicate the direction called. If correct, the Cub falls in behind the compass. If wrong he/she goes back to join his/her Six. The first team to line up behind the compass wins. Use eight points of the compass. Outdoor Challenge – Compass and mapping. Silver Wolf requirement/ Revision - Know the 8 main points of the compass.
