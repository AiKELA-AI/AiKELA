```text
RELAY GAME: Balloon Race
The Cubs line up in relay formation. Cubs each receive a top or sideview card.
The first Cub places an inflated balloon between his/her knees and “runs” a
short distance, pick up a corresponding top view side view card and go back
again. He/she gives the balloon to the next Cub who does the same, and so
on until everyone has had a turn. The first Six sitting at the alert is the
winner.
```
