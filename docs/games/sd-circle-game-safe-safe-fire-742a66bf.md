```text
Circle game – Safe…Safe…Fire
Variation of Duck…Duck…Goose. Make a wide circle. One
Cub walks around the outside of the circle. As he/she passes
each Cub in the circle he/she taps the Cub on the shoulder
and says either “safe” or “fire”. If “fire” is called that Cub
chases the tapper around the circle who must reach and sit in
the empty space before getting caught.
```
