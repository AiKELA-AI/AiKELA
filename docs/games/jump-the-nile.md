# Jump the Nile

## Type

STEAM RELEASE

## Equipment

Stocks or ropes

## Instructions

STEAM RELEASE: Jump the Nile Place two long sticks or ropes about half a meter apart. Tell the Cubs that the Nile River runs through Egypt. It is a wide river, but full of crocodiles. Have those that want to jump the Nile line up behind one of the sticks. They will all get a chance to jump across the river. After everyone has had a turn, move the sticks a little farther apart. Anyone who lands in the "water" leaves the game to do something else. Continue until only one person is left.
