# Figure Skating

## Type

ACTIVE GAME

## Equipment

Three signs; socks

## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Figure Skating Three stations need to be set up with the three signs showing clearly – UPRIGHT SPIN, SEATED SPIN and TRIPLE AXEL. This is a race where each “skater” has to complete three moves. The player who completes all three moves first wins. The first station is UPRIGHT SPIN, where you spin around once on the floor on one foot. The second station is the SEATED SPIN, where you spin around once on your bottom, like a break-dance move. The third and final station TRIPLE AXEL, you have to jump 360 degrees three times, trying to land on one foot as well. The Cubs slide out to each station on their socks, and are racing to complete each move. The Cub who completes each skating trick and slides back to the starting line wins the figure skating event.
