Steam Release: The Shrinking Forest
Setup:
Place 20 pieces of paper on the ground
Make a circle with a rope
Each round remove some of the papers and shrink the circle
Play
Round 1: Be starfish
Round 2: Be a frog - fni d a partner and leap frog over each other
Round 3: Elephant - stomping and swaying their trunk
Round 4 Buck - galloping around
When the whistle blows - shout “into the forest” - each cub needs to find a habitat
and get onto a piece of paper.
Remove papers each round and shrink the circle
Suddenly they're having to squeeze together.
Keep removing habitat until it's ridiculous.
It wasn't only the elephants that struggled when the forest got smaller. What
happened to the frogs? The starfish? The buck?”
They ALL lost space to live.
