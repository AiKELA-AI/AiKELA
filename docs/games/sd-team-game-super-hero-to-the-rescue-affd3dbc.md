```text
TEAM GAME: Super Hero to the rescue
In their Sixes, two Cubs must transport each of their
remaining Super Heroes, one at a time, from one side of
the playing area to the other. Two Cubs hold the Cub by an
arm and leg on each side. They carry the Cub to the
opposite side of the playing area, leave him/her there and
return for the next Cub, until all the other Cubs have been
carried across.
```
