```text
BASE 1: REVISION GAME: Call for help
Cubs sit in a circle and are numbered one to six. A cell
phone is placed in the centre of the circle.
The leader shares an emergency scenario with the Pack, for
example: “There has been an accident at the public
swimming pool on the corner of Spine and Eisleben in
Mitchells Plain. A young boy has injured his head and is
bleeding.”
The leader then calls out a number. All Cubs with that
number stand up, run clockwise around the outside of the
circle, and return to their place. The first Cub to reach
the telephone pretends to dial the emergency number
and clearly delivers the message.
• If the message is correct, the Cub scores one point.
• If the Cub hesitates or gives incorrect information, the other
Cub(s) with the same number may raise a hand and have a
turn.
• The first Cub to give the correct message earns the point.
Remind Cubs that emergency numbers are for real
emergencies only. Always tell the operator where you are,
what happened, give your contact number, and stay on
the line.
```
