```text
Active game – Don’t drop the Asteroid
Each Cub gets a blown up balloon with a number drawn on it.
The object is to keep the asteroids (balloons) in the air while
the music is playing. Cubs can hit them to one another. When
the music stops they all grab an asteroid and you call out a
number. Whoever has that asteroid must pop the balloon as
complete the challenge inside. The game continues until all
the asteroids have been popped.
Challenges:
• Do a moon walk across the hall
• Sing twinkle twinkle little star in a funny voice
• Count 1-10 backwards
• Pat your head and rub your tummy
• Recite the Cub Motto
• Say red lorry yellow lorry as fast as you can
```
