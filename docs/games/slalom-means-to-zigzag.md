# Slalom (means to zigzag)

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

Cups, tin cans or cones; ping pong ball per Six; side of a box or something similar

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Slalom (means to zigzag) Cubs in their Sixes and in relay formation, need to steer a ping pong ball (or something similar) around/through the “cones” (e.g. upside down cups or tins set out at regular intervals) by fanning the ball with a piece of cardboard. Once the first Cub in each Six has managed to manoeuvre the ball, he/she needs to pick up the ball and run back to his/her Six and the next Cub will have a turn. The first team of “skiers” to navigate the ball around all the cones wins the event.
