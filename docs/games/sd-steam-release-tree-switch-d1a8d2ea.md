Steam release: Tree Switch
Allocate each Six to the corner of the hall that is
named a tree – Naboom, Yellow wood, and
Bushwillow. One of the cubs is “it” and stands in
the centre. When the leader calls two of the tree
names the two Sixes must swop over. “It” tries
to catch someone as they swop sides. Anyone
caught must help “It”. When forest is called
everyone swops over.
