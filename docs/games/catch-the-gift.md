# Catch the Gift

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

1 x broomstick

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Catch the Gift Cubs stand in a circle facing inwards and are numbered off. One Cub who is ‘it’ stands in the middle holding a broomstick with his/her hand flat on top of it. ‘It’ shouts a number and raises his/her hand so that the stick starts to fall. The Cub who was called races to the stick to catch it before it hits the ground.
