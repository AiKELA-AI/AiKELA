**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

STEAM REALEASE: Mend the Calculator Scatter the numbers around the playing area. Give each Six a calculator template – they should then find one of each number – once they have found numbers 0 to 9, they should stick them onto the calculator in the correct order (template attached can be used or the Cubs can draw their own calculator).
