# Fruit and Vegetables

## Type
Team game

## Equipment
None.

## Instructions
Divide the Pack into two teams. One team stands in a line at one end of the playing area, facing away; they are the “fruit”. The “vegetables” line up opposite and creep quietly towards the fruit. When the leader gives a signal, the vegetables clap once and run back to their starting line. The fruit turn on hearing the clap and chase them. Anyone caught becomes fruit. When no vegetables remain, the original teams change roles. Keep the playing area clear and set safe boundaries before starting.
