# Wheelbarrow Race

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Wheelbarrow Race Cubs to line up in size order - tallest to shortest. Get them into pairs - Cub one and two are the wheelbarrow (1) and the pusher (2). They need to get to a designated spot and back again - they need to swap over and do the race again. First pair back after each Cub has had a turn to be the wheelbarrow, wins.
