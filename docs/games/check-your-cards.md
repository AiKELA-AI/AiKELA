**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

TEAM GAME: Check your Cards Cubs sit in their Sixes. They are handed a pack of special cards and play the game “Happy Families”. However, the cards are made up as follows: Each card will have a greeting in another language. The Cub will be able to identify it with the symbol printed in the right corner. The Cub who starts will ask, “Please may I have the Afrikaans card for ‘thank you’. Another Cub will hand him/her the card that says “dankie”. The next Cub gets a turn and if he/she has one of the set, he/she could ask for the Zulu card for “dankie” and so it carries on. The Cub with the most cards wins. Alternative: Snap could also be played.
