# Up River The

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Up River The cubs stand in two’s in a circle, one behind the other and both facing inwards, so that two circles are formed. The Cubs in the inner circle are the rocks in the river. When the leader gives the order ‘up river’ the outer circle of Cubs start to walk around the inner circle. The next command given by the leader is ‘down river’, at this the Cubs about turn and start walking in the opposite direction. After this there are a series of other commends: Swift river – the cubs run in the same direction; Shoot the rapids – they run in and out of the rocks; Monkeys – the rocks make a noise like monkeys; Under the bank – this is for cover, they run in the same direction until they reach, their partner and squat down in front of him/her.
