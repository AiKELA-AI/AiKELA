## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Brothers Divide the Pack into two equal circles, one inside the other. Cubs pair off one outside with one inside. Each pair is known as ‘Brothers’. The inside circle faces left, the outside circle faces right. At ‘GO’ the Cubs run around until the signal is given when they all break away and find their ‘Brother’. As ‘Brothers’ find each other, they salute, shake hands and sit down. The last pair down, makes a change to their uniform. Continue until someone has made three changes to their uniform.
