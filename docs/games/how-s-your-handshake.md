# How’s your handshake?

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: How’s your handshake? Cubs sit in a circle and are numbered off 1 to ?. Two numbers are called (say 5 and 7) and these cubs must run around the circle, in the opposite direction, and when they meet they salute each other and shake hands, then carry on back to their place. The leader calls another two numbers – until all have had a turn.
