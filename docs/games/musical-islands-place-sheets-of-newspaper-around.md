# Musical Islands Place sheets of newspaper around

## Type

ACTIVE GAME

## Equipment

Sheets of newspaper/brown paper; music or whistle

## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Musical Islands Place sheets of newspaper around the playing area; say a dozen for about forty players. Or you can use brown paper, cut into odd shapes and sizes. Players walk around in single file, passing over the papers. When the music stops or the whistle is blown they have to stand on an “island”, as many as can crowd on. Any whose feet are not on the paper have to withdraw. Take away an island and play the game again. Continue until there is only one island left.

### Scouts Digital variant: Danger — Run to the Cave

**Reuse status:** Existing canonical entry reused; this separately attributed variant comes from **Grug’s Great Green Inventions (28 August 2026; source credits Caryn and Julia)**, p. 1.

The source uses hula hoops arranged in a circle instead of paper islands. Cubs run around, then enter a hoop when “Danger!” is called. Remove a hoop each round. It says this is similar to musical chairs but does not specify how to handle an unmatched Cub. Keep room around hoops, avoid crowding, and label any non-elimination adaptation.
