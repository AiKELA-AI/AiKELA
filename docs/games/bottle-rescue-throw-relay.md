# Bottle Rescue-throw Relay

## Type

Dry-land aiming relay; 10 minutes

## Equipment

One empty 2 litre plastic bottle tied to a rope per Six; a hoop approximately 2 m away per Six.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Sixes line up. A Cub gently throws the bottle so it lands in the hoop. On a successful throw, the Cub retrieves the bottle, returns it to the next Cub and sits at the back. On a miss, they retrieve and pass it on, then return to the back for another go. Continue until Cubs have had their turns.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** This is dry-land practice: use an empty plastic bottle, clear landing areas and no people as targets. Keep ropes untangled and supervise retrieval so no one throws while another Cub is in the target area.
