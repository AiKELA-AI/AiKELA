# Knotting Market On ‘go’ the

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

One rope per Cub; 3 counters per Cub

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Knotting Market On ‘go’ the Cubs may do any knot and bring it to show the leader. If it is correct, the Cub receives a counter. The Cubs continue to do the knots until time is up, but no Cub may do the same knot twice running. The Six with the most counters is the winner.
