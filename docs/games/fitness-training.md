# Fitness Training

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

1 skipping rope, bean bag and ball per Six

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Fitness Training Cubs in their Sixes stand behind a starting line. At a turning line, 10m away are 3 items: skipping rope, bean bag and ball. On the word “go”, the first player runs to the turning line, takes the skipping rope and jumps 10 times, tosses the bean bag in the air and catches it 10 times, bounces the ball on the floor 10 times. He/she runs back to his/her team, touches the next player who repeats the actions.
