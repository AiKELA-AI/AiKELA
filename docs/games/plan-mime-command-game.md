# PLAN Mime Command Game

## Type

Movement/command game; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Clear space; no equipment specified.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Cubs move around until “Stop” and a PLAN word is called. They freeze and mime: Pack—packing for beach/pool; Look—looking for dangers; Ask—asking an adult permission to swim; Never—saying never. The source removes a life from the last correct mime, e.g. rolling down a sock; elimination is not otherwise specified.

```text
ACTIVE GAME: Plan
Cubs run around the playing area until the leader calls
“stop” followed by a command, such as “pack.” The Cubs
must immediately freeze and mime the correct action.
The last Cub to perform the correct mime loses a life (for
example, one sock rolled down).
The commands are:
Pack Cubs are to mime packing a bag for a day at
the beach or swimming pool.
Look Cubs are to mime looking for dangers.
Ask Cubs are to mime asking an adult for
permission to go swimming.
Never Cubs are to mime saying never.
```
