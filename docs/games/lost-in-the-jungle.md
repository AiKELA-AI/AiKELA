# Lost in the Jungle

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

Animal names on slips of paper

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Lost in the Jungle Slips of paper on which names of the jungle animals are written, are hidden over a certain area. On the leader’s instruction, the Pack is given 5 minutes to find the names and come back to the Den. Once back they describe the animal they found without saying the name. The rest of the Pack must guess the animal.
