# Water the Horses

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

One paper cup half-filled with water per Cub; a turning point about 2 metres away

## Instructions

Divide the Cubs into Sixes. Give each Cub a paper cup half-filled with water. In turn, Cubs race to a turning point about 2 metres away carrying their cup. At the turning point, they kneel, hold the cup between their teeth, put their hands behind their backs, and drink the water. When the cup is empty, they return to their Six and the next Cub takes a turn. The first Six to finish wins.

**Safety adaptation:** The source asks Cubs to run while carrying a cup and drink with it held in their teeth. To reduce spill, choking and collision risks, have Cubs walk while carrying the cup, stop before drinking, and hold the cup in their hands. Keep the course clear and use clean drinking water.
