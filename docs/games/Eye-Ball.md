# Eye Ball

## Type
Circle game

## Equipment
One ball.

## Instructions
Cubs stand in a circle with legs wide apart and feet touching. The space between each player’s feet is a goal, defended with hands only. Cubs bend their knees and crouch with palms facing the middle and fingers pointing down. One Cub taps the ball with a hand, trying to send it through another player’s legs. The ball may only be tapped with hands; do not stop and roll it. Cubs may not reach over another player’s goal and should watch the ball so it does not go through their own legs. Assign a leader before the game.
