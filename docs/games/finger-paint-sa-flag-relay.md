# Finger-paint the South African Flag Relay

## Type

Team relay / colouring; 15 minutes

## Equipment

Flag outlines on clipboards, paints, washing basins and towels. The source lists five paint colours and five flags; do not treat this list as an accurate flag-colour specification.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Each Cub paints the appropriate part of the flag in the correct colour before the next teammate has a turn. The first Six with a correct completed flag sits down. Wash hands and discuss why clean hands matter.
