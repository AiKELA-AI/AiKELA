# Big Shoes

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

A pair of LARGE shoes per Six

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Big Shoes Cubs form up behind a line; a large pair of shoes is placed in front of each Six. One at a time, the Cubs take their shoes off, step into the large shoes and then race to a point and back to their Six. If they “lose” their shoes, they must start again.
