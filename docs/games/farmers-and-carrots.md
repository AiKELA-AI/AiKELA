# Farmers and Carrots

## Type

ACTIVE GAME

## Equipment

whistle (or something to stop and start the game)

## Instructions

 - The carrots
 -- All cubs lie in a circle on the floor on their fronts, heads facing in, holding hands with the cubs on either side.
 - The Farmers
 -- One or two cubs start as farmers. 
 - The farmers must harvest the carrots by pulling on their feet. 
 - If a pair of cubs loose their handhold with each other, they are out and join the farmers. 
 - The last pair of carrots in the game win.
 
 Don't allow the circle to fragment, stop the farmers so that two rings can rejoin. It may be nessessary to stop the game each time carrots are harvested to allow the circle to reset.
