# Sweet-cable Knotting Relay

## Type

Relay game / knotting practice. Source slot: 20 minutes; two rounds. Cub-age activity subject to food suitability checks below.

## Equipment

Long flexible sweet cables in two colours, bags, a chair opposite each participating Six and a hand towel. The source mentions two colour bags on a chair in front of each Six; it does not state quantities. An adult checks the work at each chair.

## Instructions

Reused source mechanic:

1. Cubs line up in their Sixes, with sweet bags on the opposite chairs.
2. First round: each Cub takes one sweet of each colour, ties a reef knot, then returns to the Six before the next turn.
3. Second round: each Cub takes one sweet cable, makes a short hank and returns.
4. The source allows Cubs to eat their sweets while waiting for another turn. Apply the hygiene and eating adaptation below before using that part.

The source does not specify a winning rule, points scale or whether failed attempts are retried. Decide these locally and label them as adaptations; do not present them as original rules.

## Safety and adaptations

**Scouter guidance / adaptation:** Wash hands before handling food; the source's “Wash hands” line is after the relay. Check allergies, dietary needs and whether the sweets are suitable for the Cubs. Keep eating seated and separate from running. Do not share handled sweets. Use clean short ropes instead if food is unsuitable; that changes the materials, not the two-round skill mechanic. Walk to the chair if space is limited.

**Theme variation:** The source describes untangling Mrs Knot's sweet-shop order. Rope versions can be rethemed as cargo preparation or campsite tidying. Credit this relay and state what changed.

## Advancement / badge link

**Verified practice connections:** [The Cub Trail, November 2019, p. 13](../The-Cub-Trail-Nov-19.pdf#page=13): Silver Wolf short-rope hanking and making/using the reef knot as one of the required knots. The complete knot list also includes slip reef, round turn and two half hitches, and figure of eight; this relay does not cover the whole list. An edible cable is a practice material: assess the required rope skill separately rather than assuming the food version proves it. [Hanking task](../advancements/silver-wolf/outdoor-know-how-to-hank-a-short-rope.md); [knotting task](../advancements/silver-wolf/outdoor-make-and-use-the-required-knots.md).
