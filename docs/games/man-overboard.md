## Type

## Equipment

## Instructions

ACTIVE GAME: Man Overboard Play in a small area, inside or out-of-doors. The leader is the captain of the ship in very stormy seas. The crew must obey orders as follows: “Man overboard” – all rush to one side to look for him/her. “To the lifeboats” – all rush to the opposite side. “There’s a big one coming” – all hurl themselves flat to prevent being washed overboard. The fun lies in the captain calling orders very rapidly and naturally mixing the sequence.
