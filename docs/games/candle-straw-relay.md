# Candle & Straw Relay

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

1 x candle and box of matches per Six; one straw per Cub

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Candle & Straw Relay Each Cub is given a straw. They have to race to the opposite end of the hall where their candle and box of matches is located. They must light the candle and then blow it out by blowing the flame through the straw.
