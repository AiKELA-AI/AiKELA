**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

STEAM RELEASE: Pack the Suitcase Before the start of the game, scatter 10 pictures of different clothes around a wide area – one set for each Six. In their Sixes, the Cubs need to go and look for the pictures – they must not pick up more than one of a certain picture – i.e. all the pictures they pick up must be different. Once they have picked up a picture, they should give it to their Sixer who will put it in the suitcase (an envelope). Once they have collected all 10 pictures and they are checked that there are no duplicates, the Cubs are to return to the hall and sit quietly until everyone is finished.
