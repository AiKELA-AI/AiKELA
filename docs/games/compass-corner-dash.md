# Compass Corner Dash

## Type

Direction/movement game; 10 minutes

## Equipment

Four markers labelled North, South, East and West; compass.

## Instructions

**Reuse status:** Reused from the supplied source; wording organised for a Scouter. Programme-specific transitions are not extracted here.

Position the markers using a compass. Cubs start in the middle and run to the direction called. Source variations: face North; go to the opposite of East; face South and point West; take five steps North then turn East; face West then turn 90 degrees right. The source gives three jumping jacks after a mistake, then immediate return. Finish with a sequence of directions in order.

## Preparation and review

**Scouter guidance / extraction notes:** Use a clear, bounded area and a walking option; replace penalty exercise with immediate retry if needed, naming that adaptation. Do not confuse facing a direction with pointing toward another marker. Retheming may change the fictional setting while retaining the sourced mechanic; identify the adaptation and this source.
