```text
TEAM GAME: Rescue
Step over the fallen logs – sticks on the ground
Crawl through the brambles – under a chair
Hop across the stepping stones – hula hoops
Dodge the witch’s trees – weave through chairs
Reach the rescue tree – chair at the far end
On “Go!”, they carry the ball through The Dark
Forest. When they reach the far end, they collect
one pebble (beanbag) and un back.
When they get back, they must throw the ball to the
next Cub in their Six.
The next Cub may only start once they have caught
the ball. If they drop it, the thrower must try again.
After throwing successfully, the first Cub sits down
at the back of the line.
The winning Six is the first to have all Cubs sitting
down with their collected pebbles.
```
