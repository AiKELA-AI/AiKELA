# Bean Relay

## Type

RELAY RACE

## Equipment

Beans; knives

## Instructions

RELAY RACE: Bean Relay Cubs in relay formation. Each Cub is to carry a bean on a flat knife to a pre-determined area and back. Continue until everyone has had a turn.
