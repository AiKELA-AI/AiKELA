# Light up your life

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

Candle and box of matches per Six

## Instructions

REVISION GAME: Light up your life Cubs in relay formation. First Cub will run up, strike a match correctly, light the candle, blow it out and run to the back of the line. Next Cub runs up and does the same.
