# Sardines

## Type

WIDE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

Cubs play in their Sixes or groups of about five or six. One Cub hides while the others search. When a player finds the hidden Cub, they quietly join them in the hiding place. Continue until everyone is hidden.

**Source variation:** In the 20000 Leagues under the Sea programme, each Sixer takes a turn as the hidden player. The rest count slowly to 30 before searching; the game ends when the last person finds the hiding place. Choose a safe, bounded area and agree where Cubs may hide.

## Advancement covered / badge link

No activity-specific advancement task is stated. The 20000 Leagues under the Sea programme lists telling time by watch or clock advancement context for Caracal, Cheetah, Leopard and Lion; it is programme context only and is not linked to this game.
