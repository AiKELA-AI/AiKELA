# Sentries (alternate game)

## Type

Quiet Game

## Equipment

Two blindfolds; two chairs

## Instructions

Equipment: Two blindfolds; two chairs

The prize goes to the quietest team in this game. Set up two chairs about seven feet apart. These are the sentry posts and two blindfolded players are seated on them, facing each other. The other players divide into two teams. At signal from the leader, the first player in each line sneaks forward on tiptoe and tries to pass between the two sentries without a sound. If either sentry hears anything, he/she calls out and points in the direction from which the sound came. If he/she is right, the player is “captured” and out of the game. If he/she points in the wrong direction, the player sneaks ahead. Each team goes through just once. The winner is the team that gets the most players past the sentries.
