```text
Steam Release: Roz Needs Water
Three-legged Water Relay
Each Six divides into pairs.
● Tie the inside legs together.
● Each pair flils a small cup with water.
● Walk (or carefully race) to the basin at the other end.
● Pour the water into the basin
● Return and tag the next pair (give them your cups).
● This can be repeated thrice (depending on time)
● Once all Cubs have had a turn, they all gather at the basin, and flil the
cooldrink bottles to water the garden
Debrief
Ask:
● Was it easy carrying water?
● Did you spill some?
● Would you want to do that every day?
People asked the same question hundreds of years ago. Instead of carrying water all
day, they discovered that rivers could do some of the work for them.
Tonight, because everyone did a good turn by helping Roz to water the garden,
there are no special winners - everyone is a winner and everyone earns 1 marble!!
```
