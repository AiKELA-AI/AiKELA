```text
Steam Release: White Rabbit’s Rush! Don't Be Late! (PLAN B)
Set-up:
● Place one hula hoop in each of the four corners of the “playing fei ld”
(Wonderland Woods).
How to play:
● The Cubs are all "White Rabbits".
● Two Cubs are selected as “Card Guards”.
● The rabbits start hopping or running around the Wonderland Woodlands,
while the card guards stay close to the Old Wolves
● The OW shouts “The Queen is coming to the Tea Party!“ - all the “rabbits
sprint to the Mad Hatters Tea Party Corners (hula hoops) to avoid being
tagged by the card guards. If the space in the hoop is too full, they need to
run to the next hoop to be safe
● The Card Guards can only tag a white rabbit when the leader shouts the
warning
● If tagged, the rabbits must do 5 star jumps to unfreeze themselves and to
get back into the game
● The hoops can be reduced gradually to make the game more interesting
```
