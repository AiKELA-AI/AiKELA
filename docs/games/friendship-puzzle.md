# Friendship Puzzle

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

One puzzle per Six

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Friendship puzzle Print the attached puzzle on different coloured paper/card. Cut out into puzzle pieces. In relay formation, and one at a time, the cubs run up, find a piece of their puzzle and run back to their Six. Then the next Cub goes and so on until all the puzzle pieces have been collected. Once the Six has all their puzzles pieces, they need to put the puzzle together.
