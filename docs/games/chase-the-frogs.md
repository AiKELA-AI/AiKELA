# Chase the frogs!

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Chase the frogs! Pack in a circle. They are spaced well apart and bend over. Two Cubs are outside, one chasing the other. When the chased Cub is tired they leap over the back of another Cub in the circle, who then becomes the chased.
