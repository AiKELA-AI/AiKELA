**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

QUIET GAME: Horse drawn cart Pictures of food, water, sunscreen, etc. are hidden around the playing area. Cubs are to reserve their energy levels for the long journey home by walking around to find the pictures and are then put them in order of priority.
