# The Mummy Wrap

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

1 x toilet roll per Six

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: The Mummy Wrap Each Six is given a toilet roll and they must wrap up one of their Six, their arms separate from their body. When completely wrapped up the ‘Mummy’ must hop to the finish line, trying not to break the wrapping.
