# Snake Sting The Pack

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

1 rope

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Snake Sting The Pack stand in a circle. One Cub outside the circle is the snake, who walks round trailing the rope. The snake taps someone on the back and then runs round as fast as he/she can. The Cub who has been stung runs after him/her trying to catch his/her tail. If the snake gets to the vacant place before the tail is caught, he/she still remains the snake. The tail must always be caught by the hand and not trodden on.
