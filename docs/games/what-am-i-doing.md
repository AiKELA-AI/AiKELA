# What Am I Doing?

## Type

QUIET GAME

## Equipment

Broomstick or similar

## Instructions

QUIET GAME: What am I doing? The Pack forms a circle and passes around the broomstick. When the leader blows the whistle, the Cub holding the broomstick has to act with it, e.g. using it as a fishing rod, sword or even a hair brush. If the Cubs act something which has been done before, they pay a forfeit.
