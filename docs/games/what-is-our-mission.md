# What is our Mission?

## Type

WIDE GAME

## Equipment

Envelope containing puzzle pieces per Six

## Instructions

WIDE GAME: What is our Mission? Cubs need to go and find all the clues - working in teams of no more than four Cubs. When they find the envelope for their team, they must come back to the hall, put the puzzle together and read their mission: The mission will be to get certain items from around the hall and go and make a shelter (see attachment)
