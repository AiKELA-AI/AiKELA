```text
RELAY GAME: Rescue
Reminder: Never swim out to help someone, rather
throw something that floats into the water for the
person in difficulty to grab and use to float.
Sixes line up in relay formation. In front of each Six,
draw a circle about two metres away. Each Six has an
empty bottle attached to a rope and, at the word ‘go’,
they gently throw the bottle so that it lands right in the
circle. If they are lucky, they run out, pick it up, run
back to their line and hand the bottle to the next Cub in
line after which they go to the back of the line. If the
bottle does not land in the circle, the Cub who threw it
must run and get it and have another turn.
```
