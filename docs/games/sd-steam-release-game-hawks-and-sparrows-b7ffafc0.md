```text
Steam Release Game: Hawks and Sparrows
1. Mark two safe “nests” approximately 15–20 metres
apart. Choose two Cubs as hawks in the middle.
2. The remaining Cubs are sparrows and begin in one nest.
3. On “Sparrows, leave your nests!”, they run to the
opposite nest without being tagged.
4. Tagged sparrows freeze with their arms stretched out
like wings.
5. A free sparrow can rescue them by touching one wing
and naming a bird that has not already been used
during that crossing.
6. Hawks may tag gently but may not enter the nests or
tag Cubs during a rescue.
7. Repeat in the opposite direction and change the hawks
after two or three crossings.
```
