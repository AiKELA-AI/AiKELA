# Stir the soup

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

1 cane or straight stick 1 metre high

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Stir the soup. Cubs stand in a circle; the leader stands in the middle as “it” and balances a stick upright on the floor, holding its top with fingertips. Number the Cubs in order. “It” calls a number and releases the stick. The Cub with that number runs in and tries to catch the stick before it falls. If the Cub catches it, they take over as “it”. Make sure everyone knows their number when they change places, and call numbers frequently to keep the game moving.
