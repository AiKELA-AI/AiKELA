# Sticks

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

5 sticks

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: Sticks Split the Pack into two teams and line them up facing each other; number them off from each end. Place 5 sticks in the centre between the two teams. When a number is called, those Cubs rush to get one of the sticks and take it back to the end of their row. The first Cub to get to 3 sticks is the winner! The Cub can also take from the other teams ‘nest’. Return sticks to centre and play again.
