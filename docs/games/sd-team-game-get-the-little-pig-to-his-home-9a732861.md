```text
Team Game – Get the little pig to his home
Have pictures of the 3 little pigs, straw, straw house, bricks,
brick house, sticks and stick house. Have a set of pictures for
each team. Cubs to run up find a picture and using a specific
knot, need to tie the little pig to one of the materials used to
make the house. The next Cub runs up and attaches the
house to the material using a different knot etc. Once all the
pigs have been joined to their houses and material, the team
shows the Scouter who checks the knots for accuracy. The
knots used will depend on the ability of the Cub. Young
Cubs=reef knot, older Cub=figure of 8
```
