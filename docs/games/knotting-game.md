# Knotting

## Type

WORK SESSION GAME

## Equipment

One piece of rope or heavy string per Cub

## Instructions

Spend five minutes learning or revising the reef knot, with older Cubs helping younger Cubs. Play in Sixes. The Sixer runs around a marked area and returns to the Six. The Sixer and the next Cub tie their ankles together loosely, like a three-legged race, then walk around the area and back. The second Cub then joins to the next Cub, and the Six continues until everyone is linked. The source says the first Six to get organised wins.

**Safety:** Keep the ankle ties loose and easy to release. Use a walking pace, clear the route, and stop if anyone stumbles or feels uncomfortable. Do not pull or drag a Cub.

## Advancement covered / badge link

- **Silver Wolf practice link:** [Successfully take part in three separate games and/or activities which involve using knots](../advancements/silver-wolf/outdoor-successfully-take-part-in-three-separate-games-and-or.md) — This game uses a reef knot in a team relay and can count as one knot activity. Cubs must still take part in two other separate knot games or activities.
