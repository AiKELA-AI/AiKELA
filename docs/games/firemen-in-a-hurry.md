# Firemen-in-a-hurry

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Firemen-in-a-hurry Cubs take off their shoes and then line up in Sixes with their shoes opposite their Six at the far side of the playing area. On ‘go’ the Cubs run to find their shoes, put them on, tie their shoe-laces correctly, and line up in their Sixes. Check the knots! NB: Ensure shoes are marked before playing this game!
