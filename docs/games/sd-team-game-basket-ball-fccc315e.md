```text
Team Game – Basket Ball
Divide the Pack into 2 teams. Each team has a chair
and 1 Cub stands on the chair as the goalie. The teams
throw the ball to one another and try to score a goal by
throwing the ball to their goalie. If the ball is caught, the
goalie changes places with the Cub who threw the ball.
```
