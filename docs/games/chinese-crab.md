# Chinese Crab

## Type

RELAY GAME

## Equipment

None

## Instructions

RELAY GAME: Chinese Crab Pack in Sixes. Sixer is on hand and knees facing rest of the Six, with his/her toes on a chalk line drawn about 1 metre in front of the Six. At a given signal, Cub proceeds backwards, still on hands and knees, to a chalk circle or cross, drawn on the floor about 1 metre in front of the Six. He/she goes around this mark and then, still backwards, returns to the back of the Six, when the next Cub starts off. First Six finished and sitting with arms folded wins.
