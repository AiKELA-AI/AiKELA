```text
Game: Birds Fly
1. Cubs spread out facing the leader.
2. The leader calls the name of an animal while flapping
their arms and saying, for example, “Hadedas fly!”
3. If the named animal can fly, the Cubs flap their wings.
4. If it cannot fly, they must remain completely still—even
if the leader flaps.
5. A Cub who moves incorrectly performs a fun action,
such as three “chick hops”, before rejoining.
6. Gradually increase the speed and mix birds with animals
such as bats, butterflies, elephants, penguins and
ostriches.
7. Allow individual Cubs to take turns as the caller.
For the final round, use only birds and test whether the Cubs
remember which birds are flightless.
```
