# Something Good to Eat

## Type

REVISION GAME

## Equipment

1 beanbag and 3 beans per Cub as counters/rewards

## Instructions

Have the Cubs sit in a circle. One Cub sits in the centre with the beanbag. The leader calls out a letter, and the Cub in the centre throws the beanbag to another player. Before the centre Cub counts to ten, the player must name something good to eat that begins with that letter. If they cannot answer in time, or repeat an answer already used, they give one bean to the centre Cub. Continue with new letters and players.

**1st Pinelands adaptation:** Use beans as the counters/rewards. Keep them out of Cubs’ mouths and collect them after the game.
