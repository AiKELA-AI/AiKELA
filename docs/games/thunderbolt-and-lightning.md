# Thunderbolt and Lightning

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

2l cold drink bottle attached to the end of a rope

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Thunderbolt and Lightning Cubs in a circle with a leader in the middle. The leader has a plastic bottle with a little bit of water in it, that he/she swings around and the Cubs must jump over. If Thunderbolt and Lightning hits them, they lose a life.
