```text
ACTIVE GAME: Eco-Ranger Challenge: The Ultimate Wildlife Relay
Game Setup
Teams: 3 teams of 6 players (1 runner per team at a time).
Equipment Needed: Ropes, open-ended crawling barrels, sticks, "Journey of a Raindrop" worksheets, and "South African Endangered Animals" charts.
Course Layout
Start Line: Team queuing area supplied with skipping ropes.
Halfway Station: Open-ended crawling barrel with the "Journey of a Raindrop" worksheet setup.
Finish Line: Wooden stick frame holding the "South African Endangered Animals" worksheet.
Player Gameplay Routine
Leg 1: Jump rope from the Start Line to the Halfway Station, crawl completely through the barrel, and fill in 1 step of the 5-step "Journey of a Raindrop" worksheet (Player 6 verifies and approves all 5 steps).
Leg 2: Pick up a stick, balance it horizontally on one hand while running from the Halfway Station to the Finish Line, and mark 1 correct threat for an assigned animal on the chart.
Leg 3: Sprint directly back to the Start Line and tag the next teammate.
Winning Condition The first team to have all 6 players complete the course with both worksheets fully and accurately completed wins!
Bones:
1st Place: 3 Bones
2nd Place: 2 Bones
3rd Place: 1 Bones
```
