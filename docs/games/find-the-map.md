**Programme run-sheet instructions:**

STEAM RELEASE: Find the Map South African maps are cut up into about 12 puzzle pieces – one map per Six and each piece marked with the Six colour on the back to avoid confusion. Puzzle pieces are scattered around the playing area. In Sixes, the Cubs are to run picking up pieces and once they have all the pieces, they need to stick the pieces onto a piece of paper to form the map of South Africa.
