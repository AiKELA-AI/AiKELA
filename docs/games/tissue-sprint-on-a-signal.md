# Tissue sprint. On a signal

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Tissues

## Instructions

GAME 3: Tissue sprint. On a signal, Cubs throw an unfolded tissue out ahead of them, go to it, pick it up, and repeat until they have reached the finish line. A tissue may not be caught or touched after it is thrown before it hits the ground. If it is, the Cub must take two giant steps backwards. No one can move forward while holding the tissue or by letting it blow up against their body or clothing.
