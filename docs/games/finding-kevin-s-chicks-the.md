# Finding Kevin’s Chicks The

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Blindfold/scarf; keys (or something similar)

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: Finding Kevin’s Chicks The Cubs sit in a circle. A bunch of keys (or something similar) is placed in the middle of the circle. Once Cub, who is blindfolded, is chosen to be the keeper of the chicks (keys). He/she sits next to the chicks (keys) to protect them. The leader calls on a Cub who must creep very quietly towards the keeper and try and retrieve the chicks (keys) without being heard. If the keeper hears something he/she must point in the direction of the noise. If he/she points in the right direction, the Cub who was caught becomes the keeper.
