```text
Memory and sense game rotations
Work in sixes and as a group
Base 1: Traditional Kim’s Game
A spy always watches silently, taking in everything and forgetting nothing
● 15 items covered on a table
● Everyone must be silent and observe what the items are for 1 minute
● Work together and write down as many items as you can remember
● Marbles:
○ Less than 5 correct: 1 marble
○ Less than 12 correct: 2 marbles
○ More than 12 correct: 4 marbles
○ All 15 correct: 5 marbles
Base 2: Touch and feel
A spy creeping through the dark needs to know what they are touching and be sure
to remember it
● 8 items in a bag
● Everyone must be silent
● Each cub has a turn to feel the 8 items. They must be quiet and remember
what they were
● After each cub has had a turn, they work together to try and remember them
all
● Marbles:
○ Less than 2 correct: 1 marble
○ Less than 6 correct: 2 marbles
○ More than 6 correct: 4 marbles
○ All 8 correct (and accurate): 5 marbles
Base 3: The time lock
Kim has left a time lock - can you find the right times to unlock the safe?
● 12x 24hr times
● Quick explanation of how 24 hour clock works
● Everyone must be silent while one cub solves each step of the time lock
● The cub is given a 24hr time (GW has a complex time, e.g. 13h45, and silver
wolves have a simple time, e.g. 15h00)
● There is a paper plate analogue clock face with hands. They must put the
hands of the clock at the correct time to match the 24hr time
● Marbles:
○ Less than 4 correct: 1 marble
○ Less than 8 correct: 2 marbles
○ More than 8 correct: 4 marbles
○ All 12 correct:: 5 marbles
Base 4: The rope observation
It’s not just about remembering what you see, but recognising what it is - will Kim’s
challenge leave you in a knot?
● 6x cub knots on a rope
● Work together and identify all 6x knots
● Once done, GW to teach all the silver wolves a reef knot, or a slip reef if they
already know the reef kot
● Marbles:
○ Less than 2 correct: 1 marble
○ Less than 4 correct: 2 marbles
○ More than 4 correct: 4 marbles
○ All 6 correct and GW ables to teach the knots: 5 marbles
```
