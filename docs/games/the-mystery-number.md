# The Mystery Number

## Type

CIRCLE GAME

## Equipment

Whistle

## Instructions

CIRCLE GAME: The Mystery Number The Pack forms a circle with the Sixers in the middle. The Sixers choose a mystery number known only to them. The Cubs march around in a circle chanting the number of each step they take. When they reach the secret number, the Sixers chase them. After ten seconds, the leader blows the whistle and the chase ends. Any Cubs who have been caught go into the centre and help the Sixers. The next number is decided upon and the game goes on until time is up and the Cubs who remain free are acclaimed as winners.
