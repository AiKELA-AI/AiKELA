# Shoe Throw

## Type

Activity

## Equipment

Shoes

## Instructions

GAME 1: Shoe Throw Cubs loosen their shoe laces (one shoe) and lie on their backs. They need to kick off their shoes as hard as they can over their heads and see who can kick their shoes the farthest. Do best out of three.
