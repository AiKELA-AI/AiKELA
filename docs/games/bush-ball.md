# Bush Ball

## Type

TEAM GAME

## Equipment

Ball per six

## Instructions

TEAM GAME: “Bush Ball” One Six faces the other Six. First Cub in each team is 3-4 meters apart. Cub one throws ball to opposite Cub and squats down. Opposite Cub one throws to Cub two in the other team and squats down. Game continues in this manner until all are squatting down except last Cub to catch the ball who remains standing holding the ball and calls BALL at which both teams leap up to alert and the game is over. Smallest Cubs in front otherwise it becomes too difficult to throw the ball the required distance.
