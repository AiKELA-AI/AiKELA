## SCOUTS South Africa ScoutWiki

- **URL:** https://scoutwiki.scouts.org.za/wiki/
- **Organisation:** SCOUTS South Africa; the homepage describes it as its resource wiki.
- **Use:** Cub resources, programme ideas and source documents, including Programme on a Plate (POP).
- **Scope approved by the user:** Only include Cub information. Exclude content for Meerkats, Scouts, Rovers and adult volunteers. Check each page and linked document for its branch; following a link from a Cub page does not establish that the destination is Cub-specific.
- **Imported POP provenance:** The Pack leader confirmed on 12 November 2026 that all POP resources already imported into AiKELA were sourced from this website. Keep each resource's existing source filename, page link and date/version; this general provenance statement does not replace item-level references.
- **Authority:** Apply the source hierarchy in `../AGENTS.md`. Distinguish wiki guidance and historical programme examples from official SSA requirements. Check requirement wording against authoritative SSA source documents; flag outdated, conflicting or ambiguous information. Do not treat a wiki page as proof that every statement is a current official requirement.
- **Access and citation:** Read the relevant page before citing it. Record its exact URL and any available document date/version. Preserve source attribution and check reuse terms before copying images or source documents.
- **Homepage checked:** 12 November 2026. It lists separate branches for Cubs, Meerkats, Scouts, Rovers and Adult Leaders.
