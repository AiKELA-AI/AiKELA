# Hans and his dog

## Instructions

Hans lived in Switzerland with his aunt and uncle, as he hadn’t a father and mother of his own. His uncle was a guide. One day a prince came to the village and sent for his uncle as he needed a guide. Hans was taken to see the prince because he was just five years old. The prince asked his age, and gave him a gold piece as a birthday present, to buy what he wanted most. His aunt said he must buy a silver watch chain to wear on Sundays. After a time, it was put away in a chest with his best clothes; when Hans asked for it to play with once, his aunt was horrified at the idea. After a year, a fair came to the village. Hans wore his best clothes and went to it with his aunt. She had promised him a ride on the merry-go-round, but she got talking to friends and forgot.

A man came and sat down by Hans with a puppy. “Anyone like to buy him?” he asked the world at large. “I would,” said Hans, “but I only have this silver chain.” The man took the chain in exchange. Hans ran to find his aunt, who was still talking. She was angry, and tried to find the man again, but could not do so, and for this reason she let him keep the puppy. “A dog is better than nothing,” she reasoned. Hans called the dog “Prince”, after the giver of the gold piece. It grew in two years to be a fine St Bernard dog, and Hans was very fond of it. One snowy day, Hans’ soldier cousin borrowed the dog, and left it at an inn, having forgot it. “He’ll come back; if not, I’ll fetch him in the morning,” he told Hans.

But Hans was anxious about his dog. “Why not go myself?” he thought. He knew the way, so went. It was very cold. He could hardly whistle. After a time, he felt sleepy. “I’ll have a rest and then go on,” decided Hans. Soon he was a dark heap on the snow, and the snowflakes started to cover him. Just then, Prince came down the road. He had hastened after the soldier cousin when he discovered he had been left behind, and was thinking of Hans when he saw him there. All dogs in Switzerland know that those who sleep in the snow seldom wake up. Prince barked: “Bow-wow-wake up.” Hans did not hear, but the people in the village did, and they came to see what was the matter. Now even Hans’ aunt agrees with him that Prince is better than all the silver watch chains in the world.

Maude Lindsay
