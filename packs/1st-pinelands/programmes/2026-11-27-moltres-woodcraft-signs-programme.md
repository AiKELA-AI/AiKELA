## Advancement and source notes

The Cub Trail (November 2019), page 13: Silver Wolf Trails — “Know how to make and recognise at least ten woodcraft signs.” and “Follow a woodcraft trail.” Gold Wolf Trails — “Lay a woodcraft trail where 20 signs are used for others to follow.” Making and recognising may be observed individually, but this session teaches five chart meanings and does not establish completion of the ten-sign requirement. All routes are tabletop practice; no claim is made for either actual-trail requirement. No interest badge requirement is claimed.

Reused/adapted: steam-release game from docs/games/in-and-out-game.md (POP Pyramids/Herd Boy); paired trail-sign practice from Scouts Digital Program_Doc_ID_1_7_2026-08-21.pdf, page 2. Map/stones method supplied by the Pack leader, adapted as docs/activities/landscape-map-woodcraft-signs.md. Pokémon framing and italic linking passages are newly written connective text for this programme, not an imported yarn. No standalone yarn is needed.

Preparation: check the maps and stones, arrange a clear table space, place stones in trays, and mark a safe line for the steam-release game. Use rounded stones, keep them out of mouths and never throw them. Check the available map quantity before allocating pairs.
