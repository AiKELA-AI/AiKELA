# 2027 Term 1 Outline

**Focus:** Silver Wolf and Gold Wolf Awareness. Term 1 runs from 13 January to 19 March; following the Pack practice of skipping the first and last Fridays leaves eight meetings. This plan schedules activities and opportunities, not automatic completion. Cubs report their own evidence directly to a Scouter outside the meeting when ready; a parent may send a letter or WhatsApp message for ongoing work. No meeting is reserved for collecting reports.

Gold — show sportsmanship in Pack games and know why it matters; explain sunburn and heat-exhaustion dangers and prevention; practise the figure-eight hop (about 4 m diameter loops, changing feet at the second loop) and choose physical challenges toward the four-of-five requirement. ## Ongoing evidence

Bird observations require at least two weeks; tree care requires one month; Silver seed growing continues until the seedling is big enough to plant in soil. Cubs can send evidence to a Scouter out of band, including a parent’s letter or WhatsApp, when each task is complete. Gold Cubs may also use mature Pack-made compost during the Growing Things activity; if compost still needs to mature, they report its later use when done. Individual Cubs must complete the required number of Healthy Body choices (Silver: 3 of 5; Gold: 4 of 5) and report their evidence. Physical options involving climbing, swimming or ropes require appropriate adult supervision and a suitable setting.
